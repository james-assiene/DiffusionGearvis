Lemon drizzle cake

The classic lemon drizzle has changed a great deal over the last decade, and the people who know it best say the change is not over. Local councils have published new figures every spring, and each year the numbers have surprised at least one group of experts. Residents interviewed for this piece described the shift in practical terms: shorter queues, longer evenings and fewer empty shops.

Ingredients

  • 225 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 225 g caster sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 275 g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 4 tbsp milk
  • finely grated zest of 2 lemons
  • juice of 1.5 lemons
  • 175 g granulated sugar for the drizzle

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180C (160C fan) and line a 30 x 23 cm tin.
  2. Beat the butter, sugar, eggs, flour, baking powder, milk and zest until smooth.
  3. Spread into the tin and level the top.
  4. Bake for 35-40 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Mix the lemon juice and granulated sugar.
  6. Spoon the drizzle over the warm cake.
  7. Leave to cool in the tin, then cut into 24 squares.
