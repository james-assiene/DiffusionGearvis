Classic beef and sausage lasagna

Serves 8 · Prep 45 min · Cook 1 hr 15 min · ★ 4.8 (2,310 ratings)

Jump to recipe

Every family has a lasagna, and this is ours. My grandmother made it on Sundays in a dented steel tin that has outlived two ovens, and the smell of the sauce still means the weekend to me.

The secret is patience. The meat sauce needs a full half hour on a low heat so the tomatoes lose their sharp edge, and the finished lasagna needs fifteen minutes of rest before you cut it, or the layers slide apart on the plate.

I use a mix of beef and sausage for depth, a ricotta layer loosened with egg so it stays creamy, and plenty of mozzarella on top for the golden crust everyone fights over.

You can assemble it a day ahead: cover it and keep it in the fridge, then add ten minutes to the baking time. It also freezes well for up to three months.

A word on noodles: the dried kind you boil first gives the neatest layers. No-boil sheets work too, but add a splash of water to the sauce so they have something to soak up while they bake.

Choose a deep dish, about nine by thirteen inches and at least three inches tall, and set it on a baking sheet in case the sauce bubbles over. Your oven will thank you.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 lb Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 28 oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 6 oz can tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 15 oz ricotta
  • 1 lb mozzarella, shredded
  • 3/4 cup parmesan, grated
  • 2 eggs

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 375 F. Cook the noodles in salted water until just tender, then drain and lay them flat.
  2. Brown the beef and sausage in a large pan. Add the onion and cook until soft, then the garlic for one minute.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and the tomato paste. Simmer on a low heat for 30 minutes, then add half the basil.
  4. Mix the ricotta with the eggs, half the parmesan and the rest of the basil.
  5. Layer sauce, noodles, the ricotta mix and mozzarella three times, ending with sauce, mozzarella and parmesan.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, uncover and bake for 25 more, until bubbling and golden. Rest 15 minutes before cutting.
