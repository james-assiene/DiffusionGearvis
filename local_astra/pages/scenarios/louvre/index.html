Plan your visit

Open 9:00–18:00 daily except Tuesday

Last entry one hour before closing. Rooms start closing 30 minutes before the museum closes.

The museum is one of the largest in the world, with nearly 35,000 works on show in three wings: Denon, Sully and Richelieu. Allow at least three hours for a first visit.

Bags larger than a cabin suitcase are not allowed inside. Free lockers are available under the Pyramid.
