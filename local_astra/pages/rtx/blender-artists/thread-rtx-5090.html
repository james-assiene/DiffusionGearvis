RTX 5090 not showing in Cycles Render Devices

pixelmoth

I just put an RTX 5090 in my machine. I'm on Blender 4.2.3 and the card does not show up under Cycles Render Devices at all, neither with CUDA nor with OptiX. When I try to render on the GPU I get:

CUDA binary kernel for this graphics card compute capability (12.0) not found

The driver is the newest one. What am I missing?

Classic driver problem. Do a clean reinstall of the NVIDIA driver with DDU (Display Driver Uninstaller) in safe mode, then install the CUDA Toolkit 12.8 so Blender can find the compiler. That fixed it for me on an older card.

cycles_tinkerer

Your driver is fine, and Blender does not use a separately installed CUDA Toolkit. Blender 4.2.3 simply has no kernels for compute capability 12.0, so the card cannot appear. The Blackwell kernels came with 4.4: see issue #134001 on projects.blender.org and the 4.4 Cycles release notes on developer.blender.org.

Install Blender 4.4, then open Edit › Preferences › System › Cycles Render Devices, choose OptiX and tick the RTX 5090. Finally set Render Properties › Device to GPU Compute.
