Cycles CUDA error: out of memory

rendergull

My forest scene renders fine on the CPU, but on the GPU Cycles stops after a few seconds with:

CUDA error: out of memory

The card has 8 GB. Is there a setting for this?

2 likes

nodewrangler

The scene does not fit in the card's memory. In Render Properties › Performance › Memory, enable Use Tiling and lower Tile Size to 512. Shrinking large textures also helps.
