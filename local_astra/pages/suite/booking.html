Before you send

Tables for up to six can be booked here. For larger groups, call the restaurant and ask for the events team.

We seat the whole party together, so please tell us the number of guests you expect, including children. High chairs are available on request; add it to the note.

If you need to cancel, let us know at least two hours ahead so that we can offer the table to someone on the waiting list.

Window seats, the garden room and the counter are given on a first come, first served basis. Mention a preference in the note and we will do our best.

Opening hours

Tuesday to Saturday, 6 pm to 11 pm. Sunday lunch from 12 noon to 4 pm. Closed on Mondays.

Finding us

At the end of Mill Lane, beside the old bakery. Parking is on the street after 6 pm.
