## Goal
`PipeWireAudioIO.capture()` stops raising `NotImplementedError` and becomes the only way audio
enters this system: 10 ms blocks of 48 kHz mono f32 (`CAPTURE_FORMAT`, `CAPTURE_BLOCK_MS`) whose
timestamps come from the **device's capture clock**, in the same clock domain as T-0006's
`rendered_ms()`, so `is_rendering(t_ms)` and a capture block's `t_ms` can be compared without a
wall-clock subtraction. On top of that: an anti-aliased 48 → 24 kHz decimation and framing into the
1920-sample (80 ms) blocks moshi-server 0.6.4 expects (`STT_INPUT_FORMAT`), an xrun policy that
keeps the STT timeline contiguous (the Kyutai delayed-stream model's clock only advances while
audio arrives — measured, see Implementation notes), and a **self-speech gate** on the STT tap only,
never on the raw stream, so the reflex VAD can still hear a barge-in while we are speaking
(docs/02 §4 `SPEAKING | user_speech_onset | PAUSED`). This replaces the spike in
`scripts/demo/_listen.py` (arecord at 24 kHz, level-based silence at `SILENCE_FRACTION=0.25` of
peak with a 0.02 floor against ≈ 0.116 room noise, `DRAIN_S=2.5` s of trailing silence to flush the
delayed stream): the module below carries *none* of those policies — endpointing belongs to the
reflex lane and T-0003's `ENDPOINTING` row — but it must honour the two hardware facts the spike
established, that the model needs continuous audio to flush and that a stream which stops dead
never produces its last words.

**Split and ordering.** T-0021 (this file, one day) = the capture pipeline and everything provable
on the **null-sink monitor rig**, which needs no microphone, no speaker and no display — `pw-record`
on `astra-test-sink.monitor` is a bit-exact echo path, which is exactly what the gate must survive.
**T-0023** (planned after this merges) = the acoustic half and the measurable slice of B15: real
speakers → real mic → `astra-stt` on 8080 with zero self-transcription and the ungated positive
control, the echo-path delay re-measured against T-0006's number, and the 10-minute zero-xrun /
flat-RSS run. AEC (ERLE, double-talk) stays out of both — see "Out of scope".

## Acceptance criteria

Each is checkable by a test or a rig measurement before the implementation exists. "The monitor"
means a `pw-record` capture of `astra-test-sink.monitor` taken by the rig *independently* of the
module under test; it is an oracle, not a re-implementation.

1. **Capture seam on the merged device, one clock domain.** `audio_device.py`'s `AudioDevice`
   protocol gains an input side: `start_capture()`, `stop_capture()`, a delivery callback or bounded
   read of f32 mono frames, `captured_ms() -> float` (device-reported ADC position, monotonic,
   non-decreasing) and `input_latency_ms: float`. Capture and playback are served by **one device
   object and one clock base**, so `block.t_ms` and T-0006's `rendered_ms()`/`is_rendering(t_ms)`
   are directly comparable. `audio_capture.py` imports no device library (it takes `AudioDevice` by
   injection, like `PipeWireAudioIO`). **Backend branch, both accepted:** if T-0006 merged the
   `sounddevice` backend, capture and playback are one duplex `sd.Stream` (not two streams) and
   prerequisite P1 (`libportaudio2`) still gates only the `audio`-marked tests; if T-0006 merged a
   libpipewire backend, two `pw_stream`s are allowed **only** if both timestamps are converted
   through the graph clock (`pw_stream_get_time_n`), never by subtracting arrival times — proved by
   a test that stalls one side and checks the other side's timestamps are unaffected. Unit tests
   pass with neither library installed. *(docs/01 D7; docs/02 §2.7; T-0006 criterion 1.)*
2. **Block cadence is exact and independent of the negotiated quantum.** `capture()` (the
   `AudioIO` protocol method) yields `bytes` of exactly 1920 bytes = 480 f32 samples = 10 ms at
   48 kHz, always; `capture_frames()` (concrete class only, no protocol widening) yields the same
   audio as a frozen `CaptureBlock(samples, t_ms, xrun: bool, gated: bool, seq: int)`. The graph
   default quantum on this box is 1024/48000 ≈ 21 ms and the requested 480/48000 may not be
   granted, so re-blocking is mandatory and the **negotiated** quantum is reported, never assumed.
   *Adversarial:* a device double delivering 1024-frame buffers must still produce 10 ms blocks with
   no sample lost or duplicated over 10 s (conservation: samples out + carry == samples in).
   *Negative:* no short or long block is ever yielded, including at `stop()`.
3. **Timestamps come from the capture clock, not from arrival time (headline).** `block.t_ms` is
   derived from `captured_ms()` (+ `input_latency_ms`), is monotone, and for consecutive non-xrun
   blocks differs by exactly `CAPTURE_BLOCK_MS`. Bound: `|t_ms − true capture time| ≤ block_ms +
   input_latency_ms`. *Adversarial (the test this task exists for):* a device that stalls 100 ms and
   then delivers ten blocks in one burst must produce ten blocks spaced 10 ms apart covering the
   stall window; an implementation timestamping with `loop.time()`/`time.monotonic()` at delivery
   gives ten near-identical stamps and must fail loudly. This is the capture-side twin of T-0006
   criterion 2, and it is what makes onset timestamps and `played_ms` truncation commensurable
   (docs/02 §4: transcript segments are attributed by reflex-onset *audio* timestamps).
4. **xrun policy: the STT timeline is contiguous; the raw timeline is honest.** On a device-reported
   xrun or missing frames, (a) the raw stream marks the next block `xrun=True` and its `t_ms` shows
   the real gap — it never lies; (b) the STT-bound stream substitutes silence of exactly the missing
   duration so its timeline has no hole, and counts `xruns` and `substituted_ms`. Substitution is
   capped: more than `MAX_SUBSTITUTED_MS` (500 ms) of continuous substitution stops manufacturing
   audio and surfaces a named error instead. *Rationale, measured:* the delayed-stream STT advances
   its clock only with audio — `scripts/demo/_listen.py` had to append `TAIL_SILENCE_S = 3.0` of
   real silence because a wav that stopped dead transcribed to nothing at all. *Adversarial:* a 1 s
   stall yields ≤ 500 ms of substituted silence then the error, not 1 s of fabricated audio.
5. **Backpressure never touches the device thread, and the drop is visible.** A bounded queue of
   ≥ 32 blocks (320 ms); on overflow the **oldest** block is dropped and counted (`dropped_blocks`)
   — deliberately the opposite of T-0006 criterion 8's event queue, which keeps the newest; the
   asymmetry is tested in both directions so nobody "fixes" one to match the other. The next
   delivered block's `t_ms` shows the true gap and the STT path fills it per criterion 4.
   *Negative (source scan):* the device callback awaits nothing, calls no `asyncio` primitive and
   allocates no unbounded structure (`.claude/rules/conductor.md`; T-0006 criterion 13).
6. **48 → 24 kHz is anti-aliased and stateful (second headline).** In `audio_resample.py`: output
   length is exactly half the input for **every** chunking; feeding a signal in 10 ms blocks is
   bit-identical to feeding it whole (filter state carried); a full-scale 18 kHz tone is attenuated
   **≥ 40 dB** in the 24 kHz output; a 1 kHz sine round-trips at SNR ≥ 40 dB with output in
   [−1, 1] and no clipping. Oracle is float64 numpy, not the implementation. *Why the 18 kHz case is
   the criterion:* naive `x[::2]` passes every length and chunking test and aliases 18 kHz to 6 kHz
   at full scale, straight into the STT's speech band. The filter's group delay is either
   compensated in `t_ms` or documented, constant, ≤ one block, and applied consistently.
7. **STT framing is ours, and it conserves samples.** `stt_blocks()` yields exactly 1920 samples
   (80 ms) of 24 kHz mono f32 per block — moshi-server 0.6.4's block size, matching
   `STT_INPUT_FORMAT` and `scripts/demo/_listen.py`'s `BLOCK = 1920` — each carrying the capture
   `t_ms` of its **first** sample. Over a 60 s synthetic stream no sample is dropped or duplicated
   (conservation against the input), and a partial final frame is zero-padded only at explicit
   `close()`, never mid-stream. **Ruling, stated so T-0007 can be planned against it: T-0021 owns
   the 48 → 24 kHz resample and the 80 ms framing.** `MoshiSttClient.feed(pcm_24k_f32, t_ms)`
   (T-0007) is a consumer: it does not resample, does not re-frame and does not re-timestamp.
8. **The self-speech gate is on the STT tap only.** `SelfSpeechGate(is_rendering, hangover_ms)` is
   pure and injected: a block is gated iff `is_rendering(block.t_ms)` or the block starts within
   `hangover_ms` of the last rendering end. Leading edge is immediate (the first block overlapping
   playback is gated); trailing edge is the hangover, defaulting to **T-0006's measured echo-path
   delay p95 + one block**, with that number and its regime recorded in the module docstring and in
   the report. Gated blocks are replaced by silence of the same duration in the STT stream
   (preserving criterion 4's contiguity) and counted (`gated_blocks`); **raw `capture()` blocks are
   never dropped, zeroed or delayed**. *Adversarial 1:* playback of consecutive utterances with 5 ms
   gaps must not un-gate in the gaps (no chatter). *Adversarial 2 (negative):* during playback the
   raw stream keeps delivering unmodified blocks at cadence — an implementation that gates at the
   source silently destroys barge-in (docs/02 §4 `SPEAKING → PAUSED`) while every STT test still
   passes, so this test must exist and must fail that implementation.
9. **The gate works against a bit-exact echo path (rig).** On the null-sink rig, play the chirp
   fixture into `astra-test-sink` through T-0006's playback path while capturing
   `astra-test-sink.monitor` through this module: with the gate on, the STT-bound stream's energy in
   the playback window is ≤ −60 dBFS (silence); with the gate off — the **positive control, without
   which "no energy" could mean a dead capture** — the chirp is present at > −20 dBFS and
   cross-correlates with the source at lag ≈ the monitor path delay. A monitor is 100 % echo, so
   this is the strictest possible gate test and it needs no speaker, no microphone and no
   microphone-in-the-room regime.
10. **`audio.capture` is reported, not invented.** No new span name (`telemetry/spans.py` is not
    edited — `audio.capture` is already in `SPAN_NAMES`). The module supplies, on the session audio
    clock, the two numbers the session needs to write `spans["audio.capture"] = (start_ms, end_ms)`
    for a turn — per STT frame, the capture time of its last sample and the time it was yielded —
    plus a `CaptureStats` snapshot (`blocks`, `xruns`, `substituted_ms`, `dropped_blocks`,
    `gated_blocks`, `quantum_ms`, `device`). The module does **not** write `turns.jsonl` and does not
    import `telemetry.turns_log` (T-0007 owns the record). *Negative:* `git diff main --stat
    local_astra/telemetry/ local_astra/bench/` is empty.
11. **Module slice of the capture budget, with its regime.** On the null-sink rig (§Regime), over
    n ≥ 3000 blocks with `seed=0`: the delay from a frame's **last** sample's capture time to the
    `stt_blocks()` yield is p95 ≤ **20 ms** (docs/01 §8 "Mic capture + AEC + resample — 20 ms"),
    reported with p50. The structural +80 ms of filling a 1920-sample frame is moshi's block size,
    not this module's latency: it is stated in the report and excluded from the number, never hidden
    inside it. Zero xruns and zero dropped blocks over a 60 s run; RSS growth < 5 MB over the same
    run (bounded rings). *Negative:* `bench/thresholds.py` is not edited; B3/B4/B15 remain
    end-to-end numbers owned elsewhere.
12. **Negative — playback does not move, and nothing else does either.** (a) Every test module
    T-0006 shipped (`tests/unit/test_audio_clock.py`, `test_audio_played_text.py`,
    `test_audio_resample.py`, `tests/contract/test_audio_io_conformance.py`,
    `tests/integration/test_audio_null_sink.py`) passes unmodified; `played_ms`, `mark_drain`,
    `playback_events`, `playback_reference` and `flush` semantics are unchanged. (b)
    `local_astra/contracts/**`, `local_astra/telemetry/**`, `local_astra/bench/**`,
    `local_astra/reflex/**`, `local_astra/conductor/session.py`, `local_astra/conductor/stt_client.py`
    and `hud/**` are untouched; no new contract type (`CaptureBlock`/`CaptureStats` are plain frozen
    dataclasses in `conductor/`, not Pydantic, not on a wire). (c) **No AEC:** no import of
    `livekit`, `webrtc`, `speexdsp` or `module-echo-cancel` wiring; `Profile.aec` default stays
    `"none"` (docs/01 R4, docs/06 R3). (d) The module opens no socket and imports no networking
    module (docs/02 §9 loopback-only; the loopback-socket test is extended to the capture path by
    the test-author). (e) Within `local_astra/`, still only `conductor/audio_device.py` imports a
    device library or spawns `arecord`/`aplay`/`pw-*` (T-0006 criterion 12c, re-asserted for
    capture). (f) Event-loop lag ≤ 20 ms p99 over 10 s of **duplex** (capture and playback at once) —
    the new case, and the likeliest regression.

## Implementation notes
- **The one thing that matters** (capture twin of T-0006's): the block timestamp is the device's ADC
  position — `sounddevice`'s `time_info.inputBufferAdcTime` plus `Stream.latency[0]`, or
  libpipewire's `pw_stream_get_time_n()` — never the moment a callback ran or a coroutine resumed.
  Everything else is plumbing around that number and its shared origin with `rendered_ms()`.
- **Duplex or two streams:** prefer one duplex stream on the merged backend; it is the cheapest way
  to satisfy criterion 1's single clock domain and it is what any future AEC3 will need (docs/06 R3:
  a reference whose delay to the echo drifts per utterance cannot be cancelled). If the backend
  forces two streams, convert through the graph clock and say so in the report.
- **Do not port the spike.** `scripts/demo/_listen.py`'s level-based endpointing
  (`SILENCE_FRACTION=0.25` of running peak, floor `0.02`, room noise ≈ 0.116 peak) and its
  `DRAIN_S=2.5` s tail exist because that script had no Marker and no VAD. Endpointing is the reflex
  lane plus `SttFlushed` (T-0003's `ENDPOINTING` row). A capture module that decides when a turn
  ends is a defect even if every test passes. What *is* carried over is the hardware lesson behind
  criterion 4: the delayed stream advances only on audio.
- **numpy only.** `scipy` is not a dependency and must not become one. Polyphase/windowed-sinc FIR,
  odd taps for linear phase, cutoff ≤ 0.45 × 24 kHz, stopband ≥ 40 dB, state carried across chunks —
  the same discipline as T-0006's upsampler, in the same file.
- **Quantum:** request `PIPEWIRE_LATENCY=480/48000` and report what the graph granted (1024/48000 is
  the default here); another client can force it. Re-block unconditionally.
- `.claude/rules/conductor.md` is binding: the capture thread is not the event loop, nothing in
  `conductor/` blocks the loop > 20 ms, and reflex-lane work stays off this module.
- **Known landmines the reviewer will mutate for:** (1) timestamp ← arrival time →
  `test_block_timestamps_come_from_the_capture_clock_not_arrival` must go red; (2) decimator ←
  `x[::2]` → `test_18khz_tone_is_attenuated_not_aliased` must go red; (3) gate hangover ← 0 → the
  chatter test must go red; (4) xrun ← skip the missing audio →
  `test_xrun_substitutes_silence_...` must go red; (5) gate moved to the raw stream →
  `test_raw_capture_is_untouched_during_playback` must go red. If the suite stays green on any of
  these, the tests are wrong, not the mutation (T-0003's history: a defect survived 73 tests).
- Do not weaken a test to fit a threshold. If the 20 ms handoff slice proves impossible on this
  hardware, that is a **spec change** to docs/01 §8's capture line, raised with the measurement and
  its regime — not an edited assertion, and never a `pending` marker on a red test.
