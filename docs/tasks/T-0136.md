## Goal

**The problem.** In the T-0151 run, arm B S2#11 clicked "Add to cart" for the blue mug. The cart badge at the top right changed to "Cart: 1 item". Step 2's label said only `This screenshot is the browser screen now, after your last action.` The model wrote "It seems the cart didn't update" and clicked again: an over-click.

The model lacks a fact the harness can measure for free: whether anything visibly changed, and where.

**The change.** This task adds `Session(action_outcome=True)` (cli `--action-outcome`, profile `action_outcome`, live only, off by default). After each executed `click_at`/`type_at`, it diffs the frame the model saw against the frame it will see next, from the pixels alone:
- the hover styling near the pointer is tolerated, not counted;
- the result is one value from a closed vocabulary;
- that value is appended to the T-0134 label as one fact clause.

The diff is a pure numpy module: deterministic, no model call, about 1–4 ms per step. Its constants are calibrated on a corpus of before/after pairs rebuilt by replaying the logged T-0132/T-0134/T-0151 action sequences (plus probes), and hand-labelled blind to the detector. A locked 2-arm A/B (arm A HEAD's live default, arm B plus `--action-outcome`) then decides adoption. No instruction sentences are added.

### Group A2: the detector (CPU)

**A2-1. Module `local_astra/conductor/action_outcome.py`.**
- Pure, using numpy and stdlib only at import. PIL is imported lazily inside `gray()` only, as `reground.crop_jpeg` does. No I/O, no clock, deterministic.
- **Public names:**
  - `OutcomeKind = Literal["none", "near", "elsewhere", "page"]`;
  - `Region`: the 9 literals `top left`, `top`, `top right`, `left`, `centre`, `right`, `bottom left`, `bottom`, `bottom right`;
  - frozen `Outcome(kind, region: Region | None, changed_cells: int, cells: int)`, with `.text`;
  - `NO_VISIBLE_CHANGE = "no visible change"`;
  - `gray(jpeg: bytes) -> np.ndarray`;
  - `outcome_of(before, after, point, pointers) -> Outcome` (arrays);
  - `outcome_of_jpeg(before_jpeg, after_jpeg, point, pointers) -> Outcome`.
- `point` and `pointers` (0–2 points) are viewport px of the full frame.

**A2-2. Vocabulary (closed, exactly 12 strings).** `.text` is one of:
- `no visible change` (`none`);
- `changed near your click` (`near`);
- `changed elsewhere (<region>)` (`elsewhere`, 9 forms);
- `page changed` (`page`).

`region` is set exactly when `kind == "elsewhere"`.

**A2-3. Rule shape.** Only the constants are calibrated; the shape is fixed.
1. **Cells:** grayscale frames, optionally at a draft decode scale `DRAFT_SCALE ∈ {1, 2}`, are cut into `CELL_PX` cells.
2. **Changed pixels:** a pixel is changed when `|Δ| > NOISE_DELTA`. Inside the **hover zone** (a box of `HOVER_HALF_W × HOVER_HALF_H` around each pointer) the threshold is the higher `HOVER_DELTA` instead. The zone is tolerated, not masked out.
3. **Changed cells:** a cell is changed when it has at least `MIN_CELL_PIXELS` changed pixels.
4. **Page:** the label is `page` when the frame sizes differ, or when changed cells make up at least `PAGE_FRACTION` of all cells.
5. **Near:** otherwise `near` when any changed cell intersects the **near box**. The near box is definitional, not calibrated: `±0.25 W × ±0.10 H` around `point`, which is ±320 × ±80 px at 1280×800 and covers text typed into a 560 px input clicked at its centre.
6. **Elsewhere:** otherwise `elsewhere` when any cell changed. The region is the 3×3 ninth holding the most changed cells; ties go to the first in reading order.
7. **None:** otherwise `none`.

The precedence is page, then near, then elsewhere, then none.

**A2-4. Properties (Hypothesis).**
- identical frames give `none` for every point;
- `kind` is symmetric under swapping before and after;
- the result is deterministic;
- any change with `|Δ| ≤ HOVER_DELTA` confined to the hover zone gives `none`;
- uniform noise `|Δ| ≤ NOISE_DELTA` anywhere gives `none`;
- a single strong block far from every pointer gives `elsewhere` with that block's ninth;
- a strong block inside the hover zone gives `near`;
- a full-frame shift of a textured frame gives `page`;
- `.text` is always in the 12-string set.

**A2-5. Calibration.**
- **Who labels:** the A2 test-author hand-labels every distinct corpus pair (all of them, or a stratified sample of 300 by seed 0 if there are more) from the contact sheets, before the detector exists.
- **The label definition** is A2-3's geometry, with hover styling under a pointer and caret blink judged `none`.
- **Cross-check:** labels that disagree with the oracle (for example the cart grew while the label is `none`) are listed for the reviewer. Off-screen effects are legitimate (H-T136-4).
- **Split:** pairs go to dev or test by `int(pair_id, 16) % 2`, stratified by label class. The implementer tunes the constants on dev only and reports the dev numbers.
- **Measured on the test split:**
  - exact agreement, region included: ≥ 0.90;
  - true `none` labelled `none`: ≥ 0.95, with at least 40 true-`none` pairs;
  - true changes labelled `none`: ≤ 0.05;
  - true `page` recall: ≥ 0.95.

  Each is reported with a Wilson interval.
- **Report-only:** what T-0134's byte-identity `updated` would say on the same pairs (the cua lesson: exact hashing mislabelled 51 % of true no-ops).

**A2-6. Cost.** `outcome_of_jpeg` on corpus pairs: p95 ≤ 20 ms, measured with the 1-minute load stated.

**A2-7. Negative.** `action_outcome.py` is imported by nothing in A2. No other `local_astra/` file changes.

### Group B: session (CPU)

**B-1. Keyword.** `Session(action_outcome: bool = False)`. It raises `ValueError` when:
- the kit's mode is not `live`;
- `live_feedback` is `None`, or its `screen_label` is false (the clause lives in the label).

It composes with the overview frame, auto overview, page overview and ReGround.

**B-2. Measurement.** An action is measured exactly when the settled step is `executed` `click_at`/`type_at`, there is a before frame (the newest image of that step's request as sent), and B8 obtained a fresh after frame. It is then computed once, in `_live_step_screen`, by `outcome_of_jpeg(before, after, (x_px, y_px), pointers)`:
- `pointers` is (the session's previous executed pointer point, if any; the current point);
- identical identities give `none` without decoding.

Otherwise the action is **unmeasured**, with a reason in a closed set:

| Reason | When |
|---|---|
| `not_pointer` | not an executed `click_at`/`type_at` |
| `blind` | the turn has no image |
| `no_after_frame` | B8 could not get a fresh frame |
| `overview_frame` | the call was on a T-0151 overview frame; the screenshot the model saw was the overview, so "previous screenshot" would be false |
| `scrolled` | `scrolled_px ≠ 0` |
| `decode` | a frame fails to decode (the fake's non-JPEG bytes) |

Measurement never blocks the event loop for more than 20 ms.

**B-3. Placement.** The next step's label carries the clause only when its opening is `LABEL_UPDATED` and the step was measured:
- **Text:** `"[" + LABEL_UPDATED + OUTCOME_CLAUSE.format(outcome=o.text) + extent_clause + "]"`, with `OUTCOME_CLAUSE = " Compared with the previous screenshot: {outcome}."`.
- **Example:** `[This screenshot is the browser screen now, after your last action. Compared with the previous screenshot: changed elsewhere (top right).]`
- **`LABEL_UNCHANGED` and step-1 labels are unchanged.**
- **Repairs:** every body of that step, its repair included, carries the same clause.

**B-4. Logging.**
- The `live_label` record of `llm.request` gains `outcome: {"kind", "region", "changed_cells", "cells"}` when a clause is placed.
- Each live turn line gains `action_outcome: {"none", "near", "elsewhere", "page", "unmeasured"}` counts, one per executed pointer action.
- Each unmeasured action writes a `note` with its reason.
- With the keyword off: no keys, no notes.

**B-5. The T-0137 contract.**
- `_TurnLog.action_outcomes: dict[int, action_outcome.Outcome]` is keyed by the loop step of the measured action and populated only for measured actions.
- No change is `kind == "none"`, `.text == NO_VISIBLE_CHANGE == "no visible change"`.
- Unmeasured steps are absent. Their reasons are in `_TurnLog.action_outcome_unmeasured: dict[int, str]`.
- The settled payload (`screenshot` key and the rest) is **not** changed, on or off.

**B-6. Never spoken or stored.** The clause and outcome reach no `TtsSpeak`, history, HUD message, `Episode` or `machine.events()`.

**A1c-4. Builder: frame groups.** Every build writes `frame_groups.json`, mapping each frame sha256 to its cluster id. The rule:
- two frames are equivalent when they have the same size and a max full-resolution gray |Δ| ≤ 8 (the O-A1 (7) jitter rule);
- clusters are the transitive closure of that relation;
