# T-0054 — the fence lifecycle and the voice guard's refusals reach the log, the turn record and the panel

**Status:** merged 2026-09-17 at 8dbcc52 (composed tree f7fd9c7: 2534 passed serial and parallel, node 173/173; rulings A16–A18, B11–B13) — earlier: in progress 2026-09-17 — tests written red at 8259b21 (27 rows, four modules, zero assertions removed); two implementers dispatched. Planned 2026-09-16 · **two groups, concurrent, no shared file, merged together** (A the server and the wire, B the page) · **Blast radius:** medium, one reviewer per group, with named escalation triggers · **Origin:** T-0052 design decision D4 and ruling A33 — "the turn speaks its prose, the interface data is silently dropped, and **no log line records that a refusal happened**" · **Reserved by T-0052 as its group C**

**This is not a protocol that can be skipped.** It is not a one-line diff: a new server→client message type, a new session-log kind, three new turn-record fields, a publish rule, and a panel that must tell three states apart without ever claiming more than the server said. It also closes the half of the human's original complaint — *undiagnosable* — that T-0052 deliberately left open.

## Goal

T-0052 taught the client to carry a `<a2ui>` fence across the canvases of one request and taught the speech gate to refuse a string that is structurally interface data. It then stopped at the contract: `Commit.fence_close` and `Commit.fence_continued` are written and read by nobody, and `SpeechGate.refused_count` / `refused_sources` are counted and read by nobody. So today a turn whose surface was abandoned at the bound, a turn whose surface was reassembled from three canvases, and a turn that never had a surface at all produce the *same* session log, the *same* `turns.jsonl` line and the *same* pane on the page; and a refusal — the one event that means "a second line of defence just fired, something upstream is wrong" — leaves no trace anywhere outside the gate object, which is discarded at the end of the turn. After this task the `llm.commit` log record carries how each `ui` commit's fence ended and whether it continued an earlier canvas; the turn record carries `ui_canvases`, `ui_fence` and `speech_refused`; a new `speech.refused` log record carries the count and the sources the gate retained; and a new droppable-never `astra.ui.fence` message lets the shown pane say *reassembled from N canvases*, *cut off*, *abandoned*, *nothing was shown* and *N pieces of interface data were not spoken* — so a human at the desk sees a refusal without opening a log file. **Non-goals:** no change to `llm_client.py`, `speech_gate.py`, `ui_gate.py`, `turn_machine.py`, `hud_server.py` or any threshold; no change to what is spoken, to what is rendered, or to which canvases the gate accepts; no new field on any existing wire message.

## Acceptance criteria

Each is independently checkable by a test. Group letters: **A** is the server and the wire (`session.py`, `session_log.py`, `contracts/protocol.py`); **B** is the page (`hud/src/*`). Criteria 19–21 are joint and are asserted at the merge of both.

### Group A — the log, the record, the wire

1. **The `llm.commit` record carries the fence lifecycle.** For every commit the backend yields, the session log's `llm.commit` record gains `fence_close` (the `FenceClose` literal or `null`) and `fence_continued` (bool), taken from the `Commit` and never re-derived. `session.py:1383-1401` copies commit fields one by one, which is exactly why the two fields T-0052 added stop there today. *(T-0052 criterion 12 / D4; docs/02 §8 session log; test-plan Q11 "error taxonomy".)*
2. **A `thought` or `answer` commit records a null fence.** `fence_close` is `null` and `fence_continued` is `false` on every non-`ui` commit — the record says "this was never a fence", not "this fence ended cleanly". *(Negative for criterion 1: a writer that stamps the last-seen value onto every record would pass criterion 1 and fail here.)*
3. **The turn record answers "was this surface reassembled, abandoned, or never there?"** `TurnRecord.extras` gains exactly three keys, written on every turn line: `ui_canvases` (int ≥ 0 — the number of distinct `canvas_idx` values that produced a `ui` commit this turn), `ui_fence` (the `FenceClose` string of the **last** `ui` commit of the turn, or `null` when there was none) and `speech_refused` (int ≥ 0). They sit in `extras` beside `ui_repairs` / `ui_resamples` / `ui_valid` because they are the same family and a bench reader already reads that family from there; splitting one of five out to the top level is the inconsistency that makes a reader guess. *(Design decision D2 below.)*
4. **The three "never existed" / "reassembled" / "abandoned" cases are distinguishable from the turn line alone.** Three scripted turns — (a) no `ui` commit; (b) two canvases carrying one fence closed structurally; (c) a carry abandoned at the bound — produce three different `(ui_canvases, ui_fence)` pairs: `(0, null)`, `(2, "inferred")`, `(≥1, "abandoned")`. Asserted as three distinct tuples, not as three literals in isolation. *(This criterion is D4 of T-0052, stated as a test.)*
5. **A refusal is recorded, with its evidence.** When the turn's `SpeechGate.refused_count` rises, the session log gains a `speech.refused` record carrying `count` (the gate's running count), `sources` (the gate's retained sources, **byte-exact**), and `truncated` (true when `count > len(sources)`, i.e. past `MAX_REFUSED_SOURCES = 8`). `local_astra/telemetry/session_log.py` `KINDS` gains `speech.refused`; it is the only new kind. *(T-0052 criterion 10 makes this possible and A33 names it as the debt; `.claude/rules/conductor.md`: "a subsystem that silently disables itself must publish that it did".)*
6. **Nothing logs from inside the pure modules.** `local_astra/conductor/speech_gate.py` and `local_astra/conductor/llm_client.py` have **zero diff** (`git diff` over both is empty). The tap reads the gate's counters from `session.py`; it never calls into the gate to make it report, and `_segment` stays an adapter that hands the gate's output back unmodified. *(T-0052 criterion 10, verbatim; docs/02 §4.)*
7. **An interrupted turn still records what it counted.** `_sync_gate` drops the gate — never closes it — when a turn is cut off (T-0004 A9), so a refusal counted before a barge-in must already be on `_TurnLog` when the record is written. A scripted turn that refuses one sentence and is then interrupted writes `speech_refused: 1` and its `speech.refused` record. *(Adversarial: the most likely way to lose the number is to read it at turn end from an object that no longer exists.)*
8. **`astra.ui.fence` exists, is server→client only, and is never replayed.** `contracts/protocol.py` gains

   ```
   AstraUiFence(type="astra.ui.fence", turn_id: str, canvases: int ≥ 0,
                close: FenceClose | None = None, continued: bool = False,
                speech_refused: int ≥ 0 = 0)
   ```

   in `SERVER_TO_CLIENT` and `ASTRA_MESSAGE_TYPES`, never in `CLIENT_TO_SERVER`; `extra="forbid"` holds; the two direction sets stay disjoint and jointly cover every model. **It has no `replay` field** and is not journalled: `hud_server.py` has **zero diff** and `TurnJournal.record` returns early for it by construction. *(Verified at plan time against `hud_server.py:193-206`; the residual is stated in "What stays invisible".)*
9. **It is published when it says something new, and never otherwise.** The session publishes `astra.ui.fence` when the tuple `(close, canvases, continued, speech_refused)` for the open turn changes, and once more at turn end if the last published tuple is not the final one. Two consecutive identical tuples are never both published. *(Bound: at most one message per `ui` commit plus one per turn.)*
10. **An ordinary turn gains nothing on the wire.** A scripted spoken turn with no `ui` commit and no refusal publishes **zero** `astra.ui.fence` messages, writes **zero** `speech.refused` records, and its wire trace is message-for-message identical to the same turn before this task. *(Negative criterion; the cost of an observability feature must be zero on the healthy path.)*
11. **`astra.ui.fence` is not droppable.** `Outbox.offer`'s droppable set is unchanged — only `astra.turn.state` may be dropped (T-0028 criterion 12). A full outbox closes the socket with its reason for a fence message, exactly as for a surface. *(Negative: making it droppable is T-0047's rule change in T-0047's file, and a dropped refusal notice is the failure this task exists to end.)*
12. **Nothing else on the server changes.** Zero diff in `turn_machine.py` (its sha256 pin in `tests/unit/test_session_loop.py` is unchanged and it gains no import), `ui_gate.py`, `llm_client.py`, `speech_gate.py`, `hud_server.py`, `executor.py`, `local_astra/bench/thresholds.py`, `local_astra/telemetry/turns_log.py`. `_gate_surface` still calls `UiGate.gate(..., final=False)` — see D3. No new socket, no non-loopback address.

### Group B — the page

13. **`hud/src/protocol.js` mirrors the new type strictly.** `SERVER_TO_CLIENT` gains `astra.ui.fence` with its full field table (`turn_id` required string; `canvases` int ≥ 0; `close` enum of the four `FenceClose` values, optional; `continued` bool; `speech_refused` int ≥ 0). A message with a bad field decodes `kind: "malformed"` and is ignored and counted; an unknown `close` value decodes malformed rather than rendering. `tests/unit/test_hud_constants_parity.py::test_the_pages_server_to_client_set_equals_the_contract` is green **at the joint merge**. *(T-0037's landmine, and the reason groups A and B merge together — see Ordering.)*
14. **`hud/src/ui/fence.js` is DOM-free and clock-free.** `describeFence(message) -> { status, label, canvases, refused }` with `status ∈ {"none","whole","reassembled","incomplete","abandoned","refused-only","unknown"}`. It is a pure function of one decoded message: it holds no state, reads no clock, and **derives nothing the server did not send** — in particular it never infers "complete" from the absence of a message. *(`.claude/rules/perception-memory.md`: the shown channel echoes validated server state; docs/02 §4.)*
15. **The three states the human could not tell apart now read differently**, each pinned by its own row, with the wording fixed here (T-0052 ambiguity 6, decided by this planner):

    | server state | `status` | what the pane says |
    |---|---|---|
    | no message for the turn | `none` | nothing — T-0008's placeholder stands |
    | `close ∈ {written, inferred}`, `continued=false`, `canvases=1` | `whole` | nothing (today's pane, unchanged) |
    | `close ∈ {written, inferred}`, `continued=true` | `reassembled` | `reassembled from N canvases` |
    | `close="open"` | `incomplete` | `the interface was cut off — showing what arrived` |
    | `close="abandoned"` | `abandoned` | `the interface never finished — what you see is incomplete` |
    | `close=null`, `speech_refused>0` | `refused-only` | `part of the reply was interface data and was not spoken; nothing was shown` |
    | `speech_refused>0` with any `close` | (as above) | the line above, **plus** `N pieces of interface data were not spoken` |

    The panel exposes `[data-ui-fence]` (the status), `[data-ui-fence-canvases]` and `[data-ui-fence-refused]` on its own container so a test and a screenshot can read the same fact.
16. **A healthy turn's DOM is unchanged.** For `status ∈ {"none","whole"}` with `speech_refused = 0`, the panel's DOM after the message sequence is identical to the DOM the same sequence produces today: no new visible element, the repairs badge untouched, the surface element untouched. *(Negative criterion, and the one most likely to be violated by an implementer who renders an empty line.)*
17. **The panel never throws and never sends.** A malformed fence message, a fence message before any surface, a fence message for a different `turn_id`, and a host with no `document` each leave the page's socket, capture pipeline and other panes working and produce at most one log line. `hud/src/ui/fence.js` and the new code in `index.js` add no `fetch`, no `<audio>`, no non-loopback URL and no client→server message (`tests/unit/test_hud_no_audio_no_egress.py` and `test_hud_egress.py` extended over them). *(T-0008 criterion 10; T-0024 criterion 2 negative; docs/03 §9 E8.)*
18. **Nothing else on the page changes.** Zero diff in `hud/index.html`, `hud/styles.css`, `hud/src/ui/style.css`, `hud/src/ui/bridge.js`, `hud/src/ui/a2ui.js`, `hud/src/ui/action.js`, `hud/src/state.js`, `hud/src/main.js`, `hud/src/panels.js`, `hud/src/ws.js`. The new elements carry class names but no new CSS rule — styling them is T-0056's file and is left owed, deliberately. *(Negative: `hud/src/panels.js` and `hud/src/main.js` are T-0047 group B's and `hud/styles.css` is T-0056's; an implementer who reaches for either has left this task.)*

## Implementation notes

### Decisions taken at plan time (the implementer does not reopen these)

**D1 — a new message type, not a field on `astra.ui.surface`, and the reason is a measured asymmetry in the page's decoder.** `hud/src/protocol.js` is strict in both directions (`validate()`: "`extra inputs are not permitted`"). A **field** added to an existing message in Python and not mirrored turns *every* message of that type into `kind: "malformed"` — the shown pane would go blank on every turn until the page caught up, which is T-0037's landmine and T-0047 criterion 17's warning. A **type** added in Python and not mirrored decodes as `kind: "unknown"` and is *ignored and counted* (`protocol.js` header, four outcomes). So the new-type shape degrades gracefully and the new-field shape is a page-killer. That is why criterion 8 adds a type, why criterion 12 forbids touching existing messages, and why groups A and B are safe to implement concurrently. It also answers T-0052's ambiguity 6 on the wire shape: this is the decision, and it is made on a property of the decoder, not on taste.

**D2 — the three turn-record fields go in `extras`, not on `TurnRecord`.** `ui_repairs`, `ui_resamples` and `ui_valid` are already there and are read from there by the bench and by `tests/unit/test_session_record.py`. Adding three siblings to the same dict costs `turns_log.py` no diff, keeps the family together, and leaves that file free for the follow-up that owes it real work (PROGRESS follow-up 12). The honest cost: `extras` is `dict[str, Any]`, so the three fields get no Pydantic validation and no schema a reader can discover. That is a real loss and it is accepted for one release; the owed fix is a typed `ui:` sub-model in `turns_log.py`, which belongs with follow-up 12 because it touches the same file and the same pinned line.

**D3 — `_gate_surface` keeps `final=False`, and this task does not touch it.** T-0052's D4 named `_gate_surface`'s `final=` argument as part of the plumbing. It is not, and the reason is measurable: `A2UIValidator.validate_messages(..., final=True)` promotes forward references from **warnings to errors** (`local_astra/ui/a2ui_schema.py:428`), so passing `final=True` on a structurally-closed fence changes what the gate *accepts*, not only what it reports — with T-0010's progressive-render property and T-0052 ruling A6's measured seven-component surface at stake. A change to acceptance belongs in a task that owns `ui_gate.py` and can measure the delta on the flight fixture. **Consequence, stated so the panel is honest:** `AstraUiSurface.final` therefore stays `False` forever today, so nothing on the wire says "this surface is complete" — which is itself an argument for `astra.ui.fence` existing, since the panel must learn completeness from the fence's ending rather than from the surface's own flag. *Routed as a follow-up together with PROGRESS follow-up 11 (the resample budget silently truncating at `max_resamples=3`), which lives in the same file and the same question.*

**D4 — the refusal count rides in the fence message rather than in a second type.** A refusal that fires is, by the guard's own predicate, a string that was structurally interface data and did not reach the shown channel; the human's question is one question — *what happened to the interface this turn?* — and it is answered in one place, next to the surface. The alternative, a second message type, would double the mirror surface in the most dangerous file on the page for one integer. The residual, stated: a **false positive** (the guard refusing ordinary prose — T-0052 rulings A21/A14 measured how narrow the line is) will be reported next to a surface it has nothing to do with. The `sources` in the `speech.refused` log record are what lets a human tell the two apart, which is why criterion 5 keeps them byte-exact rather than keeping only a count.

**D5 — the panel echoes, it does not derive.** `describeFence` is a pure function of one decoded message. It must not accumulate across messages, must not infer a state from the *absence* of a message, and must not compute elapsed time. The only thing it derives is the label's wording from the fields it was given. A panel that remembers is a panel that can contradict the server after a reconnect — and this message is deliberately not replayed (criterion 8), so a remembering panel would be wrong precisely when a page reloads.

### Constraints and landmines

- **The gate dies with the turn.** `_sync_gate` (`session.py:980-987`) sets `self._gate = None` at IDLE and replaces it on a new `turn_id`; an interrupted turn's gate is *dropped, never closed* (T-0004 A9). Any tap that reads `refused_count` at `_write_record` time reads from an object that may already be gone. Accumulate onto `_TurnLog` as the count changes. Criterion 7 is the row for it.
- **`_segment` runs inside the machine's step.** `machine.segment = self._segment` (`session.py:638`), and the machine calls it from `step`. `session.py` is not a pure module so logging there is legal, but the tap must not log per call — only on a change in `refused_count` — and must not perturb `_segment`'s return value, which the machine's conservation law (`"".join(sources) + remainder == text`, `turn_machine.py:53-62`) checks and which T-0052 ruling A20 shows is one mistake away from a silent turn with no log line.
- **`llm.commit` is written before `submit`** and deliberately so (the comment at `session.py:1380` explains why: a commit the session later drops is still in the log). Add the two fields to the record; do not move the call.
- **`KINDS` is validated at `record()`** (`session_log.py:232`) and an unknown kind is turned into a `note` with a complaint (`:405`). A kind added to the writer and not to `KINDS` therefore *does not crash* — it silently becomes a `note`, which is the failure shape this task exists to end. Add it to `KINDS` in the same diff.
- **The session log redacts by key name** (`REDACTED_KEYS = {redacted, bytes, digest, phash, mime}`). `sources` is plain text and is not redacted, which is consistent with `llm.commit` already logging canvas text verbatim and with `.claude/rules/conductor.md` ("thinking **is** logged in the session log"). Do not invent a new redaction rule here; if a reviewer thinks refused sources should be redacted, that is a spec question for the rules file, not a quiet implementation choice.
- **`AstraMessage` is a discriminated union and `TurnJournal.record` narrows over it.** Adding a member must leave `hud_server.py` compiling under `mypy --strict` with **zero diff** — verified at plan time: the `elif not isinstance(message, AstraUiSurface): return` arm (`hud_server.py:203`) catches every non-journalled type before the narrowing branches. If the implementer finds mypy disagreeing, that is a finding to report, not a licence to edit T-0047's file.
- **`hud/src/ui/index.js` must keep never throwing out of `mountPanels`** and must keep its "no document / no subscribe" early returns intact (`index.js:55-62`, `:113-127`). The fence handling hangs off the same `h.subscribe` callback the surface bridge already uses; it must run even when `bridge.handle(message)` returns false, which today short-circuits the handler on its first line (`if (!bridge.handle(message)) return;`). That early return is the single most likely place to get this wrong.
- **`mypy --strict` on `local_astra/`**; `session.py`, `session_log.py` and `contracts/protocol.py` are all in the strict set. No `Any` leaking out of the new record builders.
- The fast suite ceiling is 120 s serial / 60 s under the Stop hook, and the suite is already at ~135 s measured (T-0031, and PROGRESS's structural-overrun note). New rows are pure and cheap; nothing here may add a sleep, a real socket or a real file beyond `tmp_path`.
