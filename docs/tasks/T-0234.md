## Evidence (logs/sessions/20261102-155518-245443.jsonl, commit 794a681b)

- 8 browser actions were refused with "the screen changed since this frame; take a new frame". 6 of these were T-0204 pre-click refusals. In 4 of those 6 the crop around the point had not changed at all (`changed_share` 0.0). The refusal came only from a layout-shift rect intersecting the box: Amazon and Nintendo pages shift constantly as banners and images load.
- After such a refusal, the next tool step came back empty 4 times (t2-gen-s7, t4/t5/t6-gen-s2). T-0231 arms its re-ask only after an *executed* action, so each of those turns ended silently after "I will click on the search bar.". In t4–t6 James said "try again" three times, and each try failed the same way.

**R-1 (implementer report at 623edb49).** The T-0207 completion check still arms only after an *executed* browser action. After a refused-only click, the re-ask's direct answer is voiced without a check, as on BASE, so `test_refused_then_answer_step_runs_completion_check` is re-pinned to that. `tests/unit/test_t0137_session.py::test_stuck_speech_committed_only[silent]` is a named re-pin: its empty step after a refusal is now re-asked, by intent. The test-author also re-pins `test_t0204_preclick.py::test_google_search_does_not_reset_run` (shift-alone) and adds the `settle_off` fixture to the new module.

**R-2 (review at ca4a0001, amends B1).** The reviewer's synthetic probe showed that a whole-crop share hides a small target moving under the point: a 16 px icon in white space shifted 8–40 px gives a `changed_share` of 0.016–0.031 and executes. The new B1:
- When the crop was taken and a shift rect is near the point, the page counts as moved if **any of the 3x3 blocks (8 px) centred on the point's block changed** (mean |luma delta| above `PRECLICK_BLOCK_DELTA`), or if the whole-crop share is ≥ `PRECLICK_CHANGED_SHARE`.
- With no shift near the point, the 10 % share rule applies as before.
- The four desk refusals had share 0.0, so they still execute.
- Accepted residual (orchestrator ruling, reported to James): identical controls shifting by exactly one row are invisible to pixels; BASE's shift-alone rule caught them.
- A click that executes despite a nearby shift writes a note with `result: "executed_despite_shift"`, plus `shift` and `changed_share`.
- The test-author adds a boundary row for `>=`: a clamped crop with exactly 10 %.

## Acceptance criteria

**A. Re-ask after a refused action** (amends T-0231 R-B3).
- A1. The empty-step re-ask is also armed when the previous step's browser action (click_at, type_at, press_key or scroll) was **refused**. It is not armed on `failed` or `timeout`, or after a non-browser call.
- A2. Everything else in T-0231 B is unchanged: one re-ask per step, a cap of 2 per turn, the notes, the `empty_reask` phase, and lost_thread when the step is still empty.
- A3. Negative: a turn with no empty step is byte-identical to main.

**B. Pre-click guard: pixels decide** (amends T-0204 B2).
- B1. When the crop around the point was taken, the page counts as *moved* only if at least `PRECLICK_CHANGED_SHARE` of the crop's 8x8 blocks changed. A shift rect near the point no longer decides on its own.
- B2. When the crop could not be taken (timeout or error), BASE's rule applies: a shift rect near the point decides.
- B3. The note still records `shift` and `changed_share`. `MAX_MOVED_REFUSALS`, thresholds and everything else are unchanged.
- B4. Negative: a moved page with changed pixels is still refused. This is pinned with the existing T-0204 rows, re-pinned only where a row asserted "shift alone refuses".
