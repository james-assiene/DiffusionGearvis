**Status:** done 2026-10-19 (merged 34aec39); tests at d8323d3; implementation module: `local_astra/conductor/session.py` · **Blast radius:** high (the conductor session's voiced text, `TtsSpeak.source`, the history built from it and the repair seed, on the tools-on path at every voiced step; two reviewers, A and B) · **Branch:** `T-0126-eot-literals-tools-on` from `main` at `4a25a85` (literal; code-identical to the T-0124 merge `2580ddd`) · **Parent:** T-0124 (criterion 9 re-read in its Rulings; the rows marked "T-0126"); T-0117 (C(raw), criterion 1; H-T117-5); T-0092c (I4, I10, I12, I15); T-0088a criterion 3 (the gate's trim rule) · **Sequencing:** T-0126 owns `session.py` while open; T-0125, T-0122 and any other task touching `session.py` start after T-0126 merges.

**Protocol cannot be skipped:** this changes what is stored, published and used as a repair seed on the tools-on path (CLAUDE.md: "store only what was spoken").

## Goal

With tools on (the default kit, loop on or off), `_AnswerCleaner` deletes only the tokenizer literals (`SPECIAL_TOKENS`, I4). The gate also cuts at `<end_of_turn>` and `<|endoftext|>`. They are in `speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS` but are not tokenizer literals, so they pass the cleaner.

The gate trims them, and everything after them, from the voice. Under conservation A1, though, each `TtsSpeak.source` keeps those bytes. From there they reach `played_text`, history, `astra.spoken`, `turns.jsonl`, memory, the `tool.call` record's `spoken_prefix` and the repair seed. For example, with the loop off, `Hi <end_of_turn> bye. ` voices `Hi` but stores `Hi <end_of_turn> bye. `.

After this task, the tools-on cleaner cuts its cleaned text at the earliest of these two strings, as the gate does:
- the voice is unchanged;
- every played `source` is free of both strings and of everything after them;
- history equals the join of what was played;
- `spoken_prefix` is what was spoken;
- one `note` counts the dropped characters.

Three things stay as they are:
- I4: tokenizer literals are still deleted with tools on (`Press <|tool_call> now. ` still stores `Press  now. `).
- The tools-off path.
- Streams with neither string, which stay byte-identical to `4a25a85`. The one exception is the commit split of a tail the wider hold may keep back (criterion 10's allowance).

## Acceptance criteria

**The rule.**
- **E** = the gate trim literals that are not tokenizer literals: `tuple(t for t in speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS if t not in SPECIAL_TOKENS)`, i.e. `<end_of_turn>` and `<|endoftext|>`.
- **C(raw)** is T-0117 criterion 1's cleaned text of the concatenated raw `thought_open=False` answer texts of one stream (unchanged).
- **D(raw)** is C(raw) cut at the earliest occurrence of any member of E. C(raw) holds no `SPECIAL_TOKENS` literal and a cut only removes a suffix, so D(raw) holds no member of `SPECIAL_TOKENS ∪ speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS` and no further round is needed.
- C runs first. So an E literal inside a closed thought block goes with the block. An E literal spliced together by a literal deletion (`<end_of<pad>_turn>`) is cut as if written in full.

1. **Each literal, loop off.** For `lit` in E, one commit `answer(f"Press {lit} now. ")` gives exactly `Press ` in each of these sinks:
   - the join of every `TtsSpeak.source`;
   - the audio plays' sources;
   - `played_text` and `history[-1].assistant_spoken`;
   - `astra.spoken` sources and `astra.spoken.played` texts.

   No member of `SPECIAL_TOKENS ∪ _TRIM_TOKENS`, and no whole word `now`, appears in any of them, nor in `turns.jsonl` or the memory episodes. The joined voiced `text` is `Press`, as at base. Likewise `Hi <end_of_turn> bye. ` stores `Hi ` and voices `Hi`.
2. **Split, spliced and across a thought commit.**
   - `Hi <end_of` / `_turn> bye. ` gives `Hi `, and so does `Hi <|endof` / `text|> bye. `.
   - `Hi <end_of<pad>_turn> bye. ` gives `Hi `.
   - `Hi <end_of` / a `thought_open=True` commit `draft` / `_turn> bye. ` gives `Hi ` (I15). The open commit reaches the machine as sent and neither flushes nor resets the hold.
   - No `LlmCommit` the machine receives holds an E literal, or a prefix that D(raw) later cuts once a later commit completes it.
3. **Stopped means stopped.** `First. <end_of_turn>Second. ` / `Third. ` stores `First. ` and voices `First.`. Nothing after the cut is submitted, before or at `llm.done`.
4. **C first; I4 unchanged.**
   - `Here <|channel>a<end_of_turn>b<channel|> ok. ` stores and voices `Here  ok. `. T-0117's note counts the block as at base, and no T-0126 note is written.
   - `Okay <end_of_turn> <|channel>thought SECRET-THOUGHT` stores `Okay `. T-0117's note is as at base (`unclosed` true), and the T-0126 note has `chars == len("<end_of_turn> ")`.
   - The I4 rows are unchanged: `Press <|tool_call> now. ` stores `Press  now. ` and voices `Press now.`; `Hi <eos> bye. ` stores `Hi  bye. `.
5. **Every voiced step, loop on** (`TurnPolicy(tool_loop=True)`).
   - A step-1 final answer `Hi <end_of_turn> bye. ` gives criterion 1's result.
   - In a two-step search turn, the step-2 report `answer("Found it: the café opens at nine. <end_of_turn>user: and tomorrow? ")` voices and stores `Found it: the café opens at nine. `.
   - Turn 2's request, which replays history, holds neither `<end_of_turn>` nor `tomorrow`.
   - The T-0126 note carries `"step": 2`.
6. **The call path, loop off (H-T126-3).** Step 1 is `answer("Let me look. <end_of_turn>junk ")` followed by a valid call.
   - It voices and stores `Let me look. `.
   - The `tool.call` record's `spoken_prefix` is `Let me look. `, and `call.spoken_prefix == D(raw)`.
   - With a repairable draft, the repair request's continued assistant text is `Let me look. <|tool_call>`.
   - The verdict, card states and execution equal `4a25a85`'s.
7. **All of it cut.** With tools on, `answer("<end_of_turn> bye. ")` alone:
   - synthesises nothing, and plays nothing but clips;
   - gives `history[-1].assistant_spoken == ""`;
   - reaches IDLE;
   - writes a `turns.jsonl` `outcome` equal to what `4a25a85` records for a tools-on `answer("<pad>")`.
8. **The note.** One session-log `note` per stream (pass 1 and each loop step) where the cut removed at least one character:
   - The note is `{"what": "special literals in an answer commit were not voiced or stored", "request_id": <machine id>, "step": k, "chars": len(C(raw)) - len(D(raw)), "cut": true}`.
   - It comes after the held tail is submitted, after T-0117's note when both apply, and before the `tool.call` record and `llm.done`.
   - Criterion 1 gives `chars == len(f"{lit} now. ")`.
   - No value holds stream text, and there is exactly one note per stream.
   - No note is written when nothing was cut, and none for I4's deletions.
   - T-0117's note is unchanged, and `llm.commit` keeps the raw text.
9. **Streaming and chunk independence (I12, tools-on form).** For every split of raw into commits:
   - the concatenation of every `feed` output plus `flush` equals D(raw);
   - each cumulative release is a prefix of D(raw);
   - no release holds an E literal or a prefix that D(raw) later cuts;
   - `spoken_prefix == D(raw)`.

   The hold is no wider than T-0124's rule: from the first `<` after the last character that is `>` or outside `_GATE_LITERAL_CHARS`. So `x < y`, `Since 3 < 5, the first step` and `Done <x9` stay unheld exactly as at base, and `test_i12_p5_a_maths_answer_is_voiced_before_pass1s_llm_done` stays green unedited.

## Implementation notes

- **Where the fix goes:** before `submit`, not at the gate (A1 conservation and the machine's join check). Do not change the gate, the machine, the client, the router, `live.py` or `loop.py`.
- **Deriving E:** take it from `speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS` minus `SPECIAL_TOKENS`, rather than restating the two strings.
- **Where to cut:** after `_clean`'s final `drop_special_literals`, so T-0117's `thought_chars`/`unclosed` are unchanged.
- **The hold must cover splices** (`<end_of<pa` / `d>_turn>`). T-0124's `_GATE_LITERAL_CHARS` rule is enough, and it is the upper bound. A tighter rule is allowed only if the property rows pass. Prove monotonicity with the property rows.
- **Keep commits that clean to nothing unsubmitted** (M26); criterion 7 depends on it.
- **Loop steps:** `_run_step` builds the same cleaner, so loop steps are covered with no edit there. Every note goes in `_end_tools_on_stream`.
- **General:** mypy strict. No heredocs to the scratchpad. If a test looks wrong, tell the reviewer.
