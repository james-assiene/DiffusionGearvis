## Goal

**Evidence:**
- Gearvis typed a search on Amazon and pressed Enter. `type_at` returned in 0.45 s with `"page": "new page loaded"`, and the after-frame showed only the Amazon header, with no results.
- The model reasons from that frame for a whole step. A page that finishes loading during the step's generation stays invisible to it.
- At step 12 it thought "I can't see the search results yet". It answered "I cannot see the search results yet. Please wait for the page to load or refresh the screen.", and the turn ended.
- T-0200's blank re-capture does not apply, because the frame was not one flat colour.

**What changes.** The live kit can offer one more tool, `wait`, with no arguments.
- Executing it dispatches no input. It waits `WAIT_S`, settles, and returns a fresh after-frame through the same `_capture` path as every action, including T-0200's blank re-capture.
- The model then sees the page as it is now, with the usual "screen now" label and `screenshot: updated|unchanged`.
- A wait is a loop step and uses the step budget.
- Waits are bounded per run of waits and per turn, so a turn cannot stall.
- No action anywhere gains a fixed wait (James: visible speed is the selling point). A wait is spent only when the model asks for one.
- With the tool offered, one guidance sentence tells the model to call `wait` instead of telling the user to wait.

### Group B — executor `wait`, its limits, `cli` wiring (off by default)

- **B1. A wait takes the action path without input.**
  - On `wait`, the executor applies the same rules as `scroll`, in this order:
    1. the guard
    2. the end-when-closed check
    3. `ensure()`
    4. the restart check (`browser restarted`)
    5. the page-serial check
    6. the T-0192 checkout check (a refused wait gives `CHECKOUT_REASON`)
    7. `ACTION_TIMEOUT_S`
    8. T-0198 fronting
  - It then runs `asyncio.sleep(WAIT_S)`, `settle()`, fronting, the after-settle checkout read (T-0192 A4) and `_capture()` (T-0200's action-path budget).
  - The outcome is `executed`, with action `WAIT_ACTION` (`"waited 1 s"` at `WAIT_S = 1.0`, built from the constant).
  - `resolved` holds `frame_after`, plus `hosts`/`hosts_more` and `checkout`, exactly as for `scroll`.
- **B2. No input, no page fact.**
  - During a wait the driver receives no mouse, keyboard, scroll, `goto` or `replace_page` call.
  - `navigation_mark`/`navigated_since` are not read (`wait` is not in `_NAVIGATION_TOOLS`), so the outcome never carries `navigated` and the response never carries `page`.
  - A `wait` with any argument is refused `bad arguments` before anything is called, as a second check behind A3.
- **B3. The run-of-waits limit.**
  - The executor counts waits per `inv.turn_id`. A wait counts once its sleep has begun.
  - A wait proposed after `MAX_CONSECUTIVE_WAITS = 3` waits in a row in the same turn is refused with `WAIT_RUN_REASON`, before `ensure()`: no sleep, no capture, no fronting.
  - Only an `executed` `click_at`, `type_at`, `scroll`, `press_key`, `page_overview` or overview-frame action in the same turn resets the run. A search, a refused call or a failed call does not.
  - A new `turn_id` resets every count.
- **B4. The per-turn limit.** A wait proposed after `MAX_WAITS_PER_TURN = 6` counted waits in a turn is refused with `WAIT_TURN_REASON` (no sleep, no capture), whatever came between them.
- **B5. Bounded time.**
  - Against a page that stays blank forever (the T-0200 fake), with `ACTION_TIMEOUT_S` monkeypatched to 2.5 s, one wait returns `executed` within `ACTION_TIMEOUT_S`, never a timeout failure.
  - With the real constants, one `execute(wait)` takes at most `ACTION_TIMEOUT_S`.
  - A turn's total wait sleep is at most `MAX_WAITS_PER_TURN × WAIT_S`.
  - The constants are module-level and read at call time.
- **B6. Cancellation.** A `CancelledError` during the sleep propagates. The wait still counts (its sleep began), nothing is captured, and `latest_frame` is unchanged.
- **B7. Through the session (no `session.py` change).**
  - The setup:
    - A real `Session` with a live kit from `cli.load_tool_kit(…, wait_tool=True)`.
    - A fake LLM scripted `type_at`, then `call:wait{}`, then an answer.
    - A recording fake driver whose screenshots change between the two captures.
  - The wait step's tool response is the generic executed payload: `action` `"waited 1 s"`, `status` `executed`, `screenshot` `updated`. With identical pixels, `screenshot` is `unchanged`.
  - The step after the wait carries the wait's after-frame as its current image, with the T-0134 label.
  - The fourth wait in a row gets a refused response with `WAIT_RUN_REASON`, and the loop continues.
  - `git diff BASE..TIP -- local_astra/conductor/` is empty except for A's `prompt_builder.py`.
- **B8. CLI.**
  - `astra session --tools live --wait-tool on|off`, default off. Outside live, the flag is refused with exit 2, like `--live-guidance`.
  - `load_tool_kit(profile, live_browser=…, page_overview=…, wait_tool=…)` picks among the v1, overview, wait and overview+wait sets. The kit's `tools`, `tools_version` and `tool_set` all come from that one set.
  - A v1 or overview kit is built exactly as at BASE (`tool_set=None` for v1).
  - With the flag on, the session's `tool_guidance` is `with_wait_guidance(live_guidance_text(...))`, for v1, v2, v3 and v2 under `--no-web`.
  - The `--dry-run` and start lines add ` wait=on(flag)`, and nothing when off. `native:<sha12>` shows the wait set's sha.
- **B9 (negative).**
  - With `--wait-tool off`, and in the `off` and `dry_run` modes, every request body, `llm.request` record, start line, tool response and HUD message is byte-identical to BASE on every existing test.
  - These are unchanged: `DryRunToolExecutor`, `progress.py`, `announced_call.py`, `live.py`, `warmup.py`, `tools_v1.json`, the payloads, the turn machine and `session.py`.
- **B10. Real Chromium on loopback (`make browser`).**
  - Fixture `t0202_slow_results.html` is a search box. On Enter it paints a header at once and a results block 800 ms later.
    - Run `type_at` + Enter, then `wait`.
    - The wait's after-frame differs in phash from `type_at`'s after-frame.
    - Its pixels in the results region are not the background colour.
  - Fixture `t0202_never_loads.html`:
    - Waits 1–3 are `executed`, and wait 4 is refused with `WAIT_RUN_REASON`.

## Implementation notes

- **Speed (James's ruling).** Nothing sleeps except an executed `wait`, and normal actions keep their exact driver call sequence.
- **Pixel-first.** A wait reads no DOM, readyState or FCP. The after-frame goes through `_capture`, so T-0200's blank re-capture applies unchanged.
- **The counters stay in the executor.** The executor already keys per-turn state on `turn_id` (T-0198 `_fronted`). `session.py` and `progress.py` stay untouched, and `wait` stays out of `progress.ACTIONS`.
- **No `NEW_FRAME_REASONS` entry for the wait reasons.**
- **The sleep runs inside `asyncio.timeout(ACTION_TIMEOUT_S)`.** It uses `asyncio.sleep` and never catches `CancelledError`.
- **Tool files follow T-0140 ruling R-p.** Separate files keep the v1 sha, the payload checks, the CD grammars and every harness pin unchanged. `draft_name` stays v1-only.
- **Speaking policy (D16).** A wait is never narrated. `NATIVE_TOOL_INSTRUCTIONS` is unchanged. `announced_call.DENY_VERBS` keeps "wait" (H-T202-8).
- **Prefix cache.** The flag is fixed when the session is built, with no per-request toggle.
- **T-0201.** T-0201 C's A/B runs before T-0202 D merges, or its report states the wait-tool state.
