# T-0037 — HUD hello replay: a reconnecting page catches up on the open turn

**Status:** done 2026-09-14 (merged; reviewer APPROVE after A9 row) · **Blast radius:** MEDIUM (one reviewer) · **Depends on:** T-0028 group A (merged ccb85c5), T-0030 (merged), T-0036 (merged) · **Origin:** the 2026-09-14 HUD rehearsal, defect D3 (recorded in `docs/tasks/T-0036.md` criterion 4 and `PROGRESS.md` line 130) · **Ownership:** `conductor/hud_server.py` transfers from T-0028 (group A merged, group B is evidence-only); `contracts/protocol.py`, `hud/src/state.js`, `hud/src/protocol.js` were T-0030's and T-0030 is merged and closed.

## Goal

Reloading the HUD in the middle of a turn leaves the user looking at a lie: `HudServer.hello_reply()` answers a (re)connecting page with `astra.assist.state` + `astra.turn.state` and nothing else, so the fresh page shows a live SPEAKING chip over an empty transcript pane, zero utterances, no strike-through — while TTS keeps playing the utterance the pane does not show. The page cannot recover on its own: the session publishes each line once, and the messages it missed are gone. This task makes the hello reply *catch the page up on the open turn*: the server keeps a small, bounded, single-turn journal of what it already published (`astra.transcript`, `astra.spoken`, `astra.ui.surface`, `astra.spoken.played`, and an `INTERRUPTED` turn state if one was sent), and replays it — marked `replay: true`, never invented, never a thought, never anything from an earlier turn or from history — to each connection at its hello. When the journal cannot hold everything, the page is *told* so (`astra.error(code="replay_truncated")`, rendered by the existing error strip) rather than shown a partial transcript it believes is complete. After this task, the rehearsal's row (f) reconnect can be strengthened from "a fresh page reconnects" to "a fresh page shows the turn that is still being spoken, with the barge-in strike intact".

## Acceptance criteria

Group A (server + wire) — must merge before group B.

1. **The hello reply catches the page up.** With a turn open (any state other than IDLE) in which the session published a transcript, two `astra.spoken` lines, one `astra.ui.surface` and one `astra.spoken.played`, a connection that sends `astra.hello` receives, in this order: `astra.assist.state`, the current `astra.turn.state` (both byte-identical to today — T-0028 criterion 4), then the journal: the turn's last `astra.transcript`, its `astra.spoken` lines in publication order, its most recent `astra.ui.surface`, its `astra.spoken.played` messages in publication order **last**, and — if an `INTERRUPTED` turn state was published for this turn — that state before the played messages, followed by one repeat of the current `astra.turn.state` so the chip is not left reading INTERRUPTED. Every replayed message re-decodes under the real `contracts/codec` and its type is in `SERVER_TO_CLIENT` (T-0028 criterion 9).
2. **Replayed messages are marked.** `AstraTranscript`, `AstraSpoken`, `AstraUiSurface`, `AstraSpokenPlayed` and `AstraTurnState` gain `replay: bool = False`; the journal stores what was published and the hello reply emits copies with `replay=True`. *Negative:* a message published live always carries `replay=False`; no other field of any model changes; `ASTRA_MESSAGE_TYPES`, `SERVER_TO_CLIENT` and `CLIENT_TO_SERVER` are unchanged (no new message type).
3. **Nothing is invented.** Every replayed message is equal, field for field except `replay`, to a message this server actually put through `publish()` for the current turn. *Negative (mutation-sensitive, the failure mode reviewers should aim at):* no replayed message is synthesised from session state, from history, from `turns.jsonl` or from the machine; a test that publishes nothing and then says hello receives exactly today's two-message reply.
4. **Exactly one turn, and never the thought.** The journal holds messages of one `turn_id` only — the newest turn seen on `publish()`. A new `turn_id` clears it; `events.ClientDisconnected` (the last page really gone, after the grace) and `aclose()` clear it. *Negative:* after a second turn opens, no message of the first turn is ever replayed; a token that appeared only in a `thought` segment appears in no replayed message (the wire-side property of T-0028 criterion 11, re-run over the hello reply); no `astra.canvas.*` is journalled or replayed.
5. **The journal is bounded and the replay cannot kill a fresh connection.** Module constants cap what is retained: at most one transcript message, at most one surface message, `REPLAY_MAX_SPOKEN` spoken lines, `REPLAY_MAX_PLAYED` played messages, and a total `REPLAY_MAX_MESSAGES` strictly less than `DEFAULT_OUTBOX`. Publishing 10 000 partial transcripts and 10 000 spoken lines inside one turn leaves the journal at or under its cap (memory, ruling A12's "a connection that lives for an hour must not cost one message per message spoken", applied to the journal). *Negative:* the hello reply of a full journal never overflows a fresh connection's outbox, so a reconnect can never close itself with `outbox overflow`.
6. **A truncated replay is declared, not faked.** If anything was dropped from the journal for this turn, the hello reply carries exactly one `astra.error(code="replay_truncated", message=…)` **before** the replayed lines, whose message names what is missing ("earlier lines of this turn are not replayed"). *Negative:* a complete replay sends no such error; the code is not reused for anything else.
7. **The pump still never blocks.** `publish()` remains a non-blocking, bounded-work hand-off: journalling is O(1) amortised per message with no allocation proportional to the turn's history, no lock, no await, no clock read (T-0028 criterion 12, `.claude/rules/conductor.md`: nothing in the pump blocks the loop for more than 20 ms). *Negative:* `tests/unit/test_hud_egress.py::test_a_stalled_reader_does_not_delay_the_barge_in_pause` and `::test_turn_state_is_the_only_droppable_kind_on_overflow` pass unmodified, and `astra.turn.state` is still the only droppable kind — including its replayed copy.
8. **The transport stays out of the session.** *Negative, static:* `local_astra/conductor/session.py` and `executor.py` are byte-identical after this task (`git diff --stat` shows neither); `tests/unit/test_session_no_egress.py` passes unmodified; the journal lives in `hud_server.py` and reads nothing from `Session` except what already crosses the publisher.
9. **The fake moves with the wire.** `tests/fakes/hud_server.py` (`HudStub`) speaks the new field wherever its default script or helpers construct these models, so the page-side integration rows exercise the same shape as the real endpoint (CLAUDE.md: changing a wire format means changing the fake in the same PR). If the test-author finds no change is needed, that is a recorded finding, not a silent omission.

Group B (the page) — after group A merges.

10. **The page accepts the field and still rejects junk.** `hud/src/protocol.js` mirrors `replay` on the five models; `decode()` returns `kind: "astra"` for a replayed message. *Negative (this is the landmine):* a message carrying a genuinely unknown field (`{"type":"astra.spoken",…,"nope":1}`) is still `kind: "malformed"` — the strictness mirror is extended by one field, not removed.
11. **A replayed message is idempotent in the reducer.** `state.js` applies replayed messages so that a page whose in-memory state already contains the turn (the ws.js backoff reconnect: the socket dropped, the JS state survived, the page re-hellos) ends with exactly one entry per `utterance_id`, exactly one transcript line per turn — never a duplicated final line, never a second copy of an utterance. Transcript lines carry their `turn_id` so a replayed line replaces the line of that turn rather than appending.
12. **The strike survives a reload.** After a barge-in, a page that reconnects and replays sees the same struck tail as before: `played_words`/`complete` from the replayed `astra.spoken.played` are applied on top of the replayed `astra.spoken` (T-0030 criteria 17/18 — the cut comes from a server measurement and from nothing else). *Negative:* no page-side timing, elapsed clock or INTERRUPTED chip dims a single word; a replayed `astra.spoken` arriving *after* a played message for the same utterance still drops the cut (T-0030's rule is unchanged).
13. **The rendering modules do not move.** *Negative:* `hud/src/panels/spoken.js`, `hud/src/panels/transcript.js`, `hud/src/panels/thinking.js`, `hud/styles.css` and `hud/index.html` are byte-identical — replay is a reducer and wire concern; after replay the spoken pane contains exactly one `[data-utterance-id]` element per utterance.
14. **The rehearsal defect is closed end to end.** A new integration row (headless Chrome, real `HudServer` + real `Session` over the fakes) reconnects the page mid-SPEAKING and finds the turn's utterances in the spoken pane, the user's transcript in the transcript pane, the chip on the server's state, and the struck tail present after a barge-in — no `SECRETTHOUGHT…` anywhere in the DOM or on the wire.

## Implementation notes

- **Where the journal lives.** `HudServer.publish()` already sees every server→client message for the session; that is the only honest source (criterion 3) and it keeps `session.py` untouched (criterion 8). Do not reach into `Session` for a second copy of the truth.
- **"Open turn" is defined by the wire, not by the machine.** The journal keys on the `turn_id` of the messages crossing `publish()`; `astra.ui.surface` carries no `turn_id` (it carries `surface_id`), so it belongs to whatever turn was current when it was published — that is a journalling rule, not a protocol change. Do not add `turn_id` to `AstraUiSurface`.
- **`extra="forbid"` on both sides.** `contracts/base.Contract` forbids unknown fields and `hud/src/protocol.js` mirrors that literally (`extra inputs are not permitted`, validated per key). A `replay` field added to Python without the `protocol.js` mirror turns every replayed message into `kind: "malformed"` on the page — counted, ignored, invisible. That is why group B is not optional and why the parity row exists.
- **Ordering matters for the strike.** T-0030's `withUtterance` deliberately drops played state when a new `astra.spoken` for the same `utterance_id` arrives; the replay must therefore put every `astra.spoken.played` *after* every `astra.spoken` (criterion 1), or the strike is lost on reload for the exact case the rehearsal complained about.
- **Bounded-ness is the memory contract.** Transcript partials arrive per word and spoken lines per sentence; keep latest-wins for the transcript and a bounded deque for the rest. Ruling A12's bounded `_written` ring is the precedent.
- **Privacy.** Nothing from history and nothing beyond one turn ever enters this structure; the thought never reaches `publish()` in the first place, and the property test in criterion 4 is the fence, not the hope. *Amended by T-0180 (R-1): narrowed — except the `text` of a replayed `astra.thought`, which the journal keeps (T-0180 A4). No other replayed message carries thought text, and `astra.canvas.*` is still never journalled.*
- **Existing tests are not in the way.** The T-0028 contract rows assert hello-reply content with `any(...)`, never with exact list equality, so no existing test needs editing. If an implementer finds one that does, that assertion encodes T-0028 criterion 4/13 — **stop and report**; the fix is a recorded spec amendment here (and a test-author edit), never a quiet relaxation.
- **Spec deltas for the orchestrator (`/spec`, not this task's diff):** (1) `docs/02-architecture.md` §5 gains the `replay` flag and the hello-replay rule (what is replayed, in what order, bounded to the open turn, `replay_truncated`); (2) T-0028 criterion 13's "gets the current assist and turn state" is amended to "…and the open turn's published lines", and `hud_server.py`'s ownership transfer is noted in `docs/tasks/T-0028.md`.
- **Splitting.** Group A is ~half a day, group B ~a quarter; strict ordering (B needs the field). If group A alone exceeds a day, cut criterion 6 (truncation notice) into a follow-up and make the cap generous enough that truncation cannot occur in a rehearsal turn — but say so, do not silently drop the honesty rule.

## Ambiguity list (decided here; the reviewer may overrule with a recorded ruling)

1. **Replay vs. a banner.** Decided: **replay**, bounded to the open turn. A banner alone ("reconnected — earlier lines are not replayed") leaves the user with a live chip over an empty pane, which is the defect. The banner survives as the *truncation* branch (criterion 6), where honesty is the only option.
2. **Journal lifetime after the turn ends.** Decided: the journal holds the newest `turn_id` seen and survives until a new turn opens, `ClientDisconnected` fires, or `aclose()` — so a reload seconds after a turn finished still shows that turn. Alternative (drop at IDLE) is stricter on privacy and leaves the just-finished turn blank; either is defensible, the reviewer decides.
3. **`replay` on `AstraTurnState`.** Decided: yes, used only for the INTERRUPTED restatement. It is also the one kind the outbox may drop, so a dropped replayed chip costs a label, not a line — stated, not hidden.
4. **Surfaces.** Decided: the most recent `astra.ui.surface` of the turn only (the renderer is idempotent per `surface_id`), not the whole progressive sequence.
5. **Truncation notice as `astra.error`.** Decided: reuse the error strip that already renders (`main.js` `renderErrors`), code `replay_truncated`; a dedicated message type would need `SERVER_TO_CLIENT` and the page's dispatch table to move for one string.
6. **Two pages connected at once.** Decided: replay is per-connection, at hello, to that connection only; `publish()` fan-out is unchanged.

## Rulings

- **A1 (criterion 14 vs criterion 4).** A barge-in opens the next turn within milliseconds (`PAUSED · INTERRUPTED · played · LISTENING t2`),
  so "the struck tail after a barge-in" is unreachable on a reload. Criterion 14 loses that clause; the strike-across-replay is pinned by
  criterion 1's ordering and criterion 12's reducer rows. The journal keeps the newest `turn_id` only (ambiguity 2 as decided).
- **A2 (existing tests edited).** Two existing rows pinned exact shapes and were amended by the test-author, intent preserved:
  `test_protocol_codec.py::test_astra_spoken_is_unchanged` (field list +1) and `tests/hud/state.test.mjs`'s transcript `deepEqual`
  (projected to `{text, final}`). Recorded as the spec amendment the Implementation notes asked for.
- **A3 (trailing turn state).** The hello reply always ends with the current `astra.turn.state` (marked `replay=True`), whether or not an
  INTERRUPTED state was restated.
- **A4.** `test_a_hello_with_no_open_turn_is_todays_two_message_reply` is red only on `replay is False` for the turn state; accepted.
- **A5.** `replay` lives on exactly the five models named in criterion 2; `AstraAssistState` carries none.
- **A6 (fake).** `HudStub.replay_script()` constructs the five models with `replay=True`; it is the fake's change criterion 9 asked for.
- **A7 (activation).** Group A and group B Python markers removed by the orchestrator after the test-author's commit (5e65b01); node rows
