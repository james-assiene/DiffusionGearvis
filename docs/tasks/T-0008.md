# T-0008 — HUD page: transport, 1 fps frames, two channels, thinking meter

**Status:** merged 2026-09-13 (re-review MERGE); M1 human click open · **Blast radius:** medium · **Depends on:** T-0002 (contracts/codec, merged in the scaffold)

## Goal
A plain, buildless web page at `hud/` that is the eyes and the face of the assistant: it opens one
WebSocket to the Conductor, announces itself with `astra.hello`, captures the shared tab or camera
and posts JPEG frames at ≤ 1 fps as `astra.frame` (docs/01 D6; `.claude/rules/perception-memory.md`),
and renders the two output channels — the *spoken* channel (`astra.transcript`, `astra.spoken`) and
an empty, labelled *shown* channel region that T-0010 fills — plus the turn-state chip and the
thinking meter driven by `astra.turn.state` (docs/01 D11, D12). On the Python side it adds the other
half of that path: `BrowserFrameSource`, the v1 `PerceptionClient` (docs/02 §5) that ingests
`astra.frame`, keeps the 60 s ring, answers `get_frame(at=speech onset)` and `clip()`, and emits
`SceneChanged`. After this task the assistant can be *shown* something from a browser tab and the
user can see what it is doing; nothing here renders A2UI, denoising or assist checkboxes.

## Acceptance criteria

Criteria 1–13 are the page; 14–18 are the Python ingest. They are independent. **If the implementer
is past a day of work at the end of criterion 13, stop and report**: criteria 14–18 become a
follow-up task at the next free id (T-0018 — T-0017 was taken by the xargs contract task after this was written) with this file's notes copied
across. Do not stack the two halves into one long session.

**Page — transport**

1. **Hello and the client whitelist.** On socket open the page sends exactly one `AstraHello`
   (`contracts/protocol.py`) with `protocol_version == PROTOCOL_VERSION` and `client == "hud"`, and
   it is the first message on that socket; a reconnect sends exactly one more. *Negative:* over any
   run, the only client→server types this task's code can emit are `astra.hello` and `astra.frame`
   — never `astra.frame.text` (`AstraFrameText`, T-0012), `astra.assist.toggle`
   (`AstraAssistToggle`, T-0012) or `astra.ui.action` (`AstraUiAction`, T-0010). Cites
   `protocol.CLIENT_TO_SERVER`, docs/02 §5.
2. **Decoding is total, exactly as `contracts/codec.decode` is.** For every inbound frame the page
   reaches one of four outcomes and never throws, and the socket is still open afterwards: known
   `astra.*` in `protocol.SERVER_TO_CLIENT` → typed dispatch; `astra.*` not known → ignored, counted
   (the codec's `Unknown`); `type` not starting with `astra.` → ignored as a Realtime passthrough
   (`RealtimeEvent`); malformed JSON, missing/non-string `type`, a message over
   `codec.MAX_MESSAGE_BYTES`, or a known type with bad fields → ignored, counted (the codec's
   `Malformed`). `AstraError` renders its `code`/`message` in an error strip. Cites the `codec.py`
   module docstring ("the Conductor answers with `astra.error` and never a crash" — the HUD's mirror
   of the same rule).
3. **Reconnect and backpressure.** A closed socket reconnects with bounded exponential backoff
   (250 ms initial, 8 s cap, jitter) and there is never more than one socket object alive. While
   disconnected or while a send is in flight, at most **one** pending frame is retained; older ones
   are dropped and the drop count is displayed. *Negative:* no unbounded outbound queue and no
   `bufferedAmount` growth across a 10 s disconnection.

**Page — frames**

4. **Rate and single-flight.** At most one `astra.frame` per source per second
   (`perception/policy.py` `MAX_FPS = 1.0`). Driven by a 60 fps source for 10 s of controlled time
   the page emits 10 ± 1 frames per source, and at no point are two encodes or two sends in flight
   for the same source: a slow encode causes a *drop*, never a queue.
5. **Frame shape.** Every emitted message validates against `AstraFrame` when decoded by the real
   `codec.decode`, with: `source` ∈ `perception.Source` matching the capture kind; `width`/`height`
   exactly `policy.target_size(w, h, policy.bucket_for(source, w, h))` for that source (screen →
   `screen_max`, camera → `CAMERA_BUCKET`, never upscaled); `jpeg_b64` base64-decoding to a JPEG
   (`ffd8` … `ffd9`) encoded at `policy.JPEG_QUALITY`; `phash` a 16-character lowercase-hex dHash;
   `ts_ms == Date.now()`. The bucket/quality/fps constants used by the page are **compared against
   the Python values** by a test, so a divergence fails rather than silently changing what the model
   sees (`.claude/rules/perception-memory.md`: "changing a bucket requires re-running B2").
6. **Mute is at the pipeline, not the consumer.** The mute control (button and keyboard shortcut)
   calls `stop()` on every track of every stream and releases the stream; afterwards no
   `astra.frame` is produced for any source. *Negative:* setting `track.enabled = false` (which
   leaves the browser capture session and its indicator alive) does not satisfy this; unmuting
   requires a fresh acquisition call. Cites `.claude/rules/perception-memory.md` ("global mute stops
   capture at the pipeline level, not at the consumer") and docs/01 §3.
7. **Synthetic capture is labelled.** A stream that did not come from
   `getDisplayMedia`/`getUserMedia` (the injected provider the tests use) makes the page show a
   persistent, visible `SYNTHETIC CAPTURE` banner for as long as it is the source. *Negative:* there
   is no code path in which a synthetic source renders the normal capture chrome. Same honesty rule
   as `astra.canvas.synthetic` (docs/12 §2, docs/01 D15).

**Page — the two channels and the meter**

8. **Spoken channel and user transcript.** `AstraSpoken` appends to the spoken pane grouped by
   `turn_id` in arrival order, keyed by `utterance_id` (a repeated `utterance_id` updates in place,
   it does not duplicate). `AstraTranscript` with `final=false` replaces the live partial line;
   `final=true` freezes it and a later partial starts a new line.
9. **Thinking meter.** On `AstraTurnState` the state chip equals `state` (`contracts/turn.TurnState`).
   While `state == THINKING` the meter shows `thinking_ms` and advances with the page clock between
   messages; **every** message snaps it to the server value, including when the server value is
   *smaller* than the local extrapolation. Marks are drawn at 3000 ms (the ~2000-token thinking
   budget) and 4000 ms (the interim-update point) — docs/01 D11. *Negative:* a `turn.state` whose
   `state != THINKING` never starts or advances the meter, even if it carries a non-null
   `thinking_ms`, and no elapsed number is shown outside THINKING.
10. **Panel extension points.** `hud/src/panels.js` mounts optional panels by dynamic import of
    `./ui/index.js` (T-0010), `./denoise/index.js` (T-0011), `./assists/index.js` (T-0012). With all
    three absent — the state today — the page renders completely, logs one line per missing panel,
    and the shown-channel region displays a "no surface yet (T-0010)" placeholder. A loader that
    *throws* is caught and leaves every other panel and the socket working. This is what keeps the
    three later tasks out of T-0008's files.
11. **Interrupted, and no invented played/unplayed state.** `AstraTurnState(state=INTERRUPTED)` marks
    that turn in the spoken pane. *Negative:* the page renders **no** struck-through / played-prefix
    distinction. `AstraSpoken`'s docstring promises one, but no wire message carries played text
    (`MachineState.played_text` is server-side only). This is a **named spec gap for T-0007**: the
    strike-through ships when the protocol carries the field, and not before. Do not fake it from
    timing.
12. **Loopback only.** The WebSocket URL defaults to `ws://127.0.0.1:8000/v1/realtime` and may be
    overridden by a `?ws=` query parameter **only** for hosts `127.0.0.1`, `localhost` or `[::1]`;
    any other host is refused and the page shows an error instead of connecting. *Negative:*
    `hud/**` contains no absolute `http(s)://`/`ws(s)://` literal outside comments pointing anywhere
    but loopback, no CDN import, no `node_modules`, no build step, and the page works with no network
    route (docs/02 §9; CLAUDE.md "all services bind to loopback only").
13. **The HUD never touches audio.** *Negative:* `hud/**` contains no `AudioContext`, no `<audio>`
    element, no `getUserMedia({audio: …})` and requests no audio track (`getDisplayMedia` is called
    with `audio: false`). The Conductor owns PipeWire capture and playback — docs/02 ADR-013,
    docs/01 D7.

**Python — `BrowserFrameSource` (`local_astra/perception/browser_source.py`)**

14. **Conformance and ingest validation.** `BrowserFrameSource` satisfies the `runtime_checkable`
    `PerceptionClient` protocol (`isinstance`, the `tests/contract/test_fakes_conform.py` pattern).
    `ingest(AstraFrame) -> Frame | None` base64-decodes `jpeg_b64` into `Frame.jpeg`, and **rejects**
    (counted, no exception escaping) a frame whose `phash` is not the expected hex length — because
    `policy.scene_changed` raises `ValueError` on differing hash lengths, and that must never be
    reachable from client input. `Frame.ts_ms` is stamped from an **injected** receive clock, not
    from the client's `ts_ms`, which is retained only as a jitter figure.
15. **Ring and onset selection.** One 60 s ring per source (`policy.RING_BUFFER_S`), independent
    across `screen`/`window`/`camera`, additionally capped by frame count so a client that ignores
    the 1 fps rule cannot grow it without bound. `get_frame(source, at_ms)` returns the frame at or
    *just before* `at_ms` and never a later one; `at_ms=None` returns the latest; an empty ring
    returns `None`. Cites docs/03 §3 "Perception policies" ("the frame at speech onset, not the
    latest at end-of-turn") and `.claude/rules/perception-memory.md`.
16. **`clip`.** `clip(source, t0_ms, t1_ms, fps=1.0)` returns frames inside the window, ascending by
    time, spaced ≥ `1000/fps` ms, and ≤ 61 frames for a 60 s window (docs/03 §4 "Perception").
17. **Scene events with hysteresis.** `events()` yields only `contracts.events.SceneChanged`
    (`source`, `phash`), emitted when `policy.scene_changed` says so against the last *accepted*
    frame, with hysteresis: an alternating A/B/A/B sequence must not emit an event per frame.
18. **No I/O in the module.** `browser_source.py` opens no socket, reads no clock and touches no
    file — time and identity arrive as arguments (the same discipline as the turn machine, docs/02
    §4). Mypy `--strict` clean.

## Implementation notes

- **Contracts are frozen here.** `protocol.py`/`codec.py` are the spec; the page mirrors them, never
  the reverse. If something is unrepresentable, stop (criterion 20). The known example is the
  played/unplayed gap in criterion 11.
- **`AstraFrame.ts_ms` means the browser's `Date.now()`.** Decide it here and write it down in
  `hud/README.md`: the Conductor selects frames by *receive* time because the page's clock is not the
  audio clock, and `get_frame(at=speech_onset)` compares against audio time. Getting this wrong is
  invisible — the model just sees a slightly wrong screenshot.
- **`policy.scene_changed` raises on mismatched hash lengths.** Validate at the boundary (criterion
  14); a client-supplied string must never reach it unchecked.
- **Message size.** `codec.MAX_MESSAGE_BYTES` is 8 MiB; a `screen_max` JPEG at quality 85 is roughly
  300–500 KB after base64. Refuse and log a frame that would exceed 1 MB rather than shipping a 6 MB
  one the codec would happily accept and the socket would stall on.
- **Keep DOM out of `state.js`, `protocol.js`, `frames.js`, `phash.js`, `ws.js`** so `node --test`
  can import them; DOM lives in `panels/` and `main.js`. Inject `getStream`, the clock, and the
  socket factory — that is what makes criteria 4, 7 and 9 testable without a browser and without a
  permission dialog.
- **ESM over `file://` does not work** (cross-file module imports are CORS-blocked); tests and the
  screenshot command serve `hud/` over `127.0.0.1`. A single inline module in one HTML file does work
  from `file://`, which is why the probe passed — do not generalise from it.
- **`hud/` is excluded from ruff (`pyproject.toml` `extend-exclude`) and from mypy**, so the JS side
  has no automated style gate; keep it small and boring. `browser_source.py` must pass
  `mypy --strict` (mypy `files = ["local_astra"]`).
- **No new Python dependencies.** The page computes the dHash (it has the pixels); the server
  validates its shape only. Do not pull in `pillow`/`imagehash` — that would be a `pyproject.toml`
  change this task does not own.
- Rules to re-read before starting: `.claude/rules/perception-memory.md` (pixel-first, buckets,
  60 s ring, `get_frame(at=onset)`, mute at the pipeline, "the frontend never handles audio") and
  `.claude/rules/tests.md` (no tautologies; the fast suite stays under two minutes).
- `scripts/hud_shot.sh` already exists and works; add only `scripts/hud_dom.sh` (the `--dump-dom`
  sibling) and leave `hud_shot.sh` alone.
