# T-0120 — Tool-loop compaction, the pure helper: `loop.compact(steps, budget)` elides older tool responses

**Status:** done 2026-10-17 (merged; reviewer approved after the not-connected stub row at b921512; verified: `make fast-parallel` 52.64 s, 6403 passed, load 0.9; scope guard closed BASE..TIP at 2055c5b) · **Blast radius:** medium (one pure module, no session change; one reviewer) · **Branch:** `T-0120-loop-compact` from `main` at `4f84b04` · **Parent:** T-0111a; ruling H-T115-0 · **Depends on:** T-0113 (merged), T-0114 (merged) · **Blocks:** T-0115a (its E-rows import `loop.compact`), T-0120b (session wiring, planned after the T-0115 reading) · **Concurrent with:** T-0115 (CPU half), not with anything that owns `local_astra/toolcall/loop.py`

## Goal

The tool-result loop gets a deterministic way to shrink a continuation that would be over budget. `compact(steps, budget)` returns the same steps when they fit. When they do not, it replaces the responses of older steps, oldest first and never the last `keep_last`, with a short stub rendered through the template renderer, until the continuation fits a lower target. Calls and step texts are kept byte for byte. Nothing calls it yet: the session's over_budget path is unchanged in this task. T-0115a measures whether elision hurts answers, and T-0120b wires it into the session with thresholds measured by T-0115.

Planner's arithmetic (unverified; T-0115 S6 measures it): one realistic search step is about 90 chars of call + ≤ 1 500 of response + 0–200 of withheld step text, so 8 maximal search steps are 12.7k–14.3k chars, about at `MAX_CONTINUATION`. H-T114-8's "4 heavy steps" assumes a 1 000-char step text and a 5 000-char `type_at` on every step.

## Ruling recorded (H-T115-0, human, 2026-10-17, "Go with your suggestion")

- **Mechanism:** deterministic elision of older tool responses. The last k stay in full; calls and step texts are kept.
- **Timing:** applied once, when the next continuation would exceed a threshold, not at every step.
- **Model summaries:** none for now.
- **Code:** a pure `compact(steps, budget) -> steps` in `toolcall/loop.py`, called by the session before the over_budget check (the call is T-0120b's).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Identity under budget (negative; the "once" timing).** If `continuation_chars(steps) <= budget`, then `compact(steps, budget)` returns a tuple equal to `tuple(steps)` whose elements are the same objects. `continuation_chars(steps)` is the length of the final assistant text that `step_prompt` would build from `steps`.
2. **Elision order and the kept tail.**
   - Over budget, responses are elided one step at a time, oldest first, among `steps[:-keep_last]` only.
   - Elision stops at the first point where `continuation_chars <= target`.
   - Defaults: `keep_last = COMPACT_KEEP_LAST = 2` and `target = budget * 2 // 3`, which is 9 333 for 14 000.
   - `keep_last < 1`, `budget < 1`, or `target > budget` raises `ValueError`.
3. **What is kept.** Every step's `text`, `name` and `arguments` are unchanged (equal, not merely equivalent), and the step count is unchanged. Only `response` changes, and only on elided steps.
4. **The stub (H-T120-3).** An elided response becomes `elided_payload(original)`:
   - a mapping with a list under `results`: `{"note": "earlier result omitted", "results": len(original["results"])}`;
   - a mapping with a `status` key: `{"note": "earlier result omitted", "status": original["status"]}`;
   - anything else: `{"note": "earlier result omitted"}`.

   It renders through `render_response`. The expected text is hand-written in the test: `<|tool_response>response:google_search{note:<|"|>earlier result omitted<|"|>,results:5}<tool_response|>`. A response whose rendered stub is not shorter than its own rendering is never elided.
5. **Idempotent and monotone.**
   - `compact(compact(s, b), b) == compact(s, b)`.
   - A stub is recognised by its payload and never re-elided.
   - For `s2 = compact(s, b) + (x,)`, every step elided in `compact(s, b)` is still elided in `compact(s2, b)`, with identical rendered bytes. This is the prefix-stability property the cache depends on.
6. **May still be over.** If eliding every candidate does not reach `budget`, the result is the fully elided steps and is still over `budget`. The caller decides; for the session that is today's `over_budget`.
7. **Purity and imports (T-0111a criterion 13, negative).** No I/O and no clock. `loop.py` imports only the stdlib, `local_astra.contracts` and `toolcall.special_tokens`. The outputs of `render_call`, `render_response`, `step_prompt`, `search_payload`, `action_payload` and `status_payload` are unchanged: every T-0113 row passes unedited.
8. **Negative.** `local_astra/conductor/session.py`, `MAX_CONTINUATION`, `turn_machine.py` and `contracts/**` are unchanged, pinned `4f84b04..TIP`. The fast suite stays under its 60 s parallel budget.

## Implementation notes

- Measure with the same rendering `step_prompt` uses, including its `lstrip()`. Do not re-implement the template.
- Recognise a stub structurally (the payload carries `ELIDED_NOTE`), so a stub that comes back to `compact` is stable.
- Keep `LoopStep` frozen; return new `LoopStep`s for elided steps only.
- Do not add a config knob, and do not touch `session.py`. Both are T-0120b's.
- Implementer, 2026-10-17: `step_prompt` and `continuation_chars` share one private join (`_pieces`/`_continuation`), so the measure is the step-prompt text by construction and `step_prompt`'s output is unchanged. `compact` renders each step once and re-joins the cached pieces after each elision. A `results` value counts as a list when it is a `list` or `tuple`. `target` below 1 is not rejected (the criterion names only `target > budget`).

## Open questions (defaults in bold; H = human)

- **H-T120-1:** `keep_last` **2**: the latest result plus one, enough to compare a refinement with its predecessor.
- **H-T120-2:** target **`budget * 2 // 3`**. After one compaction about 3 more maximal search steps fit before the next one, so an 8-step loop compacts at most twice.
- **H-T120-3 (H):** the stub text **"earlier result omitted"**, keeping the `results` count or `status`. The alternative is the ruling's `status:"elided"`; the word "elided" may not be understood by the model.
- **T-0120b (not planned yet):** the session calls `compact(turn.loop_steps, MAX_CONTINUATION)` before the size check and keeps the compacted steps as `turn.loop_steps`. It logs a `note {what:"tool loop compacted", step, elided, chars_before, chars_after}` and adds `tool_compactions` to the turn line. Loop-off and fitting loops stay byte-identical. The threshold is a token-checked value from T-0115's chars-per-token reading. High blast, two reviewers; serialise with T-0114b, T-0117, T-0118 and T-0119 on `session.py`.
