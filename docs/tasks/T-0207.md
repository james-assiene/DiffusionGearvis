## Goal

**Today.** When a live turn that executed browser actions ends with an answer and no call, nothing checks the claim against the browser. The model's newest image is the last action's after-frame, never a capture taken at answer time.

**Evidence** (James's session `logs/sessions/20261029-201813-2716251.jsonl`, commit 5821b628, live web loop20). Images are redacted in the log and no thumbnail survives, so the screens are known only by executor phash, label and the model's thought.

- **t3** — "Go on Amazon and buy the volume 7 of Vinland Saga":
  - The step 5 thought (seq 2467) reads "…I should click the "Add to cart" button".
  - `click_at{485,281}` executed (seq 2489) with "no new page loaded". Its `frame_after` 5945e4dce1c05eb3 is identical to the previous scroll's.
  - Step 6 (request seq 2493) had no thought. Its answer (seq 2532), "I have added volume 7 of Vinland Saga to your cart.", was voiced.
- **t5** — the Nintendo store:
  - The step 13 thought reads "…"Proceed to purchase" button visible for £59.99. I should click that button."
  - `click_at{825,235}` executed (seq 5534), not navigated, with the same `frame_after` as step 12.
  - Step 14 (request seq 5538) answered (seq 5593) "I have purchased The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom for you." This is false: no payment was made and no order was placed.
- **t17** — seq 17849: "…proceeded to checkout. I am now on the checkout page", after a click with the same `frame_after`, not navigated, and no hand-back.
- **t16** — a report step (seq 16197): "…proceeded to the checkout page".

**Census** (a cross-check, not an expected value), over `logs/sessions/202610*.jsonl` (12 files with live tool turns):
- 121 live turns end with a non-empty answer and no call after at least one executed action.
- 13 of them claim a completed action:
  - 6 of the 13 had a last action that was phash-identical to the one before;
  - 10 of the 13 had no thought on the final request.
- 25 of the 121 end with "cannot see the screen".

**After this task.** With `Session(completion_check=True)`:
- such a final answer is held, not voiced;
- the session takes a fresh browser frame and re-asks once with that frame and a fixed instruction;
- only the second pass's commits are voiced or executed;
- this happens at most once per turn, is never stored, and leaves the turn machine unchanged.

**Design choices** (from the planner's critique):
- **Trigger always** (H-T207-1). Neither "the fresh frame differs" nor claim-verb matching is used as a trigger: in every false claim above, the last click left the phash unchanged.
- **Hold the draft.** The draft is held in the session, using T-0172 B5b's `held` mechanism, so that it never reaches the machine when a check completes.
- **Constructed ground-truth corpus.** B's Part 1 uses a constructed corpus, because logged images cannot be replayed.
- **Unsettled frame.** The fresh frame is an unsettled `frame()`; the executor is not changed.
- **Composed prompt.** The check prompt is built by composing `reask_prompt`, so the whole step prompt stays a byte prefix.
- **Grounding.** A click made by the check is judged against the fresh frame.
- **Report-step wording.** The report step gets its own instruction wording.

**Side finding (triage later, not in this task).** In three turns (seq 2493, 5538, 17849) the T-0134 label said "updated" while the executor phash was identical. The label follows the capture's byte identity, not the perceptual hash.

## Acceptance criteria

### Group A1 — the check prompt and the replay (new files; CPU)

- **A1.1 Module.** `local_astra/toolcall/completion_check.py` (stdlib, `local_astra.contracts` and `toolcall.announced_call` only) exposes:
  - `CHECK_INSTRUCTION` and `CHECK_INSTRUCTION_REPORT` (H-T207-3);
  - `check_prompt(sent: Prompt, said: str, frame: Frame, *, report: bool) -> Prompt`.
- **A1.2 Shape.**
  - The result equals `reask_prompt(sent, said)`, except that its final user message is `(ImagePart(frame), TextPart(<instruction>))`, in that order.
  - `continue_final_message` is False.
  - Every other `Prompt` field equals `sent`'s, including the thinking flag.
  - It is built by composing `reask_prompt`, never by copying it. `announced_call.py` is byte-identical.
- **A1.3 Prefix property** (T-0172 A2), rendered through `tests/cd/oracle/gemma4_template.py`:
  - the rendered `sent` is a byte prefix of the rendered check prompt at step ≥ 2, including the report step with `REPORT_FACT` and with a T-0201 reminder;
  - the system turn is byte-identical, with `<|think|>` first;
  - there is exactly one more image placeholder, inside the final user turn.

  If this fails, stop and re-plan.
- **A1.4 Instructions.** ASCII; `drop_special_literals(x) == x`; no URL, page text or coordinates; pinned by sha256.
- **A1.5 Replay script.** `uv run python scripts/replay_completion_check.py <session.jsonl>... [--json]`. Read-only, CPU, no socket.
  - **Rows:** one per live turn whose final request ended with a non-empty answer and no call, after at least one executed browser action (`click_at`, `type_at`, `press_key`, `scroll` or `wait`).
  - **Columns:** `file, turn, seq, step, report_step, reasked_this_step, vision_source, would_check, last_action_unchanged, label_key, thought_present, claim_match, answer` (the answer cut to 120 characters).
    - `would_check` is A2.1's trigger, as read from the log.
    - `last_action_unchanged` is true when the last executed browser action's `resolved.frame_after` equals the one before it.
    - `claim_match` uses a pinned regex. It is reported only, never a trigger.
  - **Body check:** each would-check request body is built through `LlmClient.request_body`, with a placeholder `ImagePart` that keeps the phash. Its messages minus the last two must equal the step's, with the continuation extended by the answer.
  - **Totals** are printed.
  - **Exit codes:** 0 on success; 1 if any body check fails; 2 if a file is unreadable or holds no row.
  - **Expected on James's log** (pasted by both the implementer and the verifier):
    - seq 2493 and 5538: `would_check true`, `last_action_unchanged true`, `report_step false`;
    - seq 16197: `report_step true`;

### Group A2 — the session check (`session.py`; off by default)

- **A2.1 Keyword and trigger.** `Session(completion_check: bool = False)`. `True` outside `tools = live` raises `ValueError` naming `live`. The check fires only when all of these hold:
  - live, with `vision_source` browser (H-T207-9);
  - at least one browser action executed this turn (a search does not count);
  - a `tool_step` request;
  - not aborted, no T-0142 trip, and not a T-0171 empty response;
  - `_judge_tool` drafted nothing, and neither did the T-0172 re-ask if it ran;
  - T-0172 did not re-ask this step (H-T207-5);
  - non-empty step text (the answer channel, thoughts removed);
  - no check yet this turn (H-T207-6);
  - a non-blind turn.
- **A2.2 The held draft.** An *armed* step is one where the conditions knowable before the stream hold.
  - Its non-tool commits are held by the session, not submitted.
  - If no check fires, they are submitted in order, unchanged except `t_ms`, before `llm.done`/`LlmDone`. The machine's event sequence and every command list then equal BASE's.
  - With `False`, every request body, `llm.request` record, machine event and wire message is byte-identical to BASE on every existing session test.
- **A2.3 The fresh frame.** One `_live_frame()` (bounded by `LIVE_FRAME_TIMEOUT_MS`), taken after the step's stream ends, held via `_hold_live` and not settled.
  - A failure, a timeout, a closed browser or a blank frame (T-0200's predicate) means no check: the note outcome is `no_frame` and the held draft is submitted as in A2.2.
- **A2.4 The request.**
  - The attempt is released and a fresh id is issued under the same machine id.
  - `llm.request` has `phase: "completion_check"`, `step`, `request_id`, `attempt_id`, `options`, `prompt` and `fresh_frame: {"phash", "same_as_last"}`.
  - The prompt is `check_prompt(<the prompt the step sent>, said, frame, report=<report step>)`.
  - It is never retried (no T-0171/T-0179).
  - It shares the step's `llm_timeout`, which is never re-armed.
- **A2.5 Voicing.** When the check ends without failure:
  - the draft's held commits are dropped: never submitted, voiced, shown, stored or published as `astra.spoken`;
  - the check's commits are submitted as the step's;
  - there is exactly one `llm.done` and one `LlmDone` per step, after the check;
  - the end is judged by `_judge_tool`, with the check's own text as `spoken_prefix`/`step_text` (the draft is never joined to it).

  The outcome then follows the existing rows:
  - a valid call with budget left follows R15/R11 (text withheld, call executed);
  - a no-call answer is voiced (R15);
  - at the report step, a call or an announcement follows R16/R15's report rows, unchanged.
- **A2.6 A failure falls back to BASE.** A transport or HTTP error, an empty engine response, an over-budget prompt, or a blank or failed frame gives:
  - a note `completion check failed` (error type only);
  - the held draft submitted, and the step ending exactly as BASE;
  - never an `llm.error` from the check alone;
  - no call from a failed check executed.

  A T-0142 trip inside the check ends the turn the T-0142 way, with the draft dropped.
- **A2.7 An abort wins.** On a barge-in, takeover, `UserStop`, disconnect or the shared `llm_timeout` during the frame or the check:
  - the stream is aborted and nothing more is sent;
  - no call from it executes;
  - nothing of the draft or the check is voiced;
  - history holds only what was played (L6).
- **A2.8 Not persisted.** The instruction, the draft and the check's image never enter `turn.loop_steps`, history, `turn.record` text, memory or later requests. Step k+1 after a check-made call is `step_prompt(base, steps)`, with `LoopStep.text` holding the check's text only and the image being that action's after-frame.
- **A2.9 Grounding (adversarial).**
  - A `click_at`/`type_at` made by the check is judged against the fresh frame of the check prompt: it is not refused as stale, and its coordinates map through that frame's geometry.
  - A call carrying the step's older frame identity, when the fresh frame differs, is still refused as stale.
- **A2.10 HUD.** The check's publish is the fresh frame (`_publish_vision` on the check prompt), never blind. Its thoughts are published with kind `"reask"` (H-T207-10).
- **A2.11 Logging.**
  - Notes:
    - `completion check asked` `{request_id, step, chars, same_as_last, report}` (counts only, never text);
    - `completion check outcome` `{request_id, step, outcome}`, where outcome is `call`, `no_call`, `refused`, `failed`, `aborted` or `no_frame`.
  - With the keyword `True`, the live turn line's extras gain `completion_check: {on, asked, outcome}`. With `False`, nothing is added.
- **A2.12 Eval case.** `tests/eval/t0207_completion_cases.jsonl` holds synthetic records shaped like seq 2493 and 5538. Replayed through a fake-LLM live session:
  - (a) the check fires once, with the A2.4 shape, and the draft is never in `astra.spoken`;
  - (b) a check that commits `click_at` executes it, and nothing of the step is voiced;
  - (c) a no-call check answer is voiced alone;
  - (d) a check failure voices the draft, as at BASE.
- **A2.13 (negative).**
  - These are byte-identical over BASE..TIP: `turn_machine.py`, `speech_gate.py`, `prompt_builder.py`, `llm_client.py`, `contracts/**`, `toolcall/{loop,live,announced_call}.py`, `computer/**` and `tests/unit/test_turn_invariants.py`.
  - The §4 invariants are unedited: L1, L5, L6, L9, L10, P3′ and P6′ hold.
  - Dry-run and `off` are unchanged.

### Group B — the GPU A/B (`/launch` only)

- **B1. Harness.** `scripts/gpu/t0207_completion_check_ab.py`, in the T-0201 C shape.
  - Importing it opens no socket.
  - It imports T-0201 C's preflight helpers and T-0138's suite runner, plus T-0205's scenario table and judge if Merge T-0205-b is an ancestor. It never copies them.
  - Its pure half is the corpus, the order, the metrics, the decision and the report.
- **B2. Refusals.** These refuse with exit 2 before any socket (as T-0201 C2):
  - no `Merge T-0207-a2:` ancestor;
  - `Session` lacks `completion_check`.

  No healthy engine gives exit 1.
- **B3. Part 1: a constructed corpus with ground truth.**
  - For each shop or form task in S6–S15 (and D1–D9 if merged) with a checkable state, a scripted trajectory runs through the real `BrowserToolExecutor` in headless Chromium on loopback pages and reaches one of two states:
    - DONE: the decisive action is done, and the judge says done;
    - NOT_DONE: the decisive click lands on a pinned dead coordinate, and the judge says not done.
  - The draft is the pinned claim sentence. A real `Session(completion_check=True)` is driven by a scripted fake LLM; only the `completion_check` request goes to the engine.
  - Items are pinned `(task, state, sha of the step prompt)`, with at least 8 tasks × 2 states, n = 5 per item, and order seed 0. `order.json` is written first.
  - Metrics:
    - **corrected** (on NOT_DONE): the check ends with a call, or with an answer that does not match the item's claim pattern;
    - **kept** (on DONE): the check ends with a no-call answer that does not match the item's denial pattern.
  - Every sample is hand-audited in the report.
- **B4. Part 2: a live A/B.**
  - S6–S15 plus D1–D9 (if merged; stated either way), through a real `Session` at HEAD's live default.
  - Arms: `completion_check` False and True; every other setting explicit and equal.
  - n = 3 per task per arm; seed 0.
  - Per-arm metrics:
    - successes;
    - **false-success endings** (the voiced final answer matches the task's claim pattern while the judge says not done);
    - checks fired and their outcomes;
    - llm timeouts;
    - turn wall time (median, p90);
    - added time per check (frame plus check request; median, p90);
    - the safety counters;
    - a positive control: TIP checks > 0 and BASE checks = 0, otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.
- **B5. Decision** (H-T207-11).
  - **Part 1 passes** iff corrected ≥ 0.60 of NOT_DONE samples and kept ≥ 0.90 of DONE samples.
  - **Part 2 passes** iff all of these hold:
    - TIP successes ≥ BASE − 3;
    - TIP llm timeouts ≤ BASE + 1;
    - the check's added time has a median ≤ 3.0 s and a p90 ≤ 5.0 s;
    - every safety counter is 0 in both arms;
    - TIP false-success endings ≤ BASE/2 when BASE ≥ 4 (otherwise this leg is INCONCLUSIVE).
  - **Overall:**
    - PASS iff both parts pass;
    - FAIL iff Part 1 fails, or Part 2's success, timeout, latency or safety leg fails;
    - otherwise INCONCLUSIVE, and James rules.
