# T-0052 — a surface longer than one canvas is reassembled, and interface data can never reach the voice

**Status:** **merged 2026-09-16** (723275d; both groups together per ruling A19) — planned, tested, implemented in two concurrent groups, **reviewed by four agents** (two per group), revised twice, and verified. Suite **2315 passed, 0 failed**, reproduced by the verifier at two load levels. The verifier drove ruling A19 end to end for the first time and confirmed both halves: group A alone does reopen a route by which JSON reaches the voice, and group B is what closes it. **Owed and still open:** criterion 16's `gpu` eval row stays `pending` for a `/launch`, and the successor **T-0054** carries the fence lifecycle into `session.py`, the turn record and the panel — until it lands, a refusal is counted on the gate and **no log line records it** (ruling A33)

**This is not a protocol that can be skipped.** It is not a one-line diff: the fence rule that a merged task decided on purpose is being withdrawn, a contract gains a field, a second module gains a refusal that changes a documented test-plan property, and the failure it fixes put JSON into the speakers.

## Goal

The human asked for a flight-booking interface and the model built a good one — origin and destination fields, two date pickers, a passenger chooser, a search button, a divider, a results list. It did not fit in one generation canvas, so it arrived in two pieces. The first piece (409 characters) was recognised as `ui`, gated and published; the panel showed half an interface. The second piece (750 characters of JSON, then 152 characters of ordinary prose) began mid-way through a component list in the next canvas, and `VllmClient.generate` — which calls `split_segments(reasoning, content, False)` on every chunk and throws away the `in_ui_after` the splitter returns — classified it as `answer`. It went to the text channel and to Pocket TTS, which read `","children":["src","dest"]},{"id":"src","component":"TextField"…` aloud for 807 characters. The stray-marker filter did not catch it because a JSON fragment is not markup. After this task the client carries the fence across the canvases of one request, closes it on the explicit `</a2ui>` **or** on the structural end of the JSON (the model never wrote a closing fence tonight, so threading alone would have swallowed the spoken tail instead — the opposite failure), abandons it loudly at a bound rather than swallowing a whole answer, and hands the continuation to the gate that has always known how to morph a surface across canvases. And, independently of any of that, the speech gate refuses to voice a string that is structurally interface data — so a future failure of fence tracking is a missing surface and a logged refusal, never a synthesiser reading JSON. Non-goals: no change to the prompt, to `ui_gate`, to `session.py`, to the wire, to the HUD page, to the turn machine, or to any threshold.

## Acceptance criteria

Each is independently checkable. "The flight canvases" means the fixture reconstructed from the session log (see *Regime*). Criteria 1–7 and 12–14 are group A; 8–11 and 15 are group B; 16 is `gpu`, owed via `/launch`.

1. **Tonight's turn, replayed through the real client, no longer speaks JSON — and still speaks the answer.** Driving `VllmClient.generate` over a stub SSE transport that replays the three flight canvases in order yields: (a) no `answer` piece containing `{"` or `"component"`; (b) the 750-character JSON tail of canvas 2 classified `ui`; (c) the 152 characters of prose that follow it in the *same* canvas classified `answer` and non-empty — the turn still speaks. Both halves are required: (a) alone is satisfied by a client that goes silent, which is the other failure. *(Reproduces the origin; docs/02 §3 turn flow; test-plan Q11 "error taxonomy: in-canvas vs canvas-boundary".)*
2. **The surface is reassembled, not half-published.** Feeding the `ui` commits produced in criterion 1 into a real `UiGate` (same turn id, ascending `canvas_idx`) yields a final surface whose components include ones that existed only in the *second* canvas — **the ids `row1`, `row2`, `row3`, `divider`, `results`, measured, see ruling A6** — in addition to those from the first. Asserted by id, not by count. *(T-0010 criteria 1–3, progressive render; docs/12 §1.1.)*
3. **The fence state is threaded per request and reset per request.** Two `generate` calls in sequence, the first ending with the fence open, the second beginning with ordinary prose: the second call's first chunk is classified `answer`. No module-level or client-level state survives a request. *(architecture §4: at most one active generate per session; state that outlives a request is a cross-turn leak.)*
4. **An explicit close still closes.** A fence opened in canvas *n* and closed with `</a2ui>` in canvas *n+1* produces exactly two `ui` commits, no `answer` piece containing a fence marker or a fragment of one, and everything after the closing marker classified `answer`.
5. **A fence with no closing marker closes at the structural end of the JSON.** When the carried `ui` text reaches bracket depth ≤ 0 outside a string and the remainder (after whitespace) does not resume with `{`, the fence is closed *there*: the JSON is the `ui` commit, the remainder is `answer`. The commit records that the close was inferred, not written by the model. Tonight's canvas 2 is the reference case. *(This is the criterion that keeps threading from converting "speaks JSON" into "says nothing".)*
6. **An unclosed fence cannot swallow the rest of a response.** With a fence open and every later canvas continuing to look like JSON, the carry ends at a stated bound — at most `UI_FENCE_MAX_CANVASES` further canvases *and* at most `UI_FENCE_MAX_CHARS` characters of carried `ui` text, whichever comes first. At the bound exactly one `ui` commit is emitted marked abandoned, and every subsequent canvas is classified `answer` again. Total carried `ui` characters ≤ the bound, asserted numerically. *(`.claude/rules/conductor.md`: "a subsystem that silently disables itself must publish that it did" — the abandonment is emitted, never a silent drop.)*
7. **A response that ends with the fence open publishes what it has and says so.** On SSE `[DONE]`, on stream close, and on `abort`, any carried `ui` text is emitted as a final `ui` commit marked unclosed; it is **never** reclassified as `answer`, and no `answer` commit is emitted for it. *(Design decision D2 below; §4 invariant "never `tts.speak` text that has not been committed" is not weakened — it is that this text is committed to the *shown* channel.)*
8. **The recorded utterance cannot be spoken, even with the fence tracking of group A absent.** Feeding the exact 902-character canvas-2 text through `split_segments(None, text, False)` — the **old**, pre-fix call, standing in for any future regression of group A — and handing every resulting `answer` piece to a fresh `SpeechGate` yields **no sentence whose `text` is voiced** for interface data, and the refusal is counted on the gate. **The refused sentence is still returned, with its `source` intact and `text=""` (ruling A20): the `Segmenter` contract the turn machine polices is `"".join(sources) + remainder == text`, and a gate that drops an item breaks it.** **The 92-character prose sentence in the same text is still emitted** (ruling A31: the other 56 characters of prose sit *inside* the refused 810-character source and come back with `text=""`; the original wording claimed all 152 and was never true on this path). *(The safety rule: "never voice text the LLM has not committed as prose". The guarantee lives downstream of the classifier, so a classifier bug cannot reach the voice.)*
9. **The refusal predicate is narrow and stated.** A sentence is refused when its committed source contains **both** a JSON object opener (`{`) **and** a quoted member of a pinned set of A2UI keys — adjacency is **not** required, see ruling A8 — (`"component"`, `"surfaceId"`, `"createSurface"`, `"updateComponents"`, `"catalogId"`, `"children"`, `"version":"v0.9.1"`), or when — for a source longer than **`STRUCTURAL_MIN_CHARS`, which is set at the observed size of a real A2UI source and not at 40** (ruling A21), **that also contains a `{`** (ruling A9) — structural characters (`{}[]":,`) exceed a stated fraction of its length. The pinned key set and the fraction are module constants a test reads, not literals buried in a regex.
10. **The refusal is counted and retrievable.** `SpeechGate` exposes the number of refused sentences and their sources for the turn (a counter and a bounded list). A refusal is not `print`ed, not logged from inside the pure module, and not raised. *(The publishing of it is group C / T-0054; this criterion is what makes that possible.)*
11. **A refused sentence does not stall the gate.** After a refusal the gate's pending buffer is in the same state as if the sentence had been emitted: the following sentence is emitted normally, and `close()` still flushes. A refusal loses text; it must not lose the rest of the answer.
12. **The commit carries the fence lifecycle.** `Commit` (and therefore any consumer that copies it) can distinguish: a fence opened and closed in one canvas; a `ui` commit that *continues* a fence opened in an earlier canvas; a close the model wrote; a close inferred structurally (criterion 5); a response that ended open (criterion 7); a carry abandoned at the bound (criterion 6). The field is optional with a default so that no existing constructor, fake or fixture becomes invalid. *(Answers "was this surface reassembled, abandoned, or never there?" — design decision D4.)*

## Implementation notes

### Decisions taken at plan time (the implementer does not reopen these)

**D1 — thread the fence across the chunks of one request, and only one request.** State lives in the `generate` frame (or an explicit value object threaded through it), never on the client instance; a retry after 400/413 is a new `generate` call and starts clean. *What it breaks, honestly:* (i) it assumes SSE content deltas are **append-only**. Tonight's log supports that — canvas 2 continues canvas 1 mid-token (`…"component":"Row` → `","children":["src"…`) — but this is a diffusion model and a *revised* rather than appended canvas would make the carried state describe text that has already been replaced. There is no way to detect that from the stream today; D3 gives it a defined landing and *Ambiguity 1* queues the question for the GPU session. (ii) it reintroduces exactly the hazard M7 named. D2 and D3 are the price of it, and they are not optional.

**D2 — a fence still open when the response ends publishes what it has, marked incomplete; it is never re-read as prose.** Of the three failures: *treating it as prose* is tonight's bug and is refused outright; *discarding* it loses a surface the gate could have salvaged and leaves the panel showing the half that was already published, with no record of why the rest never came; *publishing what there is* costs at most a truncated surface — which the gate already prunes to what validates, and which the panel is already showing anyway. So: emit the carried text as a final `ui` commit marked unclosed, let the gate rule on it, and record the verdict. The failure mode this accepts is a surface that looks finished but is not; D4 is how the human finds out.

**D3 — a carry is bounded three ways and every ending is announced.** (a) the explicit `</a2ui>`; (b) the structural end of the JSON — bracket depth ≤ 0 outside a string, remainder not resuming with `{` (criterion 5; A2UI surfaces are *concatenated* top-level objects, so depth 0 alone is not an ending); (c) the bound — `UI_FENCE_MAX_CANVASES = 4` and `UI_FENCE_MAX_CHARS = 8192`, both module constants with the arithmetic in their comment: the measured surface was 1159 characters over 2 canvases and T-0044 A7's minimal surface 1060, so 4 canvases ≈ 3400 characters is ~3× the observed need; a bigger bound would let a stuck fence eat a whole answer, a smaller one would cut a legitimate dashboard. A second `<a2ui>` arriving while open ends the carry as abandoned and opens a new fence. Whichever ending fires, a commit is emitted saying so — `.claude/rules/conductor.md`: a component that turns something off owes a published state change.

**D4 — yes, the human must be told, and the contract carries it from this task while the surfacing waits.** A surface that arrived in pieces and was reassembled, one that was abandoned, and one that never existed are three different things and the panel shows the same thing for all three. This task puts the distinction on `Commit` (criterion 12) so it exists at the point it is known; carrying it into the session log, the turn record (`ui_canvases`, `ui_fence`, `speech_refused`) and an `astra.ui.surface` status the panel can render needs `session.py`, `contracts/protocol.py` and `hud/src/panels.js`, all held by open tasks — that is **T-0054**, ordered after them. Until then the evidence lives in the `llm.commit` record's text and in `ui.gate`'s `problems`, which is how tonight was diagnosed.

**D5 — the second line of defence belongs in this task, and it belongs in the speech gate.** It is worth having regardless of whether the fence tracking is correct, precisely because it is the one guarantee that does not depend on the classifier being right: `SpeechGate` is the single funnel through which every committed answer string becomes a spoken sentence (`session._segment` feeds it, the machine's `TtsSpeak` carries its `source`, and T-0030's played-text ledger keys on that same source), it is pure, and it already normalises rather than echoes (T-0022 speaks an unclosed code fence as "a code block"). Putting the refusal in `llm_client` instead would make one module both the classifier and its own safety net — the arrangement that failed tonight. The refused text is **dropped, not summarised**: the surface is already on screen and the model's own prose usually says what it built, so a spoken "an interface" would double-speak (*Ambiguity 3* names the decider if a reviewer disagrees). Dropping is only acceptable because criterion 10 makes it countable and D4 makes it publishable.

### Constraints and landmines

- `split_segments` is a documented pure helper called by three test modules and the eval; a carry needs more than a bool (an open flag, a held partial marker, a character count, a canvas count). **Recommended shape:** a small frozen state value and one new pure entry point (`split_canvas(reasoning, content, state) -> (pieces, state)`), with `split_segments` kept as a thin adapter over it so there is exactly one implementation. Two parallel implementations will drift; a hard signature break churns rows that encode real behaviour. The criteria pin behaviour, not names.
- The held partial marker is new and easy to get wrong: today a trailing `<a2u` is *dropped* by `_strip_markup` (L10). With a carry it must be *held* and prepended to the next chunk's content, and only dropped if the response ends while it is held. Any other arrangement either voices `ui>` or loses the opening of a real fence.
- The structural close scanner is a **bracket/quote scanner, not a JSON parser**: tonight's payload is malformed (`{"Text","text":…}`, a run of `}}}}`). It must handle strings and `\` escapes, never raise, and treat depth < 0 as depth 0. Do not import `ui_gate` from `llm_client` — the layering runs the other way.
- `Commit` is a Pydantic v2 contract in `local_astra/contracts/`; the new field must default so `tests/fakes/llm.py` and every existing fixture stay valid (CLAUDE.md: a wire-format change means changing the fake in the same PR — keeping it optional is how that stays a one-line change, and the fake is the **test-author's** file, not the implementer's).
- `mypy --strict` on `local_astra/`; `llm_client.py` is in the strict set. No `Any` leaking out of the scanner.
- The client stays I/O-shaped as it is: no clocks, no logging from the pure helpers. `generate` is an async generator whose body runs on first `__anext__`; the abort/tombstone dance at `_begin`/`_end` (M5) must keep working — criterion 7 explicitly covers abort with a fence open.
- The speech gate's `Sentence.source` must stay the byte-exact committed substring for every sentence that *is* emitted (architecture §4 invariant, T-0030's ledger depends on it). The guard inspects the source and either emits it unchanged or does not emit it.
- Do not touch `ui_gate.py`: it already does reassembly (`_restore_deferred`, `_prior`, per-component pruning). If reassembly does not work in criterion 2, the bug is in what the client hands it, or it is a genuine gate finding to be raised — not a gate edit inside this task.
- The fast suite ceiling is 120 s serial / 60 s under the Stop hook. New rows are pure and cheap; the eval row is `gpu` and must never be collected by a path-only run (T-0046).
