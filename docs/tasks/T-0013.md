# T-0013 — Filler policy

**Status:** merged 2026-09-13 (review MERGE) · **Blast radius:** low · **Depends on:** T-0003

## Goal
`local_astra/reflex/filler_policy.py::decide` implements the decision table of docs/03 §3
"Filler policy" and the interim spoken update of docs/11 §1.1.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Thinking on → `clip:ack` at `llm.start` (elapsed 0) regardless of the EMA.
2. Thinking off → `clip:ack` iff predicted first canvas > `ack_threshold_ms`.
3. Never a filler once `first_canvas_committed`; never two fillers within `min_gap_ms`; at most `max_fillers_per_turn`.
4. `clip:still_working` at `elapsed ≥ interim_update_ms` once, only when no canvas has committed.
5. `generate_short` is never returned in v1 (reserved for the talker profile).

## Rulings after test activation (2026-09-13)

**A1 — "once" for `clip:still_working` is derived, not flagged.** `FillerInputs` gains no field:
an interim update has already gone out iff the previous filler was emitted at
`elapsed_ms - last_filler_age_ms >= interim_update_ms`. `last_filler_age_ms` is age since the
previous filler's *emission*, so `min_gap_ms` is measured from emission.
**A2 — boundaries:** `ack_threshold_ms` strict (`predicted > threshold`; `== 300 → none`, matching
docs/02 §4 row 6); `min_gap_ms` inclusive (age `== min_gap` is allowed); `interim_update_ms`
inclusive (`>=`, as criterion 4 writes it).
**A3 — fillers only while `THINKING`.** Every other state (IDLE, LISTENING, ENDPOINTING, SPEAKING,
PAUSED, INTERRUPTED) returns `none` whatever the inputs. Criteria 1–5 never said so; docs/02 §4
does.
**A4 — `clip:hmm` is not produced in v1.** It stays in the `Decision` literal (docs/03 §3) but no
criterion fires it; a later task adds the rule with its own fixtures.
**A5 — a late first tick** (no ack played yet, `elapsed > 0`) is unspecified and untested; the
implementer may ack on the first call of the turn or not, and must say which in the docstring.

**Merged 2026-09-13 (review MERGE; 14 mutations red, 3 equivalent survivors).** A5 docstring
corrected at merge (a first tick at or after `interim_update_ms` acks and never sends
`still_working`). For the wiring task (T-0007): the turn machine acks when the EMA is *unknown*
(`first_canvas_ms is None`) but `FillerInputs.predicted_first_canvas_ms` is a plain `int` — map an
unmeasured EMA to a value above `ack_threshold_ms`, or the session's first turn loses its ack.
Uncovered-but-licensed cell for a later test row: thinking off + fast EMA → no ack, then
`still_working` at 4 s as the turn's first filler.
