# T-0111f — GPU probe: grounding and the coordinate convention on the served NVFP4 model at the live frame size (ScreenSpot-Pro)

**Status:** parked 2026-10-19 (human: not a priority; cua's repo has SSP numbers; continue implementing without it) · **Blast radius:** medium (one GPU harness and one locked script run by one `/launch`; zero diff under `local_astra/`, `services/`, `plugin/`; the reading may lead to a later schema task, not this one; one reviewer plus the launch-reviewer) · **Branch:** `T-0111f-grounding-probe` from `main` · **Parent:** T-0111 (sub-task row T-0111f; human 2026-10-19: "focus on computer control") · **Depends on:** the human's local ScreenSpot-Pro copy (P0) before the CPU calibration and the launch · **Concurrent with:** T-0107, T-0126 and anything not owning the files below · **Runs in:** `scripts/locked/t0111f_grounding_probe.sh`

**Protocol cannot be skipped:** this is a new measurement with a pre-registered decision rule.

## Goal

Before the browser executor (T-0111c) exists, we need three facts about the served model as configured today:
- How often it puts a `click_at` point inside the target on a real screenshot, downscaled exactly as a live screen frame is.
- Whether it speaks our 0–999 grid, a 0–1000 grid, or pixels.
- How many visual tokens a live screen frame actually costs on the engine.

This task runs a fixed, stratified ScreenSpot-Pro (SSP) subset through the live prompt builder, tool kit and native tool-call reader in one fresh-boot `/launch`. It scores point-in-box accuracy per arm, group and target size. It then decides one of four outcomes:
- keep 0–999;
- adopt 0–1000, as a separate follow-up task;
- the model emits pixels;
- inconclusive.

Nothing in the product changes.

**Evidence that shaped this plan:**
- **The live convention is 0–999.** See `local_astra/toolcall/tools_v1.json` (`click_at`/`type_at` `maximum: 999`), `local_astra/conductor/tool_executor.py:5-7,22-26` (`round(v*(size-1)/999)`) and `local_astra/toolcall/score.py:11`.
- **The cua repo used 0–1000.** See `<cua-diffusion-gemma clone>/src/schema.py:8-13` and `src/chat_template.py:44`.
- **cua's SSP numbers are not a baseline for this task.** They were measured on fine-tuned adapters, not the base model. Its "grounding accurate" finding came from reading OSWorld episodes (`outputs/notes/FAILURE_MODES.md`).
- **The visual-token count is unverified, and the gap is likely to matter.** `perception/policy.py:14` assumes 1120 visual tokens per screen image, and `services/vllm.sh` passes no mm-processor kwargs. The cua repo found the processor default to be 280 (`src/infer.py:313-316`).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Harness `scripts/gpu/t0111f_grounding_probe.py` (new).**
   - Importing it opens no socket.
   - Arguments: `--base-url`, `--ssp-root`, `--out-dir`.
   - It reuses, read-only and by path, T-0093's `check_loopback` and T-0115a's request path, `/metrics` deltas and `/tokenize`.
   - Concurrency 1, unseeded, `LlmOptions()`.
   - Output: `logs/t0111f-grounding-<sha7>/` containing `manifest.json`, `requests.jsonl`, `report.json` and `report.md`.
   - Exit codes: 0 complete, 1 stopped or no engine, 2 refused.
2. **Refusals (exit 2, before any socket), in this order:**
   1. non-loopback;
   2. dirty tree (paths named);
   3. existing non-empty out-dir;
   4. a tool kit other than `native:bbfc53ccdd80` with repair on;
   5. an `--ssp-root` without `annotations/*.json` and `images/`;
   6. annotation shas ≠ `SSP_ANNOTATIONS_SHA256` (pinned at P0);
   7. a sampled image missing, or ≠ `SUBSET_IMAGES_SHA256`;
   8. subset ≠ `SUBSET_IDS`.

   It never downloads. `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1` is set, and nothing under `huggingface_hub` is imported.
3. **Subset (pre-registered; H-T111f-4).**
   - Load every annotation in sorted file order and then file-index order; each item's id is `<file stem>#<index>`.
   - Stratify by (`group`, `ui_type`). Draw **n = 300** proportionally, with largest-remainder rounding, ≥ 1 per non-empty stratum, and `random.Random(0)` after sorting within each stratum.
   - The ids, their sha256 and the per-stratum counts are committed in `docs/cd/grounding-probe.md` before the launch.
   - The remaining SSP items are not read.
4. **The live frame.**
   - Each image is resized with PIL to `policy.target_size(W, H, policy.SCREEN_BUCKETS[0])` (bilinear) and encoded as JPEG at `policy.JPEG_QUALITY`.
   - It goes through `PromptBuilder(DEFAULT_SYSTEM_PROMPT, thinking=True, tools=…)` exactly as T-0115a's `_base_prompt` does: one typed turn, no history, assists off, no memory.
   - The manifest records the original W×H, the sent w×h, the JPEG sha256 and byte count, and the gold bbox scaled to the sent frame.
   - Resampler parity with the browser's `drawImage` is a known difference and is recorded as such.
5. **Prompt (pre-registered; H-T111f-1).** `Click {instruction}`, using SSP's `instruction` field verbatim, plus a final period if the instruction has none. Nothing else. The template's sha256 goes in the manifest.
6. **Arms (paired; H-T111f-3).**
   - **A (live):** the kit from `cli.load_tool_kit`, unchanged.
   - **B (0–1000):** a harness-local `ToolSet` built in memory from `tools_v1.json`. Only `maximum` 999 → 1000 on `click_at`/`type_at` x and y changes, plus every `999` → `1000` in their descriptions. The tool sha is recorded and `tools_v1.json` is not edited.
   - Both arms run per item. A goes first on even positions and B first on odd ones, for 600 requests in all.
   - 10 consecutive errors stop the run.
7. **Scoring (pure; the primary metric).**
   - **The prediction** is the first `segment == "tool"` commit outside an open thought, parsed by `toolcall.native.parse_call`.
   - **A hit** needs a `click_at` that is schema-valid under its own arm's schema, with its point inside the sent-frame bbox (inclusive) after mapping by the arm's own mapping: A `/999`, B `round(v*(size-1)/1000)`.
   - **Every request counts in the denominator.** Each of these is a miss with its own class: no call, another tool, `type_at`, out-of-range, a thought-open final, an empty answer, an errored request.
   - **Report-only diagnostics:**
     - a hit under the other arm's mapping;
     - x/y swapped;
     - the values read as sent-frame pixels;
     - the values read as original pixels;
     - a `type_at` point hit;
     - distance from the bbox, in px and in grid units.
8. **Recorded per request:**
   - id, arm, stratum and sizes;
   - status, error and commits;
   - the parsed call and its miss class;
   - every diagnostic;
   - prompt tokens (`/tokenize`, outside timed spans);
   - `/metrics` prefix deltas;
   - first-commit ms and done ms;
   - canvases.
9. **Visual-token measurement (report-only; H-T111f-6).** For 6 fixed items, one per group, `/tokenize` the arm-A prompt with and without its image. The difference is the image's token count. Record it per sent w×h beside `policy.MAX_SCREEN_VISUAL_TOKENS` (1120), and flag `visual budget below the policy's 1120` if any count is lower. No bar, and no engine change.
10. **Void and controls.** Failing either makes the outcome `inconclusive: <reasons>`.
    - **V:** errored ≥ 18/600, or the run is incomplete.
    - **C:** arm-A `click_at` calls at any point ≥ 150/300.
11. **Convention rule (pre-registered; H-T111f-3).** Applied in order, after V and C:
    1. **`model emits pixels`** iff ≥ 30 of arm A's 300 items have a `click_at` with x or y > 999, or the sent-frame-pixel diagnostic beats arm A's primary by ≥ 30/300.
    2. **`adopt 0–1000 (follow-up task)`** iff B hits − A hits ≥ 9/300 and an exact two-sided McNemar test on the discordant pairs gives p < 0.05.
    3. **`keep 0–999`** otherwise.

    Report-only: exactly-999 and exactly-1000 counts per arm, the swap gain, and the discordant counts.
12. **Report-only accuracy (H-T111f-2).** No accuracy bar.
    - k/300 per arm with a Wilson 95 % interval.
    - Breakdowns by group × ui_type, by original-resolution bucket, by target-area bucket (< 0.1 %, < 0.5 %, ≥ 0.5 % of the sent frame) and by miss class.
    - p50/p95 latency.
    - cua's figures are quoted as an external reference only (fine-tuned, bf16, a different regime), never as comparable.
13. **Locked script `scripts/locked/t0111f_grounding_probe.sh` (new).**
    - Takes no arguments. Copies `t0115_loop_probe.sh`'s boot regime verbatim, with its refusals: dirty tree, `services/vllm.sh` changed since 806da12, ports bound, containers running, once per SHA.
    - Additionally refuses an unset or missing `$T0111F_SSP_ROOT`.
    - Runs `make plugin-wheel`, then the boot and the health loop (600 s), then the harness under `timeout 75m`, then the gpu eval rows. It prints the sha256 of `report.json` and `requests.jsonl`, stops the engine on EXIT, and unsets proxy variables.
14. **Eval `tests/eval/test_grounding_probe.py` (gpu rows only).** It re-derives every hit, diagnostic, tally, V/C check and the convention outcome from `requests.jsonl` and the manifest with its own scorer. It skips on gate-sha drift, covering the harness, `toolcall/native.py`, `toolcall/tools_v1.json`, `conductor/tool_executor.py`, `conductor/prompt_builder.py`, `perception/policy.py` and `conductor/llm_client.py`.
15. **Write-up `docs/cd/grounding-probe.md` (new).**
    - Before the launch: the pre-registration, with criteria 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11 and 12 verbatim; the subset ids and their sha; the P0 dataset revision and annotation shas; the calibration numbers.
    - After the run: the results with their regime, the visual-token reading and the outcome.
16. **CPU calibration on the human's local copy (pre-launch).** The pure scorer on synthetic predictions:
    - (a) the gold bbox centre hits 300/300 in both arms;
    - (b) one grid unit outside the box's nearer edge misses, wherever the box is ≥ 2 units from the frame edge;
    - (c) cua's own formula agrees with arm B's primary on the centre predictions;
    - (d) a uniform random predictor (`Random(1)`) scores within ±3σ of the analytic mean of the boxes' area fractions.

    These numbers are committed before the launch. If any of them fails, the scorer is fixed before the launch, never after.
17. **Negative.**
    - Zero diff under `local_astra/`, `services/`, `plugin/` and `hud/`.
    - `tools_v1.json` and the kit sha `bbfc53ccdd80` are unchanged.
    - Earlier harnesses and locked scripts are byte-identical.
    - No network calls, and no SSP data committed.
    - No second run without a ruling.
    - The fast suite gains ≤ 1 s serial.

## Implementation notes

- **Guard hook.** Bash may not name `scripts/gpu` or `scripts/locked`. Use Edit/Write, and never route around the hook.
- **SSP fields** as cua read them: `img_filename`, `bbox` [x1,y1,x2,y2] in original pixels, `instruction`, `img_size`, `group`/`application` and `ui_type`. Confirm them on the human's copy; a difference is a finding to record, never a silent remap.
- **Request body.** Sampling is limited to `top_k`, `top_p`, `max_tokens` and `stop`. Tools-on bodies go through `VllmClient` only.
- **Thinking stays on.** Pass-1 drafts only, with no repair. Record the drafts the router would route `repairable` (report-only).
- **Visual tokens.** Report what `/tokenize` says. Do not pass `mm_processor_kwargs`, and do not change any engine flag.
- **Runtime (unverified).** 600 requests × about 1.5–3 s is 15–30 min; with boot that is about 25–40 min wall, with a 75 min bound. Measure the mock wall time and state it in the `/launch` request.
