## Goal

In the T-0125 search smoke at b372eda (`logs/t0125-search-smoke-b372eda/phase1/sessions/20261027-112444-1064873.jsonl`), two turns ended with only a promise: turn 2, `t2-gen-s7`, seq 904: "I will search for the current weather in Paris again."; turn 3, `t3-gen-s2`, seq 1047: "I will search for the latest stable version of Python.". In both the thought split was correct and `llm.done` carried `tool: null`; row R15 (no call) voiced the promise as the final answer.

Not specific to search. **Planner's census** (a cross-check, not the expected values) over every `logs/**/sessions/*.jsonl` on this box (2,313 files, 6,681 `generate`/`tool_step` requests): 1,987 tools-on requests ended with no call and a non-empty answer (45 tools-off). **74** of the tools-on ones are a short answer made only of first-person future intent ("I will add the blue mug to your cart." ×18, "I will scroll up to find the booking form.", the two smoke records, "I will search for example.com on Google." from the T-0160 web smoke); **32 more** end in such a sentence after an observation ("I don't see a booking form on this page. I will try to find the booking section."). Of the 106, 5 at step 1, 101 at step ≥ 2; 0 among the 45 tools-off. Announcing then calling is the norm (~1,800 requests). Near-misses the rule must reject: "I will identify the cheapest item from the list.The cheapest item is the cotton tea towel at …", "Please let me know what items you are looking for.", "I will add the cotton tea towel to the cart.\nI'm adding the cotton tea towel to the cart." (labelled by the test-author), and synthetic "I will remember that.", "I'll keep that in mind.".

This task adds a pure, tested detector; when a final no-call answer only announces an action, it makes one more engine request in the same step, under the same machine request id, from the same context with the announced sentence already said, plus an appended nudge; at most once per turn; never changing the system prompt or the cached prefix; turn machine untouched; with an off switch.

## Acceptance criteria

### Group A: the rule, the prompt helper and the census (CPU; may start now)

**A1. Rule.** `local_astra/toolcall/announced_call.py` (stdlib and `local_astra.contracts` only) exposes `announces(text: str, *, mode: Literal["tail", "all"] = "tail") -> Announcement | None`, linear, never raises; `Announcement` is a frozen dataclass with `verb` and `sentence`. Non-`None` iff all hold:
- **Size:** stripped text non-empty, ≤ 240 characters, ≤ 3 sentences (split on `.`, `!`, `?` followed by whitespace or end, and on newlines).
- **No question:** no `?` anywhere.
- **Intent:** the last sentence (`tail`) or every sentence (`all`) matches: an optional lead-in from a closed list (Now, Next, Actually, Okay, So, Then, First); one of `I will`, `I'll` (either apostrophe), `Let me`, `I am going to`, `I'm going to`, `I shall`; an optional `now|first|then|also|just`; an optional `try to` or `try`; a verb in the closed `ACTION_VERBS` frozenset (at least: search, look, check, find, click, tap, press, open, go, visit, navigate, browse, scroll, type, enter, fill, select, deselect, add, remove, refresh, reload, submit, fetch, retry, try).
- **No denial:** the verb is not in `DENY_VERBS` (at least remember, keep, note, wait, tell, explain, answer, provide, know, think, be); no conditional addressed to the user (`if you`, `when you`, `once you`, `should you`); no `let me know`.
Case-insensitive. `"tail"` is the default (H-T172-1).

**A1b. Precision amendment (2026-10-27, after the group A review).** The census corpus is shopping and booking test pages; conversational answers were absent, and A1 as first written hits "I'll go with option two.", "I'll look into it later.", "Let me check with you first.", "I'll go ahead and stop here.", "I'll try my best.", "I'll check back later.", "I will look forward to it.", "Let me check: it is 20 degrees in Paris.". In addition to A1, the matched sentence returns `None` when:
- the verb is followed by a phrasal particle that makes it non-actionable: `go with`, `go ahead`, `go over`, `go through`, `look forward`, `look into`, `check back`, `check with`, `try my best`, `try harder`, `try again later`;
- the sentence contains a deferral word after the verb: `later`, `tomorrow`, `next time`, `another time`, `soon`;
- the sentence names the user as the object (`with you`, `for you first`, `ask you`);
- the verb is followed by a colon (the answer is delivered inline: "Let me check: …");
- the intent pattern must start the sentence (anchored, `match` not `search`): "Unless you object, I will search again." is `None`;
- the conditional and `let me know` check applies to the whole text (any sentence), as implemented.
`try to` followed by a non-action word is `None` ("I'll try to help."). All A4 thresholds still hold with A1b (recall ≥ 0.95, precision 1.0).

**A1c. `go ahead and <verb>` (2026-10-27, group A review round 2).** A1b's `go ahead` particle also refused real agentic announcements ("I'll go ahead and add the mug to your cart.", "I will go ahead and click the submit button."). `go ahead and` followed by a verb is read as that verb: the sentence is judged as if `go ahead and` were absent ("I'll go ahead and add …" → verb `add`, a hit; "I'll go ahead and stop here." → verb `stop`, not an action verb, `None`). `go ahead` not followed by `and <verb>` stays `None`.

**A2. Re-ask prompt.** `announced_call.reask_prompt(sent: Prompt, said: str) -> Prompt` returns `sent`'s messages, plus `Message(role="assistant", content=(TextPart(said),))`, plus `Message(role="user", content=(TextPart(REASK_NUDGE),))`; `continue_final_message=False`; every other field equal to `sent`'s (thinking flag included). For a step ≥ 2 `sent` (a continuation) the final assistant message is replaced by one whose text is the continuation's text followed by `said`. `REASK_NUDGE` is one constant (wording the implementer's) that tells the model it said what it would do and made no tool call, asks for that call now, and otherwise asks it to tell the user in one sentence what stopped it. Rendered through `tests/cd/oracle/gemma4_template.py`, the re-ask prompt's text starts with the rendered `sent` as a byte prefix at step 1 and at step ≥ 2, and the system turn is byte-identical with `<|think|>` first. If the oracle shows the prefix property fails for either shape, stop and report (the no-user-turn fallback — `said` appended inside the continuation, `continue_final_message=True`, no nudge — needs a re-plan).

**A3. Replay script.** `scripts/replay_announced_calls.py <path>... [--json] [--rule tail|all]`, read-only, CPU, no network: for every `generate`/`tool_step` request in a tools-on session whose `llm.done` has `tool: null`, that kept no `tool` commit and has a non-empty joined answer, one line (file, request id, step, verdict, verb, answer cut to 120 characters); totals (scanned, no-call answers, hits per rule, announce-then-call); exit 0, or 2 on usage error or malformed input (naming the file).

**A4. Census.** The test-author builds `tests/fixtures/t0172/labels.jsonl` with `build_labels.py`: hand labels for every tools-on no-call answer holding first-person future intent anywhere; every other no-call answer is a negative by definition; labels made without importing `announced_call`; files under `logs/sessions/` (private chats) counted, never copied. Thresholds on the logs with `tail`: precision 1.0 (0 hits on labelled negatives), recall ≥ 0.95, both smoke records hit, 0 hits on tools-off requests. `all`'s recall reported, not gated.

**A5. Synthetic table.** `None`: "I will remember that.", "I'll keep that in mind.", "Let me know if you need anything else.", "Should I search for that?", "I will search for it if you want.", "I can search for that.", "I searched for the weather.", "Let me think about that.", "I will be here.", "The Eiffel Tower is 330 metres tall.", and "I will identify the cheapest item from the list.The cheapest item is …". ### Group B: the session re-ask, cli/config and docs (after `Merge T-0171`, `Merge T-0163`, `Merge T-0172-a`)

**B1. When it fires.** Only when all hold: `Session(reask_announced_calls=True)`; tools on (`dry_run` or `live`); a `generate` (step 1) or `tool_step` request; not aborted, no T-0142 trip, not a T-0171 empty response; `_judge_tool` returned no draft (refused or repairable drafts do not qualify); `announces(step_text)` is not `None` (`step_text` = the answer channel with thoughts removed); no re-ask yet this turn; `step − 1 < max_tool_steps`.

**B2. The request.** Previous attempt id released, a fresh id from `_ids.issue(machine_id)` under the same machine request id; logged `llm.request` with `phase: "announce_reask"`, `step`, `request_id`, `attempt_id`, `options`, `prompt`; prompt `reask_prompt(<the prompt this step sent, label and frame included>, step_text)`; no request in the turn has a different system message, tools or images before the appended messages.

**B3. Voicing.** Step ≥ 2: no `tts.speak` between the step's last original commit and the end of the re-ask; exactly one `llm.done` record and one `LlmDone` event for the step, after the re-ask; a valid call with budget left → R15 withholds the whole step text (announcement and re-ask text), and the `step text withheld` note counts both; otherwise the announcement then the re-ask's answer are voiced as one step. Step 1: the announcement is voiced as today; a re-ask call executes after the drain (T-0089 T11/T14 unchanged).

**B4. Judging the re-ask's end.** Same `_end_tools_on_stream` → `_judge_tool` path; `spoken_prefix` and `step_text` are the announcement followed by the re-ask's own text; T-0107e, T-0107a, T-0135 recovery, the T-0142/T-0142b guard, `live.classify` and P5′ apply unchanged. Every `tool.call` record of the re-ask's call carries `after_reask: true`, and only those. An executed step's `LoopStep.text` is the announcement followed by the re-ask's text. No later request contains `REASK_NUDGE` or the extra user turn (step k+1 prompt equals `step_prompt(base, steps)`).

**B5. Never twice, never worse.** At most one `announce_reask` request per turn. If the re-ask fails (transport/HTTP error, T-0171 engine-empty, over-budget prompt): a note `announced call re-ask failed` (error type); the step ends as without the re-ask (`LlmDone` with no tool, announcement voiced); never an `llm.error` from the re-ask alone; no T-0171 retry of the re-ask. A T-0142 trip inside the re-ask ends the turn the T-0142 way.

**B5b. A failed re-ask voices none of its own text (amendment 2026-10-27, reviews A and B).** The re-ask's answer commits are held in the session, at every step, and submitted to the machine only when the re-ask ends without a failure. If it fails after some commits arrived (transport/HTTP error, engine-empty, over-budget), those commits are dropped: nothing of the re-ask is voiced or stored, no call from it executes, the outcome is `failed`, and the step ends exactly as without the re-ask (announcement voiced at step ≥ 2 per R15; already voiced at step 1). A T-0142 trip inside the re-ask keeps the T-0142 ending, outcome `failed`. Rationale: a fragment such as "Searching now." voiced and then nothing is a false claim of action, worse than BASE.

**B4a. Joining (amendment).** `spoken_prefix`, `step_text` and `LoopStep.text` join the announcement and the re-ask's text with one space when neither side already has whitespace at the join; otherwise unchanged.

**B6. Abort and barge-in win.** Abort, takeover/barge-in, `llm_timeout` (shared, never re-armed) or disconnect during the re-ask: its stream is aborted, no call from it executes, no further request, history holds only what was played (never the nudge).

**B7. Logging.** Notes `{"what": "announced call re-asked", "request_id", "step", "verb", "chars"}` (a count, never text) and `{"what": "announced call re-ask outcome", "request_id", "step", "outcome"}` (`call`, `no_call`, `refused`, `failed`, `aborted`). With the keyword not `None` and tools on, every turn line gains `announced_call_reask: {"on": <bool>, "asked": 0|1, "outcome": <str|null>}`. No new span or log kind.

**B8. Keyword.** `Session(reask_announced_calls: bool | None = None)`; `True` without a tool kit raises `ValueError` naming `dry_run` and `live`; `None` and `False` behave as BASE.

**B9. cli and config.** `DEFAULT_ANNOUNCED_CALL_REASK: bool = True` (H-T172-2). `Profile.announced_call_reask: bool | None = None`; load refuses a non-bool (naming `true`/`false`) and a non-`None` value with `tools = "off"`. `--announced-call-reask {on,off}` exits 2 with `--tools off` (no graph, no page server). `cli.reask_of(profile, args, mode) -> Reask(value, source)` (flag, profile, default; `(None, "unset")` in `off`; pure). ## Implementation notes

- The turn machine stays pure and unchanged; the re-ask is session I/O like T-0135 recovery and the T-0171 retry. The decision comes before `self.submit(E.LlmDone…)` and the `llm.done` record.
- Prefix cache: never touch the system prompt or `<|think|>`; append only after the exact prompt the step sent (live label, current frame, overview included); do not rebuild from `loop_base`.
- With T-0171: an empty response never counts as an announcement; the re-ask's empty response is a B5 fallback with no retry; at most 3 engine requests per step.
- The re-ask shares the step's 20 s `llm_timeout`; never extend or re-arm.
- Smoke turn 2: steps 5 and 6 were refused with "search limit reached"; the nudge's "say what stopped you" branch exists for this; no search-limit special case without a ruling.
- `.claude/rules/conductor.md`: only answer commits reach the gate; the nudge never stored or published; notes carry counts; a subsystem that turns itself off says so.
- No spoken-prefix gate before re-asked calls (H-T104, D16). Defaults live only in `cli`; `Session`'s default stays `None`.
