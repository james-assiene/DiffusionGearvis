# T-0070 — Evaluation harness: BFCL-Live simple and Sudoku 4×4 over the running server, five arms, parity scorers, host replay, statistics, coded hard stops, calibration driver

**Status:** done (merged at da7af86; status corrected 2026-10-23 from 'in progress', T-0147 audit: no implementation in flight) · earlier: in progress · **Blast radius:** medium · **Branch:** `T-0070-cd-eval` · **Depends on:** T-0063, T-0064 (runs in
parallel with T-0065–T-0068). Needs the human's BFCL data (T-0063).

## Goal
A host-side harness can run any arm on any cut against the loopback vLLM server. It scores with diffgemma-fa's scorers
as they stood after the 2026-08-08 rescoring. It computes constraint satisfaction by replaying the committed ids through
its own copy of the automaton; it never trusts the plugin's verdict. It keeps every record in the denominator. It
reports Wilson intervals and exact McNemar on paired records. It stops early on coded conditions. Calibration and the
test-cut runs are separated so that thresholds are pre-registered. Everything is testable on the CPU against a fake
server.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Cuts.**
   - BFCL test cut = exactly the 130 ids of `exp_e5_grammar130_map` (fixture).
   - BFCL dev cut = live_simple records 130–229 (n = 100).
   - Sudoku test = `generate(250, seed=0, n_blanks=6)`.
   - Sudoku dev = `generate(100, seed=1, n_blanks=6)`.
   - Test and dev are disjoint (asserted by id and by puzzle).
   - The Sudoku test puzzles are the JAX ones: re-scoring the preserved texts against the regenerated puzzles
     reproduces every recorded `ok` flag (250/250).
2. **Prompts** are byte-identical to diffgemma-fa `build_prompt` (stock style) and sudoku `build_prompt` (fixture
   strings). Each is sent as one user message with `chat_template_kwargs={"enable_thinking": false}`, `max_tokens` 256,
   and none of temperature, seed or `response_format`.
3. **Scorer parity.** The vendored scorer applied to the preserved JAX emissions reproduces the published figures
   exactly (independent oracle):
   - BFCL unconstrained: arg acc 0.639, schema valid 0.631, exact call 0.500
   - `exp_e5_grammar130_map`: 0.663, 1.000, 0.531, with `arg_total` = 291
   - Sudoku unconstrained: solved 216/250; `j0map`: 0/250
4. **Arms.** `unconstrained` sends no `astra_cd_*` key. `end_projection`, `perstep_mf`, `perstep_mar` and
   `perstep_mar_sc` produce the T-0064 `CdRequest` with the knobs recorded in every row.
5. **CS by host replay.** For each constrained record, the committed ids from the `ASTRA_CD` records (joined on
   `record_tag`, which the harness sets per request; ordered by `canvas_idx`) are replayed through the harness's
   automaton. The join check compares the `answer_region` of the detokenised committed ids with the `answer_region`
   of `reasoning + content` as served (`--reasoning-parser gemma4` moves the header into `reasoning` and special
   tokens are dropped from `content`, so a whole-text comparison can never match). A mismatch makes the row
   `join_error`, which counts as failure. Replay and the plugin's `in_final` must
   agree; disagreement is hard stop (a).
6. **Denominators (tests.md, RESULTS [B]/[B2]).** Every record of a cut stays in `n`, and for BFCL in
   `arg_total = 291`, whatever the outcome: error, timeout, `join_error`, `TooLarge`, loud CD error. A fake that fails
   one third of the records proves it.
7. **Statistics.**
   - Wilson 95 % intervals on every rate.
   - Exact McNemar on paired per-record outcomes (BFCL exact call; Sudoku solved) for the pre-registered pairs: Mar vs
     end_projection, Mar vs unconstrained, end_projection vs unconstrained, Mar vs Mf, Mar+SC vs Mar. Bonferroni over
     10 (5 pairs × 2 tasks).
   - The "both parsed" restriction is reported alongside.
   - Values are checked against hand computation.
8. **Hard stops**, coded. Each stop writes a partial artifact marked `stopped: <reason>` and exits non-zero. Each has a
   fake-server test in which it fires, **and** a companion row in which a benign run does not fire it.
   - (a) any replay-rejected terminated constrained output, or replay/plugin disagreement
   - (b) any loud CD error of class Z0, empty or infeasible in a constrained arm
   - (c) any `join_error` in the first 20 records
   - (d) unconstrained BFCL schema valid < 0.40 after 30 records, or Sudoku solved < 0.50 after 30 (stack broken;
     JAX was 0.631 and 0.864)
     - BFCL now counts the **parse** rate (parsed < 0.40 after 30), not schema valid — T-0078, human decision 2026-10-07.
   - (e) a constrained arm hits the step cap on > 50 % of canvases after 20 records
   - (f) **reported, not a stop:** the fraction of the first 20 unconstrained records that open with a
     header-language prefix is written to the artifact and the report. (diffgemma-fa's preserved unconstrained runs
     have 0/130 BFCL and 0/250 Sudoku texts starting with `<|channel>`; a stop here would fire every time.)
   - (g) wall clock over the budget (default 4 h)
   - (h) a dirty tree or commit mismatch at start: refuse to begin
9. **Artifacts.** `artifacts/cd/<ts>-<task>-<cut>-<arm>.jsonl`: first line is the regime (commit, versions, arm knobs,
   cut, n, thinking off, `max_tokens`, calibration sha), then one row per record (id, text, committed ids, parse,
   scores, CS, steps per canvas, wall ms, bucket, error, and for `end_projection` the shadow-argmax fields). The report
   generator builds its markdown tables **from artifacts only**.
10. **Calibration driver.**
    - Grid: `entropy_bound ∈ {0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0}` × `confidence_threshold ∈ {5e-5, 5e-4, 5e-3}` for
      `perstep_mar` on the dev cuts (SPEC §3.4).
    - Selection rule: maximise exact call (BFCL) or solved (Sudoku); ties go to fewer mean steps, then to the larger
      `entropy_bound`.
    - Writes a frozen `calibration.json` with its sha. The test-cut runner **refuses** `perstep_mar`/`perstep_mar_sc`
      without it.
11. **B13 statistics** (`local_astra/bench/b13_cd.py`): paired interleaved A/B per-step times with the A/A′ control.
    Returns `not_resolvable` when the control ≥ |delta|; never `pass` in that case (the B16 pattern).
12. *Negative:* the runner refuses any non-loopback base URL; it never imports torch.

## Implementation notes
- **T-0062 decision 11 (human, 2026-09-29):** constrained arms compile BFCL with `BFCL_BASELINE_OPTIONS` but
  `whitespace` = standard JSON (RFC 8259). Add a pretty-whitespace parity arm restricted to records with |S| ≤ 512
  under pretty whitespace, reported separately and labelled as the JAX link. The regime line records the whitespace.
- Running the harness against the real engine is GPU time. `scripts/gpu/cd_eval.py` lives under `scripts/gpu/` so the
  guard gates it (T-0016).
- The paper evaluates exact match. We keep diffgemma-fa's metrics for comparability. Both RESULTS lessons are rules:
  never read a solve rate without the parse rate, and never shrink a denominator.
- `--reasoning-parser gemma4` splits the header into `reasoning`. Score the answer region (`answer_region`) of the
  detokenised committed ids, as diffgemma-fa does, not the `content` field alone.
- `max_tokens` 256 matches the JAX single-canvas regime: R = 0 after canvas 0, so the grammar completes in canvas 0.
  Multi-canvas behaviour is proved in T-0068 and T-0069, not here.

## Rulings (orchestrator, 2026-09-30, after the test-author's report)
- E1. The API the tests fix is the one in `tests/cd/_eval_support.py`'s docstring (cuts.load/score — scoring lives in
  `cuts.py`; prompts.build_prompt; runner.RunConfig/run with `whitespace` and `max_states`; stats.wilson/mcnemar_exact/
  paired/PREREGISTERED_PAIRS/N_COMPARISONS=10; report.build; calibration.grid/select/freeze/calibrate; b13_cd.evaluate).
- E2. Scoring normalisation: every arm is scored with `ci_enums` and `expand_wildcard` **on** (the grammar's own
  language, so an output the grammar accepts is not scored invalid for enum case). The published-parity figures
  (unconstrained 0.639/0.631/0.500) are reproduced with both **off**, as the preserved artifacts were, and the report
  prints the unconstrained arm under both normalisations, labelled.
- E3. The join rebuilds `<|channel>` + reasoning + `<channel|>` + content before taking `answer_region`, so records
  with a named header join (criterion 5's literal wording cannot).
- E4. Sudoku dev ids are cut-qualified; test ids stay `sudoku4_0…249` to join the preserved rows.
- E5. The header rate (criterion 8(f)) is measured from committed token ids (`return_token_ids`); the real field name
  in v0.29.0 is pinned by the gpu contract row and T-0069.
- E6. Loud CD error codes for hard stop (b) are `z0`, `empty_delta`, `infeasible` (T-0067 emits exactly these);
  `bucket_too_large` and other T-0064 refusals are loud but do not stop the run.
- E7. "Frozen" calibration is tamper-evident: the runner refuses a calibration file whose sha differs from the one
  recorded at freeze. B13's control is |A′ − A|.
- E8. Whether the preserved map texts are accepted under standard JSON whitespace is verified by the implementer
  (compile + replay on the rows the test uses), not assumed.

- E9. (review, 2026-09-30) **Record flush.** T-0067 now flushes a request's records when it is released (T-0067
  S11), so the final canvas's record reaches the log on request finish. The harness still guards against an
  incomplete join: after `record_wait_s`, if records are incomplete it sends up to 2 tiny unconstrained flush
  requests (no `astra_cd_*`, `max_tokens` 1) and waits again before declaring `join_error`; the same at the end of a
  run. Flush requests are never written as rows and never count in any denominator. The fake models both behaviours
  (records emitted at the next request and at release).
- E10. **B13 verdict is a confidence statement, not a point estimate.** `pass` only if the upper bound of a 95 %
  interval on the overhead delta (paired bootstrap or sign test on paired ratios) is ≤ the threshold, with n ≥ 20
  pairs; otherwise `fail` (lower bound > threshold) or `not_resolvable`. The A/A′ control stays as a drift check:
  `not_resolvable` whenever control ≥ |delta|. (Supersedes E7's control-only rule; reviewer simulation: +12 % true
  overhead vs 10 % threshold passed 17.6–28.9 % of the time under the old rule.)
- E11. A frozen calibration file must name `task`, `cut == "dev"` and the arm; the runner refuses one that doesn't.
  A stopped artifact's rates are marked "stopped" in the table row itself, not only in a note.

## Implementation notes (implementer, 2026-09-30)
- Layout: `cuts.py` (cuts + scoring), `prompts.py`, `arms.py`, `records.py` (engine-log tail, join on
  `record_tag`, completeness = contiguous `canvas_idx` ending on a stop/error or on `max_tokens`),
  `replay.py`, `hardstop.py` (pure), `stats.py`, `report.py`, `calibration.py`, `runner.py`;
  `local_astra/bench/b13_cd.py` (median of paired ratios; control = |median(A'/A) - 1|).
- Sudoku dev ids are `dev:sudoku4_<k>` (E4). BFCL data defaults to `tests/fixtures/cd/bfcl` (override
  `ASTRA_CD_BFCL_DIR`), both files sha256-checked; the test cut's 130 ids are pinned by a sha256.
- Frozen calibration: the file carries `content_sha256` over its canonical content; `load_frozen` refuses
  a file edited after freezing (E7). The regime records the whole file's sha256.
- Unconstrained rows score the detokenised `token_ids` (E5) or, when absent, the served text rebuilt per E3;
  BFCL unconstrained rows also carry `scores_published` (both normalisations off, E2) for the report.
- Unconstrained rows have `cs: null` (no compile for that arm).
- E8 (verified 2026-09-30, CPU, outlines-core 0.2.14): the preserved `exp_e5_grammar130_map` texts of the
  first 10 test-cut records, replayed as `encode(text) + [EOS]`, are all accepted by their automata compiled
  with `whitespace="json"` (|S| 55-86). The two rows `test_bfcl_constrained_arms_use_standard_json_whitespace`
  uses are among them.
- E9: `RunConfig.flush_wait_s` (default 2 s) after each of at most 2 flush requests (`runner.MAX_FLUSHES`);
  per-row `flush_requests`, trailer total. `records.join` flags duplicated `canvas_idx` (`duplicate_idx`) and
  gaps (`contiguous`); either is a `join_error` before any text comparison.
- E10: `b13_cd` uses the sign-test interval on the median paired overhead (exact binomial order statistics,
  no RNG); `pass` needs n >= 20 and upper bound <= threshold, `fail` needs lower bound > threshold. Check
  (CPU, seed 0, 500 trials, n = 30, 3 % multiplicative noise): a true +12 % overhead against a 10 % threshold
  passed 0/500.
- E11: `calibration.load_frozen` refuses a file without `task` (or of another task), with `cut != "dev"`, or
  without `arm` in (`perstep_mar`, `perstep_mar_sc`). `freeze` writes what it is given; `calibrate` writes all
  three. A stopped artifact's table row carries `STOPPED (<letter>)`, `n of N (stopped)` and `(stopped)` on
  every rate cell.
- Test defects found in the first pass (fixed by the test-author in e7a9b16): (1) `test_build_prompt_is_byte_identical_to_diffgemma_fa[sudoku-dev]`
  asserts dev ids equal `sudoku4_<k>`, contradicting E4 and `test_test_and_dev_are_disjoint_by_id[sudoku]`;
  the prompts themselves match. (2) `test_pretty_parity_arm_runs_only_records_at_most_512_states` maps prompts
  to ids with a dict, but three prompt pairs of the BFCL test cut are identical (32-9-0/33-10-0, 36-13-0/37-14-0,
  58-27-0/59-28-0), so three sent ids are invisible to it; the harness sends exactly the 116 records and
  excludes exactly the 14 listed.
