# T-0090 — `astra.tool.call` on the HUD wire, and an action card that shows what would have been done

**Status:** done 2026-10-11 (verified at 6d4bfac; rulings U1–U10) · **Blast radius:** medium (one reviewer) · **Branch:** `T-0090-tool-card` from `main` · **Depends on:**
nothing (concurrent with T-0087, T-0088 and T-0089). Do not dispatch while T-0047 groups A/B (`hud_server.py`, `protocol.py`, panels) are
in flight. Umbrella `T-0080d.md`.

## Goal
One server→client message type, `astra.tool.call`, carries the lifecycle of a proposed action. The HUD renders it as a card in the shown
area. In v1 a card shows what the assistant **would** do (dry run), and it says so on the card.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **The message.** `contracts/protocol.py::AstraToolCall` has:
   - `type`, `turn_id`, `call_id`, `name`, `arguments: dict`;
   - `status`: one of `proposed | repairing | dry_run | executed | refused | cancelled`;
   - `source`: `draft | repair`;
   - `action: str | None`, `reason: str | None`, `query_problem: str | None`;
   - `replay: bool = False`.
   It is in `SERVER_TO_CLIENT` only, and the codec decodes it totally.
2. **The JS mirror.** `hud/src/protocol.js` mirrors the field table and decodes it strictly (the T-0043 pattern). A byte-identical corpus
   row covers server and page.
3. **The journal** (T-0037). It holds the latest message per `call_id` of the open turn and replays it on `astra.hello` after the spoken
   lines and before the played reports. It is cleared on a new turn. It is never dropped on outbox overflow (only `astra.turn.state` may
   be dropped).
4. **The card.**
   - It shows one line per tool:
     - "Would click at (x, y) on screen [frame]";
     - "Would type "…" at (x, y)" (plus "and press Enter");
     - "Would scroll down";
     - "Would press Ctrl+L";
     - "Would search for: …".
   - It shows the status chip, the source, and a warning when `query_problem` is set.
   - It shows "dry run — nothing was done" whenever the status is `dry_run`.
   - Model text is rendered with `textContent` only, never `innerHTML`.
   - A later message for the same `call_id` updates the card in wire order (`proposed → repairing → dry_run|refused|cancelled`).
5. *Negative.*
   - No link, fetch or `window.open`: the page makes no request for a search query (H2). The zero non-loopback test and the CSP stay
     green.
   - Existing panes and their order are unchanged.
   - The page's message handling for every other type is unchanged.

## Rulings (orchestrator, 2026-10-11, on the test-author's questions)
- **U1. Click line.** x and y are the call's arguments (the 0–999 grid); the message carries no pixels. The line starts "Would click at
  (x, y)" and contains "on screen"; "[frame]" was a placeholder and may be omitted.
- **U2. Dry-run notice.** Shown if and only if the status is `dry_run`.
- **U3. Replay order.** Tool calls replay after the spoken lines, the surface and any INTERRUPTED message, and before the played reports.
- **U4. CSP.** The repo has no CSP, so "the CSP stays green" in criterion 5 does not apply. Loopback-only is covered by the Chrome request
  log row.
- **U5. JS API.** `TOOL_CALL_TYPE`, `createToolCallPanel(root) -> { handle(message) }`, the `data-tool-*` hooks and a `[data-tool-calls]`
  container inside `.pane-shown` and outside `#shown-channel`, as pinned by the tests.
- **U6.** `replay` keeps pydantic's default bool coercion on the server, as the other booleans do; the page stays strict.

## Rulings after review (orchestrator, 2026-10-11, review of c6e870e)
- **U7. Late tool calls never reopen a turn.**
  - **The problem.** Today a tool call whose `turn_id` differs from the journal's clears the journal. A late `cancelled` for turn t1,
    arriving after t2 has started, would therefore silently wipe t2's transcript and spoken lines (`truncated` stays false). That is the
    T-0037 criterion 6 failure.
  - **Server rule.** A tool call opens a turn only if its `turn_id` has never been seen. One for a superseded turn is published live but
    neither recorded nor allowed to clear anything. The journal remembers the last 8 superseded turn ids, which is enough.
  - **Page rule.** It mirrors the server:
    - the first tool call of a never-seen turn replaces the shown cards;
    - a message for a turn whose cards were already replaced is ignored;
    - a message for a `call_id` that is already shown updates it in place.
  - **Out of scope.** The same race for a late `astra.spoken.played` predates T-0090 and is recorded as a follow-up.
- **Review rows (test-author).**
  - **Notice cleared on cancel.** dry_run → cancelled hides the notice (U2 is "if and only if").
  - **The 16-call bound.** With 17 distinct `call_id`s, 16 are kept, the oldest is gone and `truncated` is set. A repeat `call_id` at the
    cap evicts nothing.
  - **New-turn clearing, both ways, on the page.** A new turn clears the cards; a late message for a superseded turn neither clears nor
    adds.
  - **U7 on the server.** The reviewer's sequence keeps t2's transcript and lines, with `truncated` false.
- **U8 (re-review at cde0200). "Superseded" means superseded by a newer turn.** U7's "never been seen" wording is replaced. A departure
  clear (T-0037 criterion 4, last page gone) does not supersede the turn it forgets; it remembers it as the turn that was left.
  - **What supersedes a turn.** The next turn to open supersedes both the open turn and the turn that was left.
  - **Departure, then the same turn.** A tool call for that turn after a departure clear is recorded and replayed, so a reconnecting page
    sees the `cancelled`.
  - **Departure, then the next turn.** A late tool call for the old turn after the next turn has opened is ignored, and the new turn stays
    whole.
  - **The page** has no departure, so it is unchanged. Its memory of 8 superseded turns is pinned like the server's.
  - **Rows (test-author):**
    - departure clear, then a same-turn tool call: it is recorded and replayed;
    - departure clear, then the next turn opens, then a late old-turn tool call: it is ignored and the new turn stays whole, with
      `truncated` false;
    - the page remembers more than one superseded turn (kills `SUPERSEDED_TURNS = 1` on the page).
- **U9 (re-review at 9710a7d). A message for a superseded turn supersedes nothing.**
  - **The problem.** A late non-tool message for t1 (for example `spoken.played` after a barge-in) arrives while t2 is open. Today it adds
    the open t2 to `superseded`, so t2's next tool call is silently dropped from the journal. Before T-0090 that call was kept.
  - **The rule.** A message whose `turn_id` is already superseded never adds a turn to `superseded`. The reopening of t1 by such a
    message stays the deferred `spoken.played` follow-up.
  - **Row (test-author).** t1 opens, then t2 opens, then a late `spoken.played` t1 arrives, then a t2 tool call is the first t2 message.
    The t2 card must be journalled and replayed, and t2 must not be in `superseded`.
- **U10 (final re-review at 90dcef9). U7–U9 are replaced by one rule, defined by first-seen order.**
  - **Why.** Three rounds of patches (U7, U8, U9) each opened a new interleaving. At 90dcef9: a stale t1 message, then t3 opens, then a
    late t2 call wipes t3. So the rule is restated as a pure function rather than patched again (CLAUDE.md: rewrite instead of stacking
    fixes).
  - **The rule.**
    - The journal keeps the turn ids in the order they were first seen (bounded: the newest plus the 8 before it).
    - The *current* turn is the newest first-seen id.
    - A turn is *superseded* iff it has been seen and is not current.
    - A tool call for a superseded turn is published live, but it is neither recorded nor allowed to clear anything.
    - A tool call for the current turn is recorded, and reopens the journal if needed. This covers the turn left by a departure.
    - Departure clears and stale non-tool messages never change the order. The reopening of an old turn by a stale non-tool message
      stays the deferred `spoken.played` follow-up.
  - **The page.** Same rule, over the turn ids of the tool calls it receives.
  - **Rows (test-author).**
    - The reviewer's sequences A and B: t3 whole and `truncated` false.
    - Existing rows that read private fields (for example `superseded`) are restated behaviourally, by what is replayed.
    - A Hypothesis property over random interleavings of turn opens, stale non-tool messages, departures and tool calls, checked
      against a reference model of this rule. The property is that the tool calls replayed are exactly the latest per `call_id` of the
      current turn, and that no superseded-turn call ever removes a current-turn message. Exclude what the deferred follow-up owns:
      lines wiped by a stale non-tool message.
  - **U10 scope note.** A tool call for a superseded turn is never recorded, even when a stale non-tool message has just reopened the
    journal on that turn (counterexample 1 of the U10 property). The deferred follow-up owns only the lines a stale message wipes.
  - **Implement U10 as the rule itself.** Keep a bounded first-seen order and decide by "seen and not newest". Do not stack conditions on
    `left` and `superseded`: the patched prototype failed the property twice.
- **Accepted limits (re-review at ce3d8a8, APPROVE).**
  - **Very late calls.** What a tool call more than 8 turns late does is undefined: it counts as never-seen. T-0089's lifecycle never
    produces one, because a cancel follows its own turn's end.
  - **Follow-up.** A stale non-tool message (`spoken.played` after a barge-in) still reopens the journal on its old turn and can wipe the
    current turn's lines. This predates T-0090 and is recorded as a follow-up.
