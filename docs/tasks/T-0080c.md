**Status:** done (2026-10-09; stages 1–2 at 0dc9134, reviews A/B, H-T80c cleared) · **Blast radius:** high (two reviewers per `/launch`; human at H-T80 before and H-T80c after) ·

## Goal
Measure on the engine whether the constrained pass mostly adjusts the model's draft cheaply, and choose with a rule fixed before any
request: the thought variant and grammar scope (dev stage), then the pass-2 policy (test stage).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Stage 1 (dev split, n = 40 tool + 8 no-tool, one boot).**
   - **Cells:** thought ∈ {T1, T2, T4} × scope ∈ {union, named} under `perstep_mar` at T-0079's knobs (eb 1.0 / ct 0.005), plus `draft_only`.
     *Amended by K-D2 (`docs/tasks/T-0080b.md`): pass-2 scope is `named` only, so stage 1 runs T1/T2/T4 × named + `draft_only`; the report states this.*
   - **Selection:** among cells with schema_valid = 1.000 and exact ≥ best − 0.10 (dev is small; exact Fraction arithmetic), the lowest
     median pass-2 wall; within 5 % of that median, fewer mean executed steps; then the order T4, T2, T1 (cheapest prompt first).
   - **Output:** the selection is written to `selection-dev.json` with its sha and committed as a ruling in this file before stage 2.
2. **Stage 2 (test split, n = 80 + 12, one boot, once).** Arms at the stage-1 (thought, scope):
   - `cd_always:end_projection`;
   - `cd_always:perstep_mar` and `cd_always:perstep_mar_qargmax` at their T-0079 frozen knobs;
   - derived `draft_only` and `cd_on_invalid:<each>`;
   - and, only if T-0081's pre-registered reading said "worth wiring", `cd_always:perstep_mar+seed` at T-0081's chosen (noise, start step).
3. **Pre-registered selection (stage 2).**
   - **Eligible:** policies with schema_valid = 1.000 (`draft_only` is eligible only if its own rate is 1.000) and CS = 1.000 on every constrained row.
   - **Accuracy filter:** keep those with exact ≥ best eligible − 0.05.
   - **Choice:** the lowest end-to-end p50 wall (pass 1 + pass 2 where sent); within 5 %, the lower p90; then fewer mean executed steps; then the order
     `cd_on_invalid` < `cd_always`, and `end_projection` < `perstep_mar` < `perstep_mar_qargmax`.
   - **Recording:** the choice and the full table are written to `docs/cd/toolcall-v1.md`.
4. **Guarantee.** Every constrained terminated row has CS = 1.000 by host replay and no loud error. A violation stops the run and becomes a failing test first
   (`/triage`).
5. **Prefix cache.** For T2 and T4 the median pass-2 prefix-hit fraction is reported. If it is < 0.8 of prompt tokens, the report flags it as a finding (the
   product premise "same prefix, warm cache" did not hold) and the human decides at H-T80c. Not a stop.
6. **Report** `docs/cd/toolcall-v1.md`, from artifacts only:
   - every number with its regime;
   - per tool and policy: exact, schema_valid, CS, wall p50/p90 (pass 2 and end to end), steps, bucket, prefix hit;
   - route recall and false-tag rate;
   - T1's `101` position histogram;
   - the stage-1 table and ruling;
   - McNemar against `draft_only`;
   - a plain sentence on the human's expectation ("the constrained pass mostly adjusts the draft"): the share of rows where pass-2's call equals the draft's call
     (when the draft was valid) and the repair rate on invalid drafts.
7. *Negative:* no threshold in `local_astra/bench/thresholds.py` changes; the test split is run once; a stopped arm is not re-run without
   the human; the BFCL/Sudoku cuts are untouched.

## Implementation notes
- GPU estimate (n = 1-free guesses, not measurements): pass 1 with thinking ≈ 0.5–3 s per case; pass 2 ≈ 0.25–0.7 s per call at b64–b256 at the
  fast knobs (BFCL dev, thinking off: `perstep_mar` eb 1.0 / ct 0.005 median 375 ms vs unconstrained 137 ms; b64 255, b128 337, b256 677
  ms; n 100, 067ee04). T1 adds whole re-thought canvases at CD cost. Stage 1 ≈ 15–30 min and stage 2 ≈ 15–30 min, plus boots.
- The knobs were calibrated with thinking off on BFCL. Their transfer to thinking-on tool calls is what this launch tests, not an
  assumption it relies on.
- Launch from a pinned detached worktree at the reviewed SHA (T-0071b C10). Each stage uses a per-SHA run log.
