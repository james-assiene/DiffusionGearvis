## Goal

Today, clause (a) of `asks_user` makes any `?` that is immediately followed by an alphanumeric character a non-question. That keeps `3?4` and `pass?word` false. It also lets `Shall I pay?Then I close the tab.` through, and the model has produced this glued shape in real output: "…Mona Lisa?Leonardo da Vinci…" (t0098c-r4-plain-04-C3).

Narrow clause (a): a `?` is a non-question only if the character right after it is a lowercase letter or a digit (`ch.islower() or ch.isdigit()`). Clause (b) and everything else stay as they are.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Definition.** `asks_user(text)` is true iff some `?` (U+003F or U+FF1F) satisfies both of these:
   - (a′) the character right after it, if any, is neither lowercase nor a digit;
   - (b) after skipping every following non-alphanumeric character, either the end of the text is reached or the first alphanumeric is not lowercase.

   The function stays linear, pure and stdlib-only.
2. **New true forms:** `Shall I pay?Then I close the tab.`, `Shall I pay?Ok`, `…Mona Lisa?Leonardo da Vinci painted it.`, `Pay？Then`.
3. **Still false:** `3?4`, `pass?word`, `Is it 3?4`, `I will click the Forgot password? link.`, `I will click the 'Forgot password?' link.`, `what? no idea.`, `?x`.
