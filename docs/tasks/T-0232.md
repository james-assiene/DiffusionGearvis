**Rulings (orchestrator, 2026-11-02, from the test-author's report):**
- **R-1 (amends criterion 1).** The real leak is a paraphrase: "Use no markdown, no lists, and numbers as words when short." against "You are speaking aloud: short sentences, no markdown, no lists, numbers as words when short." Matching is therefore on word sequences, not whole sentences:
  - lowercase, keep only `[a-z0-9_]+` words, and drop the filler words `{use, and, also, please, always, then, so, you, are, i}` on both sides;
  - a candidate answer sentence is an instruction leak when its remaining word sequence (at least 6 words) is a contiguous run of one instruction sentence's sequence;
  - it is also a leak when it contains a whole instruction sentence's sequence (at least 6 words) contiguously. This is the reverse direction: self-talk quoting the instructions.
- **R-2.** The list covers only `prompt_builder`'s constants (criterion 4). The persona and screen-reminder sentences in `session.py` are out of scope, so the persona and screen-reminder rows are dropped by name.
- **R-3.** `USER_INSTRUCTIONS_HEADER` is part of the user's block and is not in the list.
- **R-4.** A sentence after a T-0224 repeat latch reports `"repeat"` (the latch wins).
- **R-5.** The list is `speech_gate.INSTRUCTION_SENTENCES`. `tests/unit/_frozen_speech_gate_t0232_base.py` (criterion 3's oracle) joins the owned files.

- **R-6.** `tests/unit/test_t0224_speech_repeat_and_maths.py::test_a_turn_without_a_repeat_voices_exactly_what_main_voices` is flaky on main. Its `assume` filters repeats by 15 letters, while the rule counts 20 characters including spaces; the counterexample is `"Yes. Aaaa a a a aa aaaa a. Aaaa a a a aa aaaa a. "`. The test-author fixes the filter to the rule's own measure. The module joins the owned files.

- **R-7 (review at 90f18e38).** The forward run needs at least **9** remaining words (R-7b: 8 would still refuse the natural "The latest screenshot shows the page still loading.", an exact 8-word run of the wait guidance; at 9 the corpus still gives the same 18 refusals); the reverse direction keeps 6. This cuts natural false positives such as "If it does not, try again." and "The latest screenshot shows the page still loading." The logged corpus's 18 genuine leaks must all still be refused: the shortest forward leak, the 2026-11-02 one, has 9 words. Under R-7b an 8-word verbatim guidance clause such as "Scroll to see the rest of the page." is voiced. The test-author adds the reviewer's three rows: branch order, refused sentences are not voiced keys, and the reverse floor.

- **R-8.** `tests/unit/test_speech_gate_special_tokens.py::test_text_without_a_special_literal_gives_mains_output` has the same flaky filter as R-6. Its `_repeat_key` strips less trailing punctuation than the gate's `_TRAILING_PUNCT_RE`. The test-author aligns it with the gate and adds the counterexample. This is a named confirmed re-pin.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An answer sentence whose normalised form (T-0224's normalisation: casefold, whitespace collapsed, trailing punctuation dropped) equals, or is contained word-bounded in, a sentence of the fixed system instructions is refused with reason `"instructions"` and never voiced. Only sentences of at least 20 characters count. The fixed system instructions are the persona, speaking and tool guidance sentences that `prompt_builder` puts in every system message (v1–v4 guidance and the screen reminder included; the user's own instructions block excluded).
2. This uses the existing `speech.refused` record, with `reasons` index-matched. History keeps the committed source as today (T-0224 R-5). The refusal does not latch the rest of the turn.
3. Negative: a turn whose answers contain no instruction sentence voices exactly what main voices. This is a property over the existing speech-gate corpora, plus the T-0224 replay of 2804 logged sessions: report how many sentences the new rule refuses, and hand-check each.
4. The sentence list is derived from `prompt_builder` constants at import (no copy), and a test pins that it includes "You are speaking aloud: short sentences, no markdown, no lists, numbers as words when short."
