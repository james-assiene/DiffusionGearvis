## Goal

Confirmed in source at 333159f3:
- `Session._on_decision` (`session.py:3396`) builds `E.UserSpeechOnset(t_ms=now, onset_ms=decision[1])`; `decision[1]` is a word's `start_ms` on the STT-tap **audio** clock (capture ADC position from 0 at capture open; `stt_client.py:424`; `contracts/events.py:33` "audio time of the onset").
- The machine emits `C.SnapshotFrame(at_ms=ev.onset_ms)` (`turn_machine.py` 524, 557, 587).
- `Session.snapshot_frame(at_ms)` passes it unchanged to `_shared_frame(at_ms)` (live + `shared`) and to `perception.get_frame(config.source, at_ms)` (non-live). `BrowserFrameSource.get_frame` (`browser_source.py:140`) returns the newest frame with `Frame.ts_ms ≤ at_ms`, and `ts_ms` is the **session-clock** receive time.
- In James's session capture opened ~10.8 s after the session; the onset (audio 21040 ≈ session 31.9 s) arrived at session 32452; the share was at 29154 and the first frame ~30190 → no frame ≤ 21040 → blind (`astra.vision.frame {"source":"shared","blind":true}`). Later in a session the lookup would return a frame ~11 s stale.
- Typed/ui turns (`_request_turn`, 8185) stamp `onset_ms` on the session clock, which is why the 16:08 typed turn could see. Scene-change and relisten snapshots are already session time; `_before_reset` is session vs session; `UserTakeover` has no producer; `perception.clip` has no caller.
- docs/02 names the defect (T-0032 ruling A13); it breaks `.claude/rules/conductor.md` "A duration is never computed from two clocks".
- The fakes cannot express the offset: `FakeAudioIO._cap_arm` starts the capture clock at `clock.now_ms` (the T-0051 B19 lesson); the fake changes in this task.

After this task the session converts a voice onset's audio time to session time with a mapping measured from the capture frames it already handles, only for the frame lookups of the snapshot that onset produced. Machine, contracts and every other snapshot unchanged. (The machine's `ev.t_ms` was rejected: it is onset + recogniser lag and would admit frames received after the user spoke.)

## Acceptance criteria

Definitions: **audio clock** — the clock of `SttFrame.t_ms`, `SttWordEvent.start_ms`, voice `onset_ms`; **session clock** — `session.clock.now_ms`, the clock of `Frame.ts_ms` and the reset floor; **voice onset** — a `UserSpeechOnset` built in `_on_decision` (estimated ones included), not `_request_turn`'s; **the mapping** — the audio→session estimate from `(frame audio time, now_ms)` pairs in `_pump_capture`; **the onset frame** — the frame(s) `snapshot_frame` fetches for the snapshot produced by stepping a voice onset.

1. **Regression (live, shared).** Tools live, vision `shared`, capture origin 0 armed at session 11 400 (audio 11 400 ms behind), HUD frames at 1 Hz from 3 s before the onset's session time, user speaks at audio 21 040 (session 32 440): the turn's first request carries F_k, the newest frame with `ts_ms ≤ 32 440`; not blind; `turn.frames == [F_k.phash]`.
2. **Regression (non-live HUD path).** Same shape with tools off: the prompt's image is F_k.
3. **Never a later frame (negative, adversarial).** With recogniser lag 500 ms, a frame received 200 ms after the onset's session time is not used (both paths); the newest before the onset is. Kills "use the delivery stamp `t_ms`".
4. **Never an earlier frame than needed (negative, adversarial).** A frame received 200 ms before the onset's session time is used over an older one. Kills "raw audio `onset_ms`".
5. **Both offset directions.** With audio ahead by 5 000 000 ms (`OffsetAudio`), 1/3/4 hold; `test_session_listening.py::test_onset_and_end_of_turn_carry_audio_times_not_clock_times` unchanged.
6. **Typed/ui unchanged (negative).** With a non-zero offset, a typed onset frame is looked up at the typed message's own session time, as at BASE; kills "map every onset".
7. **Other snapshots unchanged (negative).** Scene-change and relisten snapshots look up at the machine's `at_ms`; kills "map every `SnapshotFrame`".
8. **Reset floor respected (negative).** A frame at or before the floor is never the onset frame; a mapped onset before the floor gives a blind turn.
9. **No mapping yet.** A voice onset before any capture frame is handled: lookup at the delivery stamp `t_ms`, one `note` `{"what": "onset.clock", …, "method": "delivery"}`; raw `onset_ms` never compared with a session time.
10. **Bounded mapping.** Mapped onset ≤ the onset's delivery `t_ms` and ≥ 0; a capture-clock restart (frame `t_ms` drops) re-bases from the first frame after it.
11. **Observable.** Every voice onset frame lookup writes exactly one `note` `{"what": "onset.clock", "onset_ms", "at_session_ms", "method": "capture"|"delivery"}`; typed/ui onsets and other snapshots write none.
12. **Telemetry unchanged (negative).** `reflex.onset` span keeps the audio `onset_ms`; `stt.decision`, `UserSpeechOnset`, `MachineState.onset_ms` unchanged; the T-0181 mic fence still uses audio `onset_ms`.
13. **Scope (negative).** Byte-identical BASE `333159f3`..TIP: `local_astra/conductor/turn_machine.py`, `local_astra/contracts/`, `local_astra/perception/browser_source.py`, `local_astra/perception/assists.py`, `local_astra/conductor/stt_client.py`, `local_astra/conductor/audio_capture.py`, `local_astra/conductor/audio_io.py`, `hud/`. `assists.texts_for` gets the same argument as at BASE.
14. **Spec.** docs/02's A13 paragraph gains a T-0199 spec delta (mapping, fallback, bounds); the takeover arm stays open, naming T-0199b. (Orchestrator at merge.)

## Implementation notes

- Machine pure and untouched. Convert at the session, keyed on the provenance of the event whose step produced the command (a voice onset built in `_on_decision`, tracked by identity), never on the numeric `at_ms`, never on `turn.origin` alone.
- Mapping: from `(frame.t_ms + STT_BLOCK_MS, clock.now_ms)` pairs in `_pump_capture`; `now − frame_end` bounds the offset from above; take the minimum over a short recent window (tight; follows a restart). Errs late by the minimum capture-to-handling latency (tens of ms on hardware, exact in the fake). No `stt_client` private state. `RESET_KEEPS`.
- Map only the frame lookups; `_land_frame`'s `reflex.onset` span keeps the machine's `at_ms`.
- Live with vision `browser` (`_live_snapshot`) needs nothing.
- Landmines: the `RESET_*` completeness test; `test_hud_server_no_egress.py` scans `session.py`; `_pump_capture` stays cheap (record a pair, no per-frame logging); T-0195 B's `_mic_diagnose`/`_mic_gate` order before `stt.feed` unchanged; the census runs in a real git worktree.
- Do not touch `BrowserFrameSource`; its docstring's "compares against audio time" becomes wrong — recorded in the docs/02 delta.
- **T-0199b (out of scope, high, machine):** `_on_onset` in SPEAKING arms the takeover at `ev.onset_ms + takeover_ms` (`turn_machine.py:536`) but the PAUSED tick compares with session `ev.t_ms` (1113): with audio ~11 s behind, any barge-in interrupts within one tick; with audio ahead, the takeover never fires.
