## Goal

In James's desk session (`logs/sessions/20261027-230926-966483.jsonl`), two web turns ran out of steps.

- **t9** (seq 7230–8590), "use the browser to read the first 5 pages of the chapter on weebcentral.com": it executed 8 steps. Step 9's `type_at` was cancelled as `step_budget`, and its prefix was voiced anyway: "I will try to reload the page to ensure the content loads correctly."
- **t11** (seq 9939–10640) did the same: "I will click on the One Piece search result." was voiced, then step 9 was cancelled.

This is the rule as written: docs/02 §4 row R16 voices the report step's text even when that step's call is cancelled.

The planner's census over all `logs/**/*.jsonl` on this box (2,639 files) is a cross-check, not expected values:
- 22 turns ended `tool_loop_end == "budget"`, all at 8 executed. 21 of them voiced an announcement of the cancelled call (`announced_call.announces`), and 1 voiced nothing.
- 25 turns ended `refused` at step ≥ 2 on a malformed draft ("repair: step 1 only", "combo not in enum", "no call prefix", "recovered from a thought: never repaired", "model text after call"). 21 of them voiced an announcement. No deliberate refusal (ask-first, declined) is among them.

After this task:
1. A live session with web on gives the model 20 executed calls per turn. Every other tools-on mode keeps 8.
2. The step after the last allowed call is told that no actions remain, so the model reports what it did and where it stopped. That report is committed answer text and is voiced as usual (D16 moment 4).
3. When a step ≥ 2 ends with a call that will not run (past the budget, or a malformed draft), its text is not voiced. If nothing is left to say, one fixed clip says the assistant stopped before finishing.

## Implementation notes

- **D16 is pinned (T-0104).** Reports are "ordinary committed answer text", and no "text built from a call" is ever voiced. Hence the model's own report (B1) and a fixed clip as the fallback. A deterministic template naming the last action or host would contradict D16 (H-T178-1).
- **Budget arithmetic at 20.**
  - **Compaction.** `MAX_CONTINUATION` (14 000 chars) stays the trigger. Web action steps add about 250–300 chars each in the evidence log.
    - 20 actions come to about 5 000 chars.
    - 5 searches of about 1 500 chars each, plus 15 actions, come to about 11 250 chars.
    - So compaction stays rare. When it fires it keeps the last 2 responses, which hold the fact.
  - **Context.** About 9.2k tokens plus history, against a 14 336-token prompt limit.
  - **`--limit-mm-per-prompt` image 4** is unaffected: the screenshot is swapped, not added.
  - **Timeouts.** They are per step, and there is no per-turn cap. t9 averaged about 2.55 s per request, so a 20-call turn (21 requests) takes about 54 s.
  - **Latency budgets.** They gate step 1, which is unchanged. Turn duration is not budgeted.
- **The machine stays pure.** The clip is a command.
  - Check how `PlayClip` interacts with `playback.drained` and the T-0114 "terminal outside its step" filter: the turn must still reach IDLE.
  - A clip must never outlive its turn (T-0092a G6/G7).
  - If clips cannot play safely within the turn, stop and report. Do not invent a TTS path for conductor text.
- **REPORT_FACT placement.** Follow T-0134c's label and the T-0136/T-0137 facts. The fact sits outside any `<|tool_response>` payload and contains no page text.
- **The T-0172 re-ask** stays off at the report step (guard `step - 1 >= max_tool_steps`).
- **The repeat guard (T-0137)** counts refused steps against the budget, unchanged. T-0179 turns it on by default. The guard's own stop and this task's ending must not both voice a line.

## Open rulings (recommended default first)

- **H-T178-1 (James): what happens when the report step still calls a tool or only announces one.** Recommended: a fixed clip with no call-derived content, leaving D16 unchanged. The alternatives are a silent end with only the HUD card, or amending D16 to allow a conductor-built line such as "I ran out of steps; I stopped on <host> after N actions".
- **H-T178-2: does an explicit `max_tool_steps` in a profile apply in web mode?** Recommended: no. Web mode reads `max_tool_steps_web`, and the help says so.
- **H-T178-3: REPORT_FACT wording.** Recommended: "You have used all your actions for this request. Do not call a tool. In one or two sentences, tell the user what you did, what you found, and where you stopped."
- **H-T178-4: where the fact applies.** Recommended: every loop-on mode. Most budget ends in the census were on loopback pages.
- **H-T178-5: the clip texts.** Recommended: "I ran out of steps before I could finish." for a budget end, and "I could not finish that." for a malformed draft.
- **H-T178-6: the web budget.** Recommended: 20. The cua repo used 15, and 321 of its 350 episodes hit the cap. Revisit after T-0178c.
- **H-T178-7: step-1 dead announcements.** These are out of scope: 15 in the census, and fixing them costs step-1 latency. Recommended: handle them in a separate task.
- **H-T178-8: how much of the step text B3 withholds.** Recommended: the whole step text. In the census, 0 of 25 such steps held a real answer.
