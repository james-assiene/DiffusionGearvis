## Goal

Three request-level measurements that a session cannot isolate.
- **Q4 (H-T111a-10):** whether the host-rendered continuation (form G) behaves like the OpenAI `role: tool` body (form A) at step 2.
- **Q5:** whether vLLM accepts an image part in a continued assistant message. T-0111c needs this.
- **E:** whether eliding older tool responses with the product's `loop.compact` hurts answers, and what one compaction costs in cache misses and latency.

The E-rows' bars decide whether T-0120b is planned as is.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Harness** `scripts/gpu/t0115a_loop_requests.py`.
   - Same refusals as T-0115 criterion 2, where they apply.
   - Importing it opens no socket.
   - Form G requests go through `VllmClient`. Form A bodies are raw JSON over loopback httpx (`Message` has no `tool_calls`).
   - Concurrency 1, unseeded.
2. **Q4:** 6 step-2 cases (2 search, 2 click, 1 type, 1 press_key; T-0111a's set) × 2 arms × n = 5 = 60 requests. Report-only: the outcome shares and the start-sentence repetition rate (the step-2 text begins with step 1's first sentence, normalised), G against A.
3. **Q5:** 3 requests, run **last** in the harness, because a crash in the patched image path kills the EngineCore (`.claude/rules/gpu-services.md`). Report accepted, rejected or engine-down. An engine-down void stops the harness, and the earlier rows are kept.
4. **E-rows, the compaction A/B.**
   - **Histories:** 4 invented-fact questions. Each history is 5 maximal `google_search` steps built from harness literals with `loop.step_prompt` on one dev screen.
   - **Placements:** the fact is in step 1's response (**old**, elided by `compact` at its defaults) or in step 5's (**kept**).
   - **Arms:** FULL is `step_prompt(base, steps)`; COMPACT is `step_prompt(base, compact(steps, budget))`, with `budget` set so that steps 1–3 are elided. The budget is checked in a unit row.
   - **Requests:** 4 × 2 × 10 repeats × 2 arms = 160, plus 40 warm requests. Per history, the step-4 prompt is sent first; then the two arms in alternating order.
   - **Per request, recorded:** the outcome (correct = a final answer containing the fact; re-search = a `google_search` call; unsupported final = a final answer without the fact; other), prompt tokens, `/metrics` prefix hit and miss, first-commit ms and done ms.
5. **E bars (H-T115a-1), exact fractions:**
   - **E1, kept placement, non-inferiority:** COMPACT correct ≥ FULL correct − 4/40.
   - **E2, old placement, recovery:** COMPACT (correct + re-search) ≥ 32/40.
   - **E3, old placement, harm:** COMPACT unsupported final ≤ 8/40.
   - **Control EC:** FULL correct ≥ 28/40 at both placements. Otherwise the E rows are inconclusive (the model cannot use 5-step histories at all).
   - **Cost, report-only:**
     - median miss tokens per COMPACT request, computed as query − hit relative to the warm request, with the predicted value = tokens after the first elided block;
     - the paired median latency difference COMPACT − FULL, in first-commit and done ms;
     - ms per miss token.
   - **Outcome:** "elision viable" iff EC, E1, E2 and E3 hold; "elision hurts: <bars>"; or "inconclusive".
6. **Report and eval:** `report.json` and `report.md` with every k/n. `tests/eval/test_loop_requests.py` (gpu, `T0115A_RUN_DIR`) re-derives the outcomes from `requests.jsonl` and skips on gate-sha drift (`toolcall/loop.py`, `llm_client.py`).
7. **Write-up:** `docs/cd/toolcall-loop-requests.md` (new). Pre-registration before the launch; results after.
8. **Negative.** Zero diff under `local_astra/` and `services/`. No test-split read. No network. The fast suite gains ≤ 1 s serial, and mock-engine runs live in `tests/integration/`.

## Implementation notes

- **The warm request** makes the miss measurement a one-compaction cost. Without it, COMPACT and FULL are both cold, and the difference measures nothing.
- **Fact strings** are invented, with a time or number that has no parametric answer. Accept stated equivalents (for example 19:40 and 7:40 pm) by a pre-registered list.
- **Same guard-hook constraints as T-0108a**: bash may not name `scripts/gpu`; edit with the Edit tool and commit with `-a`.
- Implementer, 2026-10-17. These are the choices the spec left open. The pre-registration in `docs/cd/toolcall-loop-requests.md` has them verbatim.
  - **Q4 cases:** `search-01`, `search-02`, `click-01`, `click-03`, `type-02` and `key-01`, with the cases' reference calls. Each has a short step-1 text. The action responses are `action_payload` of the `DryRunToolExecutor` strings (`to_pixels` on 1280×800).
  - **Bases:** each base is a fresh-session step-1 prompt (`PromptBuilder`, no history, the case's screen, no assists or memory). E is asked over `scroll-01`'s screen.
  - **Q5:** 3 requests: `click-01`, `click-03` and `type-02`. Each sends that case's step-1 continuation text followed by an `image_url` part (the case's screen).
  - **Q5 readings:** "accepted" means 200 and the stream ended. "rejected" means anything else while `/health` answers. "engine-down" means `/health` fails after the request.
  - **E histories:** 4, built from literals: a fact result, 5 leads and a pool of 8. Snippets are padded round robin with filler sentences up to `MAX_RESPONSE`, so by construction a block ends within 6 chars of the bound (" More." no longer fits; unmeasured, the unit row checks within 100) and is never cut. `E_BUDGET = 7000` elides exactly {0, 1, 2}, and `main` refuses otherwise.
  - **Warms:** 5 per (history, placement), sent before repeats 0, 2, 4, 6 and 8. FULL goes first in even repeats.
  - **Form A:** `call_0` is the tool-call id. The raw stream is read by `llm_client._IdReader`, so G and A are classified by the same reader.
  - **Exit codes:** exit 1 on engine-down or after 10 consecutive errored Q4/E requests. When the run stops early, Q5 is not sent.
  - **Report-only extra:** `compact_miss_tokens_first_per_cell_median`, explained in the next item.
- **Open for the reviewer (implementer, 2026-10-17): the cost row as pinned is near-vacuous under prefix caching.**
  - Within a (history, placement), every request after the first warm, FULL and COMPACT is a byte-identical repeat. Only the first COMPACT per cell is expected to miss its tail. The pinned `compact_miss_tokens_median` (over all 80 COMPACTs) should therefore be about 0, and the paired latency median should be about 0 too.
  - The report adds the median over the first COMPACT per cell (n = 8) and the predicted value (the `/tokenize` count after the first stub).
  - A cost measured on every repeat would need a warm per repeat with per-repeat-distinct prompts (80 warms), which the 40-warm design and its tests exclude. This is a planner decision.
- Implementer, 2026-10-17, on R1–R4 and R6–R9 (tests at e61622f; orchestrator decisions (a)–(d)). The write-up is updated to match.
  - **R1:** `classify_e` and `classify_q4` read `thought_open` from the last commit (`_ends_in_thought`). The tool-commit rule is unchanged: a tool commit outside an open thought still decides first.
  - **R2, decision (a):** the accepted strings are the complete list in the `E_HISTORIES` literals. `holds` case-folds both texts and requires no letter or digit on either side. It derives no forms.
    - Added: `6:45` for `market` and `one thousand one hundred eighty-seven` for `hall`.
    - Bare `7:40` is not added for `ferry`, because it names the morning time.
  - **R3, decision (b):** `cost["by_placement"][p]` is built from `_first_per_cell`: per cell, the first COMPACT after the first warm, and the first pair after it (the lowest repeat with both arms). The pooled top-level keys stay as sanity values. The pooled first-per-cell median now also counts from after the warm, which matches the eval oracle.
    - The kept predicted miss is the `/tokenize` count after the common prefix of the kept COMPACT text and the same history's old COMPACT text, which is step 5's response. This is a char-level cut, so the token boundary at the divergence is approximate (±1 token).
  - **R4, decision (c):** an E line with an error or a status other than 200 reads `errored`, whatever its commits hold. `errored` is a fifth tally column inside the cell's n. `e_outcome` is inconclusive at `E_ERRORED_INCONCLUSIVE = 3` or more of the 160, and the reason names the count. When EC also fails, both reasons are joined. Q4 lines keep their class plus the separate `errored` k/n.
  - **R6:** form A's assistant `content` is `step.text`.
  - **R8:** Q5 probes `/health` up to `HEALTH_CHECKS = 3` times, `HEALTH_RETRY_S = 1` s apart. The first answer rules out engine-down. The line records `health_checks`.
  - **Decision (d):** the exit code is unchanged. Only a stop (engine-down, or 10 errors in a row) or an incomplete schedule exits 1.
  - **R5 and R7** are test-side only. The harness already stopped at engine-down, and `_q4_report` already had the pinned shape.
