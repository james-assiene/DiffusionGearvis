## Goal

James can click a **Stop** button in the HUD header while Gearvis is thinking, speaking or working in its browser, and Gearvis stops. Speech is cut and the queued audio flushed. Generation is aborted. A computer-use loop is ended: the call in flight is cancelled, keys and buttons are released, and no further step is requested. The turn returns to **IDLE**, so the next spoken or typed question works at once. History keeps only what was actually played, marked `[interrupted]`. Nothing new is spoken: no clip, no apology.

Confirmed in source at b7963e71. Nothing in production submits `E.UserTakeover` (`grep -rn "E.UserTakeover(" local_astra` finds nothing; `reflex/vad.py` raises `NotImplementedError("T-0007")`). The existing takeover rows cannot be reused for a button:
- In SPEAKING/PAUSED, `_on_takeover` goes to `_interrupt` and then `_relisten`, which opens a new turn in LISTENING with `PrefillOpen` (turn_machine.py 556-558, 643-661).
- In THINKING it reopens LISTENING with `keep_words=True` (547-555), or opens a new LISTENING turn after an executed call (R17, 561-585).
- A LISTENING turn with no speech has no exit row (no tick or timer leaves it). The page would show LISTENING forever, one vLLM sequence slot would stay held by the prefill, and `Session._request_turn` would refuse every typed question ("a turn is already running (LISTENING)", session.py 7974-7984).

So the machine gains one event and its rows (group A0). The wire and the session come next (group A), then the button (group B).

## Implementation notes

- **Why the machine changes.** No existing row ends a speaking or thinking turn in IDLE without committing or relistening (`UserTakeover` relistens, `client.disconnected` degrades, `session.reset` wipes, `mic.muted` acts only in LISTENING/ENDPOINTING). Do not have the session submit `UserTakeover` plus a synthetic `MicMuted`/`SessionReset`.
- **Purity.** The row compares `turn_id` in the machine; the session never decides whether a stop applies.
- **The stop mark** is an explicit per-turn field in `_PER_TURN_RESET`, excluded from dumps (T-0182 `still_working_played` precedent). Do not encode it in `pause_onset_ms`.
- **Reuse `_interrupt`** for SPEAKING/PAUSED, passing the cancel reason in. The INTERRUPTED exits branch on the mark: stopped → `_end_turn` with the history entry, otherwise `_relisten` unchanged.
- **The live loop.** `_close_loop` cancels every execution of the closing turn (`_cancel_execution`), `_close_calls` publishes `cancelled("turn closed")`, and the executor releases keys/buttons on `CancelledError`. A click or partial `type_at` already dispatched is not undone; docs/02 says so. On the SPEAKING path the turn closes at the INTERRUPTED exit, so mark the `_TurnLog` when the stop step acts and read the mark in `_close_loop` ahead of `closing.interrupted`.
- **No clip, no `commit_history`, no `schedule_summary`** after a stop (as a voice barge-in).
- **`response.cancel`** exists only in docs/02 as the v2 Realtime vocabulary; nothing implements it. Use `astra.turn.stop`; a future Realtime adapter can map `response.cancel` onto `UserStop` (one sentence in §5).
- **INTERRUPTED window.** Between a SPEAKING stop and IDLE (≤ `interrupt_timeout_ms`, or `playback.stopped`), a typed question is still refused as busy. Accepted; stated in docs/02.
- **HUD.** `hud/src/stop.js` mirrors `mic.js` (accepts `astra.turn.state` and `astra.session.cleared`; `view()`, `click()`), reads state from the message, applies the stale-turn rule. Button right after `#turn-chip`, label "Interrupt Gearvis" (H-T193-4). No `aria-keyshortcuts`, no key handler.
- **Never weaken** a §4 invariant or edit an existing row's expectation to fit; if a pin says "nothing submits a client-originated machine event other than X", move it and say why.
