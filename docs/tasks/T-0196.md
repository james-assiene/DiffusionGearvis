## Goal

In James's live session `logs/sessions/20261029-121653-2974784.jsonl` (turn t1, request `t1-gen-s7`, `llm.commit` seq 6757, session_ms 449670) the model committed `<|tool_call>call:click_at{x:458,y:38}<tool_call|><tool_call|>`: a valid call with its terminator doubled.

Confirmed in source:
1. `native.parse_call` (`toolcall/native.py:229-233`) refuses any tail after the first `<tool_call|>` other than whitespace, "text after call".
2. `live._judge` (`live.py:461-470`) finds a v1 name, no T-0100 refusal, no alphanumeric text after the call (T-0102 `_model_text_after_call` turns the second close into a space) → `repairable`, `arguments=None`.
3. `Session` (`session.py:5612-5614`, T-0114 criterion 8) refuses a repairable draft at step > 1 with `"repair: step 1 only"`; the turn ended and the task failed.

At step 1 the same text costs a needless CD repair; inside a recovered thought (T-0135) it is refused `THOUGHT_CALL_NO_REPAIR`. T-0135's census already caught this shape once (`...-210810-...`, S5).

After this task `live.classify` judges a call followed only by one or more extra `<tool_call|>` literals (and optional whitespace) exactly as the same call with a single close, **only when that verdict is `valid`**. Any other text keeps today's verdict field for field. History, `astra.spoken`, step prompts, `tools_v1.json`, payloads, CD grammars, `native.parse_call` and eval oracles do not change.

## Acceptance criteria

Definitions: **BASE** is `live.py` at 611068bb. **Doubled-close shape:** `classify`'s body (after the leading tag and the stop-text drop, as today) is `C + T` where `C` ends with the first `<tool_call|>` outside every `<|"|>` string, and `T` is non-empty, made only of `<tool_call|>` literals and whitespace, and contains at least one `<tool_call|>`. **Single-close text:** `OPEN + C`.

1. **Accepted shape.** For any doubled-close text whose single-close text BASE judges `valid`, `classify(text)` equals BASE's `classify(single-close text)` in every field (status `valid`, name, arguments, reason `None`, query_problem). Covers the incident text verbatim, 2–4 extra closes, whitespace before/between/after, all five v1 tools and `page_overview` under the T-0140 set.
2. **Everything else is BASE's verdict (negative).** Any text not of the shape, or whose single-close text BASE does not judge `valid`, gets BASE's `classify(text)` field for field: extra closes then `.`, `<channel|>`, `<turn|>`, a word or a second `<|tool_call>`; missing `}` plus doubled close; schema-invalid call plus doubled close (BASE's T-0100 refusal or `repairable`); single close with no brace; no close at all. A close is never invented.
3. **`repair_verdict` unchanged (negative)** for every text.
4. **Step ≥ 2.** A step-2 `VALID_CLICK + "<tool_call|>"` (fake LLM, fake live browser) executes with the single-close arguments, records `valid` / `parse: "ok"`, no `"repair: step 1 only"`, continues the loop with no `ToolRepairStart`, repair request or `tokenize`; the step-3 request body equals the single-close run's byte for byte.
5. **Step 1.** Same text at step 1: no repair, executes, records `valid`.
6. **No duplicate token reaches the model or the user (negative).** In 4 and 5, `<tool_call|><tool_call|>` and any `<tool_call|>` outside a rendered call appear in no history entry, `astra.spoken` text, step continuation or turn line; the continuation's call is `loop.render_call(name, arguments)`; only `tool.call.draft` keeps the raw text.
7. **Safety still wins (negative).** A doubled-close call with a declining transcript, or after a spoken prefix ending "?", is refused `USER_DECLINED_REASON` / `ASKED_FIRST_REASON`, not executed.
8. **Recovered thought calls** (T-0135 A5/A6 amended): an open thought ending in `VALID_CLICK + "<tool_call|>"`, recovery on, is `valid`, executed, `from_thought`. A recovered draft that is still `repairable` (another shape) is still refused `THOUGHT_CALL_NO_REPAIR`.
9. **Byte-identical over BASE..TIP (negative):** `toolcall/native.py`, `router.py`, `loop.py`, `tools_v1.json`, `tools_v1_overview.json`, `payloads/`, `reground.py`, `thought_call.py`; `conductor/session.py`, `llm_client.py`, `turn_machine.py`; `eval/`, `decoding/`, `contracts/`, `tests/fakes/`, `plugin/`, `services/`, `docs/cd/`. `native.parse_call` on the incident body is still `ParseFailure("text after call")`.
10. **Eval/CD parities unchanged:** `tests/cd/unit/test_toolcall_live_parity.py`, `test_toolcall_native_oracle.py`, `test_toolcall_native_grammar.py`, `test_toolcall_native_parse.py` green with no edits.
11. **Docs.** docs/02 §5's statuses paragraph gains one clause (doubled-close judged as its single-close call when that call is valid); `docs/tasks/T-0135.md` gains a dated amendment line (its doubled-close row is `valid` after T-0196; A2 replay S5 becomes 4 valid, 0 repairable). **Both docs edits are made by the orchestrator at merge** (docs/02 is concurrently edited by T-0194's implementer).
