## Goal

In turn t16 a click at s2 on photopea.com opened a popup after the settle had finished. At s4 the executor's `ensure()` adopted it, so `page_serial` moved and `type_at` was refused with `the active page changed; take a new frame` (`executor.py` 593-596, seq 15666). `_live_step_screen` (`session.py` 3553-3630) keeps the image after any `refused` step, and the model has no tool that takes a frame. So the step image stayed the old page's frame. Every later `click_at` read off it carries `frame_page` = the old serial and is refused again (`plan.frame_page != d.page_serial`; seq 15791, 16394). The model then concluded "I am in an image editor, not a browser" and gave up. Confirmed in source. Which page was adopted is unconfirmed: the log keeps no URL. s2's hosts (google.com, blog.photopea.com) are consistent with a late popup.

After this task, every refusal whose reason tells the model to take a new frame is followed by a fresh frame of the browser's active page at the next step. The rule mirrors T-0177 B2's shared-frame path. The model can then act on what is really on screen.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Page change.** After a `click_at`/`type_at`/`press_key`/`scroll` refused with the executor's page-changed reason:
   - step k+1 shows a fresh own frame of the active page, taken through B8's failed/timeout path (`_fresh_step_frame`, bounded by `LIVE_FRAME_TIMEOUT_MS`);
   - it replaces the previous image and is never shown beside it;
   - it is labelled `LABEL_NOW`, never `LABEL_UPDATED`, with no outcome clause (T-0134 A3; same treatment as T-0177 B2);
   - the refused step's `screenshot` payload value is the one T-0177 B2 merged with for `SHARED_FRAME_REASON`. The test-author pins it at activation from Merge T-0177-b.
2. **The set of reasons** is exactly the refusals that say "take a new frame", held in one named constant:
   - the executor's page-changed reason (made public as `PAGE_CHANGED_REASON`, same text);
   - `OVERVIEW_STALE` and `OVERVIEW_PAGE_CHANGED` (T-0151);
   - the session's `FRESHNESS_REASON` (T-0111g B9).
   - Narrowing to the page-changed reason alone is H-T187-1.
3. **The loop gets unstuck (reproduction).** With the `late_popup.html` fixture through a real Session and a scripted fake LLM: the call refused with the page-changed reason is followed, at the next step, by a `click_at` on the fresh frame that is `executed` on the popup. Before the fix, every later coordinate call is refused.
4. **No fresh frame.** When no fresh frame can be taken (timeout, failure, closed browser): the previous image stays and a note is written (B8's note); nothing else changes.
5. **Negative, too wide.** Every other refusal keeps today's image and label, byte for byte: `bad coordinates`, `bad arguments`, `frame not from this browser`, `no frame`; the guard, ask-first and decline (T-0107a/e); a T-0137 no-progress stop; the T-0172 refusals. A `google_search` refusal also keeps the image.
6. **Negative.** Unchanged: T-0137 counts only executed steps as repeat instances; T-0136 never measures a refused step; the executor's own refusal predicate (`execute()` 591-596) is not changed or weakened; non-live and `browser`-off sessions produce byte-identical turn lines and requests (differential row).
7. **Logging.** The session log gains one note per refresh: `{"what": "live frame: refreshed after a page-change refusal", "reason_kind": <constant name>, "tool_step": k}`. No URL appears in it.
