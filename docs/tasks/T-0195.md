## Acceptance criteria

### Group A

**A1. The rule** (amends T-0051 criterion 12 and rule (h); docs/02 §4). `Endpointer.observe(event, *, may_open_on_estimate: bool = False)`. A `SttWordEvent` with `end_estimated=True` produces `("onset", event.start_ms)` iff (i) the flag is `True`, (ii) no run is open, (iii) rule (f) admits it, (iv) rule (g) admits it. When it opens, the run's state is set exactly as for any first word (first = start_ms, last = end_ms, arrival via `_arrival_of`, EMA reset). An estimated word reaching an **open** run is still refused entirely. Words without `end_estimated` behave as on BASE.

**A2. Default is BASE bit for bit.** With the keyword omitted or `False`, `observe` equals BASE on every event sequence (differential against a verbatim frozen copy). With `True` on every call, output equals BASE on every sequence with no estimated word.

**A3. The session supplies the flag from the machine.** `_on_stt_event` passes `True` only when `self._state` is IDLE, or LISTENING with `words == ()` and `t_eot_ms is None`; `False` in every other state.

**A4. The pump re-checks** (queue race). The session remembers a `UserSpeechOnset` produced by an estimated word by identity (the T-0039 A14 pattern). When `_handle` reaches it, it checks `before` against A3's predicate; if it fails, `machine.step` is **not** called, one note `{"what": "stt.estimated_onset_dropped", "onset_ms", "machine_state"}` is written, nothing else changes. Other onsets are never affected.

**A5. A one-word question is answered** (CPU, fakes). Session IDLE; FakeStt scripts `open_word("Hello,", start_ms=S, end_ms=S)` released by the hold, steps every 80 ms, the marker echo. Endpointer decisions in order: `("onset", S)`, then `("end_of_turn", S, 1.0)` (backstop) at the first step with `t_ms - arrival >= EOT_SILENCE_MS`, arrival = `max(step clock at release, S)`; the head path must not decide it. Machine IDLE → LISTENING → ENDPOINTING → THINKING, `LlmStart.transcript == "Hello,"`; with FakeLlm answering, at least one `TtsSpeak` and the turn ends IDLE with history `("Hello,", <spoken>)`.

**A6. The barge-in guard holds (negative).** In SPEAKING, PAUSED, THINKING, ENDPOINTING, INTERRUPTED and LISTENING-with-words an estimated word produces no `stt.decision`, no `UserSpeechOnset`, no `PlaybackPause`, `StopClip`, `Unfreeze`, `CancelTimer` or new timer; machine state as BASE.

**A7. The race (adversarial).** Session reads IDLE but a typed turn's `UserSpeechOnset` is queued ahead of the estimated onset: the estimated onset is dropped at `_handle`; the typed turn reaches `LlmStart` with its typed transcript, with no second LISTENING entry and no `Unfreeze`.

**A8. Rules (f), (g) and the mute still win (negative).** Flag `True`: an estimated word with `start_ms <= last t_eot` opens nothing; one longer than `MAX_WORD_MS` opens nothing; one in a muted span or the A0-S fence is still dropped before `observe` (T-0181 A6/A0-S2 rows green and unedited).

**A9. Untouched (negative).** Byte-identical to BASE: `turn_machine.py`, `contracts/events.py`, `contracts/commands.py`, `contracts/speech.py`, `conductor/stt_client.py`, `reflex/level.py`. The Hypothesis invariants in `test_turn_invariants.py` and the endpointer properties in `test_endpointer.py` unchanged and green under 10 fresh seeds; `test_the_constants_this_task_must_not_move` and `test_an_estimated_end_word_never_opens_a_run` pass unedited.

**A10. Every estimated word reaching the endpointer is recorded** (closes T-0051 B39): one note `{"what": "stt.estimated_word", "start_ms", "end_ms", "machine_state", "opened": bool}`, no word text. Words dropped by the mute before `observe` get no note.

**A11. Spec delta.** docs/02 §4 gains "Spec delta (T-0195, group A)" stating A1, A3, A4, naming the two logs and ruling B35. T-0051's "never opens a run" statements stay; the paragraph says they still hold with the default. (Orchestrator at merge.)

### Group B

All rows are `kind: "note"` written through `Session._log`.

**B1. `mic.level`, periodic.** The capture pump writes one row when `frame.t_ms >= window_start + LEVEL_WINDOW_MS` (`LEVEL_WINDOW_MS = 10_000`, capture clock). Fields: `what: "mic.level"`, `reason: "period"`, `from_frame_t_ms`, `to_frame_t_ms`, `frames`, `muted_frames`, `zero_frames`, `nonfinite_frames`, `rms_dbfs` (over all samples of live non-zero finite frames), `peak_dbfs`, `max_frame_rms_dbfs`, `min_frame_rms_dbfs` (per 80 ms frame via `reflex.level.rms_dbfs`), `words_received`, `words_estimated`, `words_dropped_muted`, `steps_received`, `onsets`, `pause_head_min`, `pause_head_max` (index `PAUSE_HEAD_INDEX`, null if none), `stt` (full `stats()` or `null`). Counts since the previous row; dBFS finite and floored at `SILENCE_DBFS`; levels `null` when no frame qualifies.

**B2. `mic.level` at each mute switch:** a `reason: "gate"` row immediately before each `mic.gate` note, closing the window; `mic.gate` unchanged (T-0181 A10).

**B3. Muted audio is not measured (privacy).** Frames fed as zeros by the mute count toward `muted_frames` only; an all-muted window has `null` levels.

**B4. `mic.unmute_check`:** one row at the first frame with `t_ms >= unmute_frame_t_ms + 5000` (capture clock), or at a new mute with `cut_short: true`. Fields: `from_frame_t_ms`, `to_frame_t_ms`, `cut_short`, `words_received`, `words_estimated`, `steps_received`, `onsets`, `max_frame_rms_dbfs`, `stt`; counts from the unmute frame.

**B5. Sufficiency.** Four scripted scenarios through a session rig (FakeStt, fake capture frames) produce rows a documented reading tells apart: (a) healthy; (b) quiet mic (-60 dBFS frames, steps continue, no words, `max_frame_rms_dbfs` within ~15 dB of `min_frame_rms_dbfs`); (c) deaf recogniser (speech-level frames, steps continue, `words_received == 0`); (d) stalled recogniser (`steps_received == 0`, `stt.queued_blocks`/`dropped_blocks` growing).

**B6. Not model-facing (negative).** `(pcm bytes, t_ms)` passed to `stt.feed` identical to BASE, muted and unmuted; no `wire.out`, HUD message, prompt, history, `turns.jsonl` or TTS text differs; no row carries PCM or word text; `stt.decision` and `mic.gate` shapes unchanged.

**B7. Cost.** O(1) state per window; per-frame cost ≤ 50 µs median over 1000 frames of 1920 samples (in-process, like `test_listen_level_cost.py`); no new thread, task or lock; arithmetic in `_pump_capture`, never the device thread.

**B8. Docs.** One sentence in docs/02 §8 naming `mic.level`, `mic.unmute_check`, `stt.estimated_word`, `stt.estimated_onset_dropped` as diagnostics (orchestrator at merge).

## Implementation notes

- Rule (h) has two jobs: no accidental barge-in (depends on machine state, which the endpointer cannot see — so the session supplies it (A3) and re-checks where the machine steps (A4)), and no fabricated span extending a run (stays whole). The IDLE onset is safe: its end is the word's start, never invented (ruling B24). LISTENING-before-words is included because such a turn otherwise only ends via a later real word.
- The A3 read of `self._state` can be stale (STT pump task; `_release_mic_held` calls it inside `_handle`); A4 makes it exact.
- A dropped estimated onset leaves the endpointer's run open; it ends on the backstop, harmlessly. Do not add a reset.
- Known residual (ruling B35): a run's last word whose `EndWord` never comes lands in `next_words`; separate task.
- Group B: compute on `frame.samples` in `_pump_capture` **before** `_mic_gate`, only when the gate passes the frame live; `reflex.level.rms_dbfs` read-only; count STT events at `_on_stt_event` entry before the mute drop; `stats()` once per row; window boundaries compare `frame.t_ms` with `frame.t_ms` only.
- `stats()` fields to carry: `estimated_word_times`, `words_without_end`, `held_word_timeouts`, `stray_end_words`, `late_end_words`, `ready_seen`, `stale_markers`, `unknown_messages`, `dropped_blocks`, `dropped_events`, `reconnects`, `connects`, `close_failures`, `queued_blocks`, `word_delivery_lag_count/min_ms/median_ms/max_ms`.
- **Desk procedure** (James; clean tree; record build sha, profile, STT device, mic device and gain, headset vs speakers): (1) start with "Log this session" on, 20 s silent; (2) no mute, say "Hello." alone (expect a reply), then a sentence; (3) mute ≥ 60 s (better ~260 s), silent; (4) unmute, within 2 s say "Hello." then a sentence; read `mic.unmute_check` and the next `mic.level` (stay connected at least 10 s after speaking: no closing row is written at session stop, so the last partial window is not reported); (5) if no words, speak loudly close to the mic; optionally repeat 3–4 with a headset mic. Reading: quiet mic → speech `max_frame_rms_dbfs` within ~15 dB of room in steps 2 and 4; stall after zeros → step 2 healthy, after step 4 `steps_received` ≪ ~125/10 s or `queued_blocks`/`dropped_blocks` grow; deaf after zeros → speech-level frames, normal steps, `words_received == 0`; neither → step 4 matches step 2.
- **Zero-frame mute as the cause:** plausible, unproven. If the repro shows a stall, recommend seeded comfort noise at ~-70 dBFS (second choice: reconnect the STT stream after a long mute); reject feeding real frames and dropping words (the recogniser would hear muted speech). The mute design does not change here.
- **GPU repro plan** (proposed as T-0181 C3/C4, not owned here; via `/launch`): arms exact zeros / seeded noise -70 dBFS (seed 0) / recorded room tone; gaps 3, 60, 300 s; per trial a fixture "Hello." plus a sentence, the gap, the same again; measure steps per 80 ms, WER after vs before, first-word lag, `held_word_timeouts`, whether "Hello." gets an `EndWord`; n = 5 per cell.

## Open questions (defaults taken)

1. Measure level from muted frames: **no** (privacy).

3. LISTENING-before-words admits an estimated onset: **yes**.
