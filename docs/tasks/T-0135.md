## Goal

In the T-0132 A/B at 2ccff41 (300 turns, regime below), **29 turns ended in silence because the model wrote its next tool call inside an unclosed thought.** They split S3 18, S1 7 and S5 4, which is 18 of S3's 20 failures. This re-derivation corrects T-0134 D-5's "15 (S3 11, S1 4)".

In each of the 29, the step's request:
- kept no `tool` commit;
- has a final `llm.commit` with segment `thought` and `thought_open: true`;
- has thought text that ends, after trailing whitespace, in a complete native call: `…<|tool_call>call:type_at{press_enter:true,text:<|"|>ada@example.com<|"|>,x:305,y:448}<tool_call|>` (`sessions/20261022-204803-1975518.jsonl`, `t1-gen-s2`).

The model omitted `<channel|>`. The id-mode split rightly reads "a tag inside a thought is thought" (docs/02 §5, T-0088 delta). No call reached `_judge_tool`, no answer was committed, and the loop ended with `final`. None of the 29 turns succeeded.

**Planner's census over the run** (CPU, deterministic). It covers the 860 `generate`/`tool_step` requests. The 316 ReGround pass-2 requests and the 2 `tool_repair` requests are excluded.
- 564 kept an answer-channel `tool` commit, and none of them ended in an open thought.
- 264 ended without a call in a closed thought.
- 3 ended in an open thought with no call tag: two "the the the…" collapses, and one answer left inside the thought.
- **29 ended in an open thought holding a tagged call.**
  - In all 29 the last `<|tool_call>` of the thought is followed only by a call and `<tool_call|>`. No other request has a call tag inside a thought.
  - `live.classify` on the tail gives `valid` for 28. The 29th (`…-210810-…`, S5) is `repairable`, "text after call", because of a doubled `<tool_call|><tool_call|>`.

**This task:**
- adds a pure recovery rule;
- wires it into the live judge stage behind a keyword that defaults to off;
- proves on CPU that the rule fires exactly on those requests;
- defines a 2-arm GPU A/B that measures what recovery does to the turns that follow.

A recovered call:
- is judged by the same `live.classify` and by the same T-0107e decline and T-0107a asked-first rules;
- is never repaired;
- executes through the same path as any other call;
- never puts a word of the thought into speech, history, the HUD or a continuation.

**Q-4 (from T-0134), checked against the logs and not real on S3.**
- `contact.html`'s submit handler runs on implicit submission (Enter in the name or email input). It refuses an incomplete form ("Please fill in all three fields.") and never sets `__state.sent`. In the textarea, Enter adds a newline that `trim()` removes.
- All 20 S3 failures end with `sent: null`, so none submitted early. 18 of them are the silent endings above.
- Executed S3 `type_at` calls with `press_enter: true` belong to successful turns 100 of 123 times; with `false`, 20 of 30.
- Two things remain:
  - an error line visible after an early Enter, unmeasured as a distraction;
  - the real-site risk that implicit submission sends a real form.

  The second belongs to the executor and safety line (T-0111), not here. Recovered calls execute their arguments verbatim, and nothing rewrites `press_enter`.

**Remedies compared** (details under Implementation notes):
- **R1. Recover the trailing tagged call (chosen).** Deterministic, no extra request, zero added latency. It fires only on requests that otherwise end the turn silent, and in the logs every such turn failed.
- **R2. Re-request the step with the thought closed** (a `continue_final_message` assistant turn holding the thought plus `<channel|>`). Rejected for v1:
  - it costs another generate per occurrence;
  - its result is non-deterministic and may write a different call or reopen the thought;
  - it sends thinking back to the engine;
  - Gemma's template handling of thoughts in a continued assistant message is unmeasured.

  It stays the candidate for the 3 call-less open-thought endings.
- **R3. The seeded constrained-decoding fallback.** Rejected:
  - the calls are already well formed; the defect is channel structure, which needs a per-step mask on `<|tool_call>` while inside a thought;
  - it is plugin work on the GPU path and is out of scope here.
- **R4. A prompt instruction.** Rejected:
  - the system prompt already says "close it … never leave the answer inside the thinking channel";
  - prompt-only fixes failed before (T-0107, T-0131);
  - `LIVE_TOOL_GUIDANCE` is frozen.

## Acceptance criteria

### Group A1 — the rule and the replay (CPU)

**A1. The pure rule: `local_astra/toolcall/thought_call.py`.**
- `recover(thought: str) -> str | None`. It is stdlib-only, linear in `len(thought)`, and never raises.
- `thought` is the text of the request's final open thought block: the `thought` commit texts of that block, concatenated in order.
- It returns `thought[k:]`, where `k` is the index of the **last** `<|tool_call>`, **iff** `thought[k:].rstrip()` ends with `<tool_call|>`. In every other case it returns `None`, including when there is no tag, or when there is any non-whitespace text after the final `<tool_call|>`.
- It does not parse or validate. Judging is `live.classify`'s job (A6).

**A2. `scripts/replay_thought_calls.py <run-dir> [--json]`** (CPU, read-only, as `replay_ui_gate.py`, T-0127).
- It reads `trials.jsonl` and `sessions/*.jsonl`.
- For every `generate`/`tool_step` request that:
  - kept no `tool` commit, and
  - whose last non-tool commit is a `thought` commit with `thought_open: true`,

  it applies `thought_call.recover` and then `live.classify`. It prints one line per firing (task, repeat, session file, request id, step, verdict status, name, arguments, reason), and totals by task × status.
- It also prints, informationally, the count of ReGround pass-2 responses of the same shape (10 in T-0132), and states that these are out of scope.
- Exit codes: 0 on success; 2 for an unreadable run directory, a missing file, or malformed JSON, naming the file.
- **Thresholds on `logs/t0132-crop-budget-ab-2ccff41/`** (exact, deterministic):
  - 860 eligible requests are scanned;
  - 29 fire (S3 18, S1 7, S5 4);
  - 28 are `valid` and 1 is `repairable` (`20261022-210810-1975518.jsonl`, `t1-gen-s2`);
  - 0 fire on the other 831;
  - every firing is the last request of a turn whose `success` is false and whose `tool_loop_end` is `final`.

**A3. Negative (A1).** `toolcall/live.py`, `native.py`, `router.py`, `conductor/llm_client.py`, `contracts/**` and `tests/fakes/**` are byte-identical over the group's closed range.

### Group A2 — the live wiring (CPU)

**A4. Keyword, off by default.**
- `Session(..., recover_thought_calls: bool | None = None)`.
- `None` and `False` behave exactly as BASE.
- `True` with a tool kit whose mode is not `live` raises `ValueError` naming `live` (`.claude/rules/conductor.md`: no silent no-op).
- No default changes in `cli` or `config` (T-0135b).

**A5. When recovery fires.** With `recover_thought_calls=True`, all of these must hold at `llm.done` of a live `generate` or `tool_step` request:
- (a) the request is the turn's live generate and was not aborted;
- (b) the request kept no `tool` commit;
- (c) its final non-tool commit is a `thought` commit with `thought_open=True`;
- (d) `recover(<the final open thought block>)` returns text.

The session then judges a `tool` commit with that text and `stop_id=None` through `_judge_tool`'s existing path, with:
- the step's real `spoken_prefix`;
- `step_text` equal to the answer channel with thoughts removed, as today;
- `source="draft"`.

Recovery never fires on ReGround pass 2, `tool_repair`, prefill or captioner requests.

**A6. Judging.**
- The verdict is `live.classify(text)`.
- The T-0107e decline and the T-0107a asked-first rules apply first, unchanged.
- A recovered `repairable` verdict is refused at every step, with a new reason constant `THOUGHT_CALL_NO_REPAIR`. No `tool_repair` request is sent (docs/02 §4 P5′).
- A `valid` recovered call follows exactly the answer-channel call's path, ReGround included when it is on: proposal, execution, the step k+1 request, the loop budget.
- Every `tool.call` record of a recovered call, and only of such a call, carries `from_thought: true`.

**A7. Never spoken, never stored** (CLAUDE.md; docs/02 §4 H4, P6′, L5, L6). No character of the thought block reaches any of the following; the call itself appears only in `render_call` form, as for any executed call:
- a `TtsSpeak`;
- history or an `Episode`;
- a HUD message;
- `machine.events()`;
- the step k+1 request body (its continuation embeds `step_text` plus the rendered call and response).

The thought commits stay in the session log verbatim, as now.

**A8. Logging.**
- When `recover_thought_calls` is given (`False` or `True`), every live turn line gains `recover_thought_calls: <bool>`. With `None` no key is added (the T-0132 A4c pattern).
- No new span and no new log kind.

**A9. Negative.**
- With `recover_thought_calls=None`, these are byte-identical to BASE in every mode: request bodies, turn lines, session-log records and HUD messages.
- With `False`, the only difference is A8's key.
- On a request that kept an answer-channel `tool` commit, everything is unchanged even with `True`, including when its thought also quotes a tagged call.
- The id-mode split is unchanged: a tag inside a thought is still thought, and thought commits are still yielded and logged as before.

## Implementation notes

- **Where the rule lives, and why not in the wire reader.**
  - The id-mode split ("a tag inside a thought is thought", docs/02 §5, T-0088) is a wire contract every tools-on mode relies on, and the fake mirrors it. Keep it.
  - Recovery is a live-stage policy, applied in `session.py` at `llm.done` from the commits the session already holds.
  - Do not add a field to `Commit` or `ToolSource`. The synthetic `tool` commit uses `stop_id=None` (allowed for a length cut), and nothing downstream reads `stop_id` except the repair, which never runs here.
- **"The final open thought block."** Take the trailing run of `thought` commits after the request's last non-thought commit (or from the start of the request), provided the last one has `thought_open=True`.
  - A tag in an earlier, closed thought must not be stitched to a later open one; `test_recover_table` and `test_answer_channel_call_unchanged` cover this.
  - `thought_open` is per canvas: it is also set on answer commits of a canvas that ends inside a thought. Check the segment, not only the flag.
- **The judging path is the existing one.** Call `_judge_tool` with the synthetic commit; do not build a parallel path.
  - The only new branches are: the repairable → `THOUGHT_CALL_NO_REPAIR` mapping (after decline and ask-first, before T-0114's "repair: step 1 only"), and the `from_thought` record key.
