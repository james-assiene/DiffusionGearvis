# T-0014 — KV cache budget: bf16 KV at 4 GiB, max_model_len 16384

**Status:** superseded by T-0048 on 2026-09-16 (human decision). Its owed criterion-6 bench is re-run there at the new KV pool, and `services/vllm.sh` passes to T-0048 with it. Do not dispatch this task; a finding about the engine's memory budget belongs to T-0048. Original status line: in progress · **Blast radius:** medium · **Depends on:** none (unblocks every GPU task)

## Goal
`services/versions.lock` `[diffusion] kv_cache_memory_bytes` goes from `"2GiB"` to
`4294967296`, and `services/vllm.sh` pins `--kv-cache-dtype bfloat16` with
`--max-model-len 16384`. After this task the engine is R2-compliant (bf16 KV, never FP8) *and*
has a context long enough for the real turn shape, which the 2 GiB budget in `docs/01-design.md`
§9 could not deliver simultaneously. This is the lock change CLAUDE.md requires a dedicated task
for.

## Why (measured, not argued)
The first successful boot (commit `e53b972`, `logs/vllm-first-boot.log`, 2026-09-13) came up with
`kv_cache_dtype=fp8_e4m3`. Nobody asked for it: the checkpoint's
`quantization_config.kv_cache_scheme` declares `num_bits: 8`, so vLLM auto-selects fp8 whenever
`--kv-cache-dtype` is `auto`. The checkpoint ships no k/v scaling factors, so it ran at scale 1.0
(`kv_cache.py:147` warning: "may cause accuracy drop"). That violates
`.claude/rules/gpu-services.md` and `docs/01` R2.

Forcing bf16 doubles KV bytes per token. 25 of this model's 30 layers are sliding-window capped at
1024, and their cost is a **constant** independent of `max_model_len` — only the 5 full-attention
layers scale. So lowering the context buys far less than proportional relief, and at 2 GiB the bf16
ceiling is **8,720 tokens** whatever else is done.

Measured with vLLM's own allocator (`check_enough_kv_cache_memory` / `get_kv_cache_capacity`) against
the real `config.json`, config construction only, no weights loaded — max concurrency by (KV pool ×
`max_model_len`), all at `kv_cache_dtype=bfloat16`:

| KV pool | 4096 | 8192 | 12288 | 16384 | 32768 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 GiB | 2.13x | 1.06x | fail (max len 8720) | fail | fail |
| 3 GiB | 3.19x | 1.60x | 1.35x | 1.26x | fail (max len 32464) |
| 4 GiB | 4.25x | 2.13x | 1.79x | **1.68x (27,465 tok)** | 1.33x |
| 5 GiB | 5.32x | 2.66x | 2.24x | 2.10x | 1.66x |

**Caveat on that table (added after the boot).** Trust the *pool* axis, not the `max_model_len`
axis. The pool axis is exact and linear (3/4/5 GiB → 1.26/1.68/2.10x). The length axis is not
physical: the 32768 cell says 1.33x, but 2 GiB at fp8 and 4 GiB at bf16 are byte-identical
capacities and the fp8 boot logged **1.68x** there. The real 4 GiB @ 16384 boot measured
**1.93x / 31,605 tokens** against a modelled 1.68x. The conclusion "4 GiB is required, 2 GiB
cannot work" survives — that rests on the pool axis — but no point value from the table may be
quoted. Note also that the token figure *fell* from 54,931 (@32768) to 31,605 (@16384) on
identical bytes: `GPU KV cache size` is `int(concurrency * max_model_len)`, a derived number, and
sizing anything from it is the error that produced the retracted claim in 62f6946.

8,720 tokens is not enough: pixel-first alone is a 1120-token bucket per image and the prompt
allows 4 images (`--limit-mm-per-prompt {"image": 4}`) = 4,480 tokens, before history, thinking or
the 256-token canvas. 4 GiB @ 16384 gives 1.68x headroom with VRAM ~26.7 GiB — inside the ≤ 29 GiB
boot assertion (`docs/02` §7, B7) with ~5 GiB free, above the ≥ 3 GiB the unprofiled sampler
transient needs.

Rejected alternatives: 3 GiB @ 8192 (1.60x, but 8k is tight once history accumulates); keeping fp8
(works at 32768/1.68x today, but is the R2 violation this task exists to remove); 2 GiB @ 4096
(2.13x, cannot hold 4 images plus history).

## Acceptance criteria
1. `services/versions.lock` `[diffusion] kv_cache_memory_bytes = 4294967296` with a comment giving
   the provenance (this task id and the measured table above). The value is spelled in **bytes**,
   not `"4GiB"`: `vllm/utils/argparse_utils.py:29` accepts a single-letter suffix only, and the case
   carries the base (`4g` = 4e9, `4G` = 2^32). `"2GiB"` is what killed the first boot attempt.
2. `services/vllm.sh` passes `--kv-cache-dtype bfloat16`, `--max-model-len 16384` and
   `--kv-cache-memory-bytes 4294967296`, and its comment block states the *correct* measured
   numbers — explicitly superseding the wrong "2 GiB holds 27,465 tokens at 1.68x" claim committed
   in `62f6946`.
3. `.claude/rules/gpu-services.md` records `--kv-cache-memory-bytes 4294967296` (not `2GiB`) and
   states that `--kv-cache-dtype bfloat16` is **required, not a default**, because the checkpoint's
   `kv_cache_scheme` forces fp8 under `auto`. Without this, the next agent re-derives the broken
   command from the rule file.
4. A boot at these settings logs `kv_cache_dtype=bfloat16` in the `EngineCore ... config:` line,
   and does **not** log `cache.py:335` ("Using ... data type to store kv cache"),
   `kv_cache.py:130` or `kv_cache.py:147` — all three are gated on a quantized KV dtype, so their
   absence is the proof bf16 took effect rather than being silently ignored.
5. `GPU KV cache size` is ~27,000 tokens with max concurrency ≥ 1.5x, and total VRAM ≤ 29 GiB.
6. Benches B1/B3/B7 run at the new regime and their numbers are recorded in `PROGRESS.md` under
   *Runs* with the full regime line. **This is the bench run CLAUDE.md requires for a lock change.**
   Until it exists, this task is not done and no latency number may be quoted.
