## Goal

The tool-call deadline R18 must count 15 s from when the call actually started. `PipeWireAudioIO` stamps `playback.stopped` and `playback.drained` with the device's rendered stream position (`audio_io.py:455-460`, `t_ms=int(device.rendered_ms())`). That position does not advance during silence, so it falls further behind the session clock with every idle or thinking second; `Session._pump_playback` (`session.py:2752`) passes the stamp straight to the machine. The machine then arms R18's `tool_result` deadline from it (`turn_machine.py:775`) and uses it as `SnapshotFrame.at_ms` after an interrupt (`turn_machine.py:924`); the session also records it as the `playback.stopped` span (`session.py:2224`).

Evidence: in W2-2 (`logs/t0160-web-smoke-abcf17d/turns/W2-2/sessions/20261027-160309-468608.jsonl`) the drain carried `t_ms` 4760 at `session_ms` 22420, so the deadline was 19760, already past; the next Tick (seq 720) closed the turn with `hud.error "tool timeout"` 23 ms after `tool.execute` started. Across all 10 trials the drain times were 3.2–4.8 s against session times of 6.2–22.4 s. Voice sessions with a speaker are hit the same way. Every other event already uses the session clock (STT/VAD delivery, LLM, TTS, tool results, Tick, HUD, `SceneChanged`, the synthesised drain in `_maybe_mark_drain`). No test caught it because `tests/fakes/audio.py` `FakeAudioIO` stamps playback events with the session clock (lines 177-178, 373-374).

The fix: the session restamps playback terminals with `clock.now_ms` when they arrive, so every event the machine steps carries session-clock time (new invariant P10). The machine stays pure and unchanged; `played_ms` and `played_text` keep their device-derived meaning.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Restamp (P10, part 1).** For `PlaybackStopped` and `PlaybackDrained` received from `audio.playback_events()`, the event passed to `machine.step` has `t_ms` equal to the session clock's `now_ms` when `_pump_playback` received it; its `type`, `played_ms` and `played_text` equal the module's. Run attribution and stale-terminal dropping (T-0092a criteria 1 and 3) behave exactly as before.
2. **W2-2 regression (P10, R18).** With the real `PipeWireAudioIO` over a device whose rendered position lags the session clock by ≥ 17 s, a typed tools-on turn whose first canvas arrives ≥ 15 s after `llm.start`, plays and drains a spoken prefix, then runs a valid tool call that the executor answers 2 s (session clock) after `tool.execute`: no `hud.error "tool timeout"`; the `ToolResult` is accepted (R12: `LlmContinue` for step 2); the turn does not reach IDLE between `tool.execute` and the result; the `ArmTimer("tool_result")` in that `machine.step` record has `at_ms == session_ms + 15_000` within one Tick.
3. **A real R18 timeout still fires on session time.** Machine level: a `PlaybackDrained` with `t_ms = T` completing a SPEAKING turn with a pending valid call and the loop on; `Tick(T + 14_999)` emits no `hud.error`; `Tick(T + 15_000)` emits `hud.error "tool timeout"` and closes the turn, as R18 specifies. Session level, under the same device lag: the session's execute deadline (`TOOL_EXEC_TIMEOUT_MS` = 10 000) still submits `ToolResult(status="timeout")` at `tool.execute` + 10 000 session ms, and R18 does not fire first.
4. **Interrupt snapshot.** Under the same lag, a barge-in that reaches INTERRUPTED and then `playback.stopped` emits `SnapshotFrame(at_ms=a)` with `a` the session time at which the terminal arrived.
5. **Span.** `turns.jsonl`'s `playback.stopped` span is on the session clock, so `playback.stopped` ≥ `playback.first_sample` for a turn that spoke under device lag.
6. **One-clock invariant (P10, new).** In the regression scenario of criterion 2, every `machine.step` session-log record satisfies `0 ≤ session_ms − event.t_ms ≤ 1000`, except `SceneChanged` (bounded only by `t_ms ≤ session_ms`). Today the W2-2 shape violates this by 17 660 ms.
7. **Hypothesis property (added, nothing weakened).** `test_turn_invariants.py` gains: for a non-decreasing sequence of event `t_ms`, every `ArmTimer` named `tool_result`, `tool_repair`, `llm_timeout`, `interrupt_timeout` or `resume` has `at_ms` ≥ the largest `t_ms` stepped so far (`takeover` excluded: armed from `onset_ms`, the ADC clock, A13). All existing properties stay byte-identical.
8. **Eval case from W2-2.** (a) Replaying the fixture's logged `machine.step` events through `TurnMachine` with the session's live policy reproduces `hud.error "tool timeout"` at the seq-720 tick; (b) with each non-Tick event's `t_ms` replaced by its record's `session_ms`, no tool timeout at that tick, and the `tool_result` deadline is the execute step's `session_ms + 15_000`.

- Rejected: arming deadlines from the last Tick in the machine (new `MachineState` field, every arm site changes meaning); fixing it in `audio_io.py` (needs a session clock through `cli.py`, and its contract tests pin device-domain behaviour).
- Do not touch `turn_machine.py`; do not weaken any §4 property. `played_ms` is device-derived on purpose (T-0006 delta 3).
- Out of scope, recorded: A13 (`onset_ms`/`t_eot_ms` on the ADC clock; the PAUSED takeover armed at `onset_ms + takeover_ms`, `turn_machine.py:511`); the ADC clock advances through silence, so its offset is a constant gap, not a growing lag.
- The pump awaits nothing new (`now_ms` is synchronous; 20 ms loop stall rule).
