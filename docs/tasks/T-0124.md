## Goal

With tools off (`kit=None`, the default), the speech gate cuts the voiced `text` at the first trim literal and deletes the drop literals (T-0088a criterion 3, A2/A3). Trim literals: `<eos>`, `<turn|>`, `<|tool_call>`, `<|tool_response>`, `<|think|>`, `<end_of_turn>`, `<|endoftext|>`, `<|channel>`; drop literals: every other `SPECIAL_TOKENS` entry. Under conservation A1 each sentence's `source` keeps every byte, and `played_text` is built from `source`, so history, `astra.spoken`, `astra.spoken.played` and `turns.jsonl` hold literals and post-stop text that were never voiced.

Worst case: `Sure, <|chan<eos>nel>thought SECRET-THOUGHT<channel|>ok. `. `<eos>` splices a channel open that T-0121's cleaner does not see (it deletes no other literal); the gate cuts the voice at `<eos>`, but `thought SECRET-THOUGHT` reaches history and the HUD, with no note.

After this task, the tools-off path applies the gate's own literal rule to the text before the machine sees it, in a second cleaner stage chained after T-0121's `_ThoughtCleaner`, so the gate never meets a literal: the voice is unchanged; every played `source` is literal-free and post-stop-free; history equals the join of what was played; one `note` counts what was dropped. Tools on is already clean (`_AnswerCleaner` deletes every literal, I4) and does not change. Streams with no special literal are byte-identical to `8b3f6d0`, except a commit ending inside a tail that could still grow into a literal (criterion 10's allowance).

## Acceptance criteria

**The rule.** **L(s)** repeats until a round changes nothing: cut `s` at the earliest occurrence of any gate trim literal (`speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS`, `<|channel>` included); then delete every gate drop literal (`speech_gate._DROP_TOKENS`). Nothing is inserted. **U(raw)** = L(T(raw)), where T(raw) is T-0121's tools-off thought cleaning of the concatenated raw `thought_open=False` answer texts of one stream. Thought stripping runs first; a literal inside a closed thought block goes with the block; a channel open that a literal deletion splices is cut, as the gate cuts it.

1. **Each literal, tools off.** For each `lit` in `<eos>`, `<turn|>`, `<|tool_call>`, `<|tool_response>`, `<|think|>`, `<end_of_turn>`, `<|endoftext|>`, one commit `answer(f"Press {lit} now. ")` gives exactly `Press ` in: the join of every `TtsSpeak.source`, the audio plays' sources, `played_text`, `history[-1].assistant_spoken`, `astra.spoken` sources and `astra.spoken.played` texts. No literal and no `now` appears in any of them, nor in `turns.jsonl` or the memory episodes. The voiced `text` joined is `Press`, as at base. Drop rows: `Hi <pad>there. ` stores and voices `Hi there. ` / `Hi there.`; `Say <|"|>yes<|"|>. ` stores `Say yes. `.
2. **The `<eos>`-spliced thought.** Tools off, `answer("Sure, <|chan<eos>nel>thought SECRET-THOUGHT<channel|>ok. ")`: history, every played source, `astra.spoken`, `astra.spoken.played`, `turns.jsonl` and memory episodes hold neither `SECRET-THOUGHT` nor `thought SECRET`; `history[-1].assistant_spoken == "Sure, <|chan"` exactly (H-T124-3); the voiced text equals base's (`Sure, <|chan`); criterion 7's note is written with `chars == 35`.
3. **History equals what was played.** For every utterance played in criteria 1, 2, 4, 5, 6: `normalise_for_tts(source) == text`; no `source` contains any `SPECIAL_TOKENS` or `speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS` literal; `history[-1].assistant_spoken` equals the join of the played sources, in order. A barge-in row cutting mid-answer after a deleted drop literal (`Hi <pad>there, this answer is long enough to still be playing at the cut. `) stores the `played_text` the audio fake reports, with no literal.
4. **Split across commits** gives the whole commit's result: `Press <|tool` / `_call> now. `; `Hi <end_of` / `_turn> bye. ` (`Hi `); `Sure, <|chan` / `<eos>nel>thought SEC` / `RET-THOUGHT<channel|>ok. ` (criterion 2's result); `Press <|tool` / a `thought_open=True` commit `draft` / `_call> now. ` (I15: `Press `; the open commit reaches the machine as sent and does not flush or reset the hold). No `LlmCommit` the machine receives contains a literal or a prefix that later completes one.
5. **Stopped means stopped.** `First. <eos>Second. ` / `Third. ` stores `First. `, voices `First.`; nothing after the cut is submitted before or at `llm.done`.
6. **Thought first, then literals.** `Here <|channel>a<eos>b<channel|> ok. ` stores and voices `Here  ok. ` (T-0121 criterion 1); T-0121's note counts the block; no T-0124 note. A spliced literal is handled like one written in full: `Press <|tool<pad>_call> now. ` stores `Press `.
7. **The note.** One session-log `note` per stream where the literal stage dropped at least one character: `{"what": "special literals in an answer commit were not voiced or stored", "request_id": <machine id>, "step": 1, "chars": n, "cut": bool}`, after the held tail is submitted, after T-0121's note when both apply, before `llm.done`. `chars` = `len(T(raw)) - len(U(raw))` (never counts what T-0121 removed): `Hi <pad>there. ` gives `chars 5, cut false`; criterion 1 gives `len(f"{lit} now. ")`, `cut true`. No value holds stream text; exactly one note per stream; none when nothing was dropped; T-0121's note unchanged; `llm.commit` keeps the raw text.
8. **Streaming and chunk independence (I12, tools-off form).** For every split of raw into commits: the concatenation of every submitted text plus the flush equals U(raw); each cumulative release is a prefix of U(raw); no release holds a literal or a prefix that later completes one. The voice is unchanged: `normalise_for_tts(U(raw)) == normalise_for_tts(T(raw))`.
9. **Tools on: guard rows, unchanged behaviour.** With the default kit, loop off, criteria 1, 2 and 4's rows store no literal and no `SECRET` (`_AnswerCleaner`); e.g. `Press <|tool_call> now. ` still stores `Press  now. ` and voices `Press now.`. Green at base, no marker, byte-identical to `8b3f6d0`.

## Implementation notes

- The gate's conservation (A1) and the machine's join check are why the fix goes before `submit`, not at the gate. Do not change the gate, the machine, the client, the router or the audio module.
- L must be the gate's rule exactly; prefer reading `speech_gate._TRIM_TOKENS` and `_DROP_TOKENS` (or `_cut_and_drop`) over restating them.
- The second stage reads the concatenation of what `_ThoughtCleaner` released (and its flush); that input only grows, so L of it is prefix-stable under the hold.
- The hold has the `_AnswerCleaner` shape (I12): from the first `<` after the last character that is `>` or outside the alphabet of every literal L cuts or deletes. That alphabet is not `_LITERAL_CHARS`: `<end_of_turn>` and `<|endoftext|>` add `f` and `x`. After a cut nothing later can change the cleaned text, so a held tail may be released at once.
- T-0121's submission rules are unchanged: empty raw commits are submitted; non-empty commits that clean to nothing are not; `thought_open=True` commits bypass both stages and do not touch the hold.
- The note's keys follow T-0117's pattern, with `step` 1 and `cut` in place of `unclosed`.
- Mypy strict. No heredocs to the scratchpad. If a test looks wrong, tell the reviewer.
