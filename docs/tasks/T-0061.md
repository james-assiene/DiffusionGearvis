# T-0061 — the doctor's healthy-engine matcher is pinned to what the driver actually reports

**Status:** planned 2026-09-17 · **Blast radius:** LOW–MEDIUM (one reviewer) · **Groups:** one, under half a day · **Origin:** T-0058 rulings A12 (the healthy-engine arithmetic), A17/A23 (the matcher's provenance was never recorded) and **C1** (the human's `/launch` cold boot, 2026-09-17 16:42, commit bd8001b: the driver names the engine `VLLM::EngineCore`, so the row reported "not gathered" on a healthy box) · **Previous owner of the files:** T-0058 groups A/B (merged 3920d13; group C's harness for its criterion 9 is still owed and is **not** in scope here) · **No `/launch`:** the evidence is the committed fixture; see *Regime*.

## Goal

`astra doctor`'s `vram.engine_start` row answers "could the engine be started on this box?". On a box where the engine is *already running*, the honest arithmetic is free memory **plus what the engine's own process gives back when it stops** (T-0058 ruling A12), so the row has to recognise the engine among the processes holding the device. It recognises it by the basename of the first word of `process_name` being in `ENGINE_PROCESS_NAMES = ("python3", "python")` — a rule whose stated provenance (a 2026-09-14 compute-apps recording) A23 found **does not exist**, and which the first real reading contradicts: with the engine up the driver calls it `VLLM::EngineCore` (26 936 MiB), so on 2026-09-17 the row said *"not gathered"* on the one box the whole task was written for. This task replaces the guess with the recording: the matcher keys on the name the driver actually prints, the fixtures carry that name, the docstring cites the committed line and the run instead of a recording nobody took, and the two directions A23 measured as unpinned get rows — a stranger listed before the engine must not be counted as the engine, and a healthy engine nobody can attribute stays a WARN that now *names the largest unattributed holder* instead of leaving the next human to re-derive it from a `/launch`. After this, the row on the live box (engine up, recogniser resident) reads PASS with its arithmetic shown, which is what the human's paste should have said on 2026-09-17.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The matcher is the recorded name, matched case-sensitively as a substring of the whole `process_name`.** `ENGINE_PROCESS_NAMES = ("python3", "python")` is replaced by a marker tuple whose only member is the family of the one recorded name — `"VLLM::"`, of which `VLLM::EngineCore` is the recorded instance — and a holder is engine memory iff its `process_name` **contains** a marker. `python3` and `python` are **gone**: they were never recorded, and they are what produced A23's false PASS. *Checkable:* a unit row parses `tests/fixtures/box/nvidia_smi_compute_apps_engine_up.csv` with the module's own `parse_nvidia_smi_compute_apps`, feeds the parsed holders to the row, and asserts the engine's 26 936 MiB is the figure the arithmetic used. Satisfies T-0058 ruling C1 and closes A23's follow-up.
2. **Every matching holder counts, not the first and not the largest.** A restart releases *all* of the engine's processes before the new one takes its snapshot, so the helper sums the MiB of every holder that matches a marker. *Checkable:* a synthetic two-holder row (`VLLM::EngineCore` + a `VLLM::Worker_TP0`, labelled synthetic in the test — no such reading exists) where first-match, largest-match and sum give three different figures, and only the sum is accepted. Satisfies architecture §7 / T-0033's "the row is a function of the facts" and A12's arithmetic.
3. **A stranger listed before the engine is not the engine — A23's measured false PASS becomes a WARN.** With holders `python3 train.py` 24 000 MiB **listed first** and `VLLM::EngineCore` 500 MiB second, free 4 200, engine healthy, `--gpu-memory-utilization 0.86`: engine-held is **500**, available 4 700, predicted 4 188 at the check against 27 591 demanded, so the row is **WARN** ("running, could not be started again where it stands", 23 403 MiB short). The old matcher returned the stranger's 24 000 and called this box PASS with "28 200 MiB the restart can use", which is the silence A23 recorded. *Checkable:* verdict plus the absence of 28 200 / 24 000 from the arithmetic. Satisfies A23's "unpinned direction".
4. **A holder that merely mentions vLLM in lowercase is not the engine.** A `python3 /home/user/vllm-new-model-03-23-2026/benchmarks/benchmark_throughput.py` holder at 24 000 MiB, engine healthy and no `VLLM::` holder present, gives **WARN "not gathered"**, not a PASS. This box really does carry a vLLM source checkout at that path (T-0058 implementation notes), so a case-insensitive `"vllm"` substring — the obvious simplification of criterion 1 — is wrong here and only here. *Checkable:* verdict, plus that no figure derived from 24 000 appears in the row.
5. **An unattributable healthy engine still says "not gathered", and now says which holder to look at.** Verdict `WARN` and the phrase `not gathered` are unchanged (`_ungathered`), and the **fix** line additionally names the largest holder that is not the recogniser and not already matched, with its pid and MiB, and says to add its name to the marker constant if that is the engine. The hinted figure **never enters the arithmetic** — a largest-holder fallback in the arithmetic reproduces criterion 3's false PASS with a bigger stranger, which is why the heuristic lives in the advice. *Checkable:* on criterion 4's box the fix names the pid/MiB of the training script and the constant's new name, and the detail still carries no arithmetic derived from it.
6. **The docstring's provenance is the fixture and the run, not an invented recording.** The comment on the marker constant and `_engine_held_mib`'s docstring name `tests/fixtures/box/nvidia_smi_compute_apps_engine_up.csv`, the run that produced it (human `/launch` cold boot 2026-09-17 16:42, commit bd8001b, `logs/vllm-coldboot-bd8001b.log`, PROGRESS *Runs*), and state plainly which part is recorded (`VLLM::EngineCore`) and which part is **our generalisation** (the `VLLM::` family, vLLM's own process-title prefix — verified against the pinned container's source or labelled unverified). The inverted rationale A23 found ("`python3` is the narrower pattern") is gone. *Checkable:* a presence-grep for the fixture filename and the commit in `local_astra/preflight.py`'s source. (Presence only: `.claude/rules/tests.md`, 2026-09-17 — an absence-grep over source text matches prose and reddens for the wrong reason.)
7. **The row the box produced on 2026-09-17, both lines, hand-computed.** A rig box description built from the committed fixture's three holders plus the free reading of that moment — `memory.free` **1 448 MiB** at ready-idle, engine healthy, util 0.86 — yields:
   - `vram.engine_start` **PASS**: 1 448 + 26 936 = 28 384 available to a restart, − 512 = **27 872** at the engine's own check, against ceil(0.86 × 32 082) = **27 591** demanded ⇒ clears by **281 MiB**; the detail states 1 448, 26 936, ~27 872 and 27 591;
   - `vram.free` **FAIL**: 1 965 MiB free by the row's own total-minus-used reading, under the 3 072 floor — **expected, untouched, and not a regression** (T-0058 ruling A19).
   The two figures for "free" differ by ~520 MiB on purpose (ruling A5). *Checkable:* both verdicts and the four MiB figures, with the derivations written out in the test docstring.
8. **Negative — nothing else about the row moves.** `VRAM_FLOOR_MIB` stays `3 * 1024`; `VLLM_TOTAL_MIB` 32082, `ENGINE_INIT_OVERHEAD_MIB` 512, `VLLM_DEFAULT_UTILIZATION` 0.92 and its version string are byte-identical; the `vram.free` row's name, verdicts and wording are untouched; the six pre-existing `vram.engine_start` rows keep their verdicts (2026-09-16 coldboot **FAIL**, healthy at 0.92 **WARN**, healthy at 0.86 **PASS**, unattributed healthy **WARN**, and both criterion-8 adversarials of T-0058); `THIS_BOX_WARNS` / `THIS_BOX_PASSES` membership is unchanged; `services/vllm.sh` is not touched at all (its guard reads `memory.free` with no attribution and is out of scope). `astra doctor` still starts nothing, spends no GPU time, writes nothing and opens no non-loopback socket (`tests/unit/test_doctor_probes.py`, `test_supervisor_no_egress.py` green and unedited).

## Implementation notes

- The change is small but it is **not** a one-sentence diff: it changes a verdict on a real box in both directions (WARN → PASS on the live box, PASS → WARN on criterion 3's), so the protocol applies.
- `_engine_held_mib` currently does `Path(words[0]).name in ENGINE_PROCESS_NAMES`. The recorded name has no path and no spaces, so the basename/first-word machinery buys nothing and hides the substring semantics criterion 1 asks for; match against the whole `process_name`. Keep `None` meaning *not gathered* — never `0`, which is a claim about the box and the wrong one (A12, and the ungathered row catches `0`-for-`None`).
- Rename the constant (e.g. `ENGINE_PROCESS_MARKERS`): the semantics change from exact basename to substring, and a stale name is how the next reader repeats A23. The ungathered fix line interpolates the constant, so its text changes with it — no test pins the old wording.
- `evaluate` stays pure and the collectors stay read-only polls (T-0033 criterion 1, `.claude/rules/gpu-services.md`). No new fact, no new collector, no new `nvidia-smi` call: the holders are already gathered.
- **Ruling A4 still bites:** the literal `ASTRA_STT_CPU` is forbidden anywhere under `local_astra/` (T-0045 criterion 20). The new fix text must not acquire it while being edited.
- **A known ±2 MiB inconsistency in the sources, and which one wins.** T-0058 ruling C1 and `PROGRESS.md` say the engine held **26 934** MiB; the committed fixture line says **26 936**, and a read-only poll of the still-running engine on 2026-09-17 says 26 936 as well. The fixture is the verbatim evidence and the tests take their figure from it by parsing, not by transcription. Two MiB against a 281 MiB margin cannot move a verdict (the same tolerance posture as T-0058 ruling A3). *For the orchestrator, not owned here:* the prose figure in `PROGRESS.md` and ruling C1 is two MiB out.
- The two readings composed in criterion 7 were taken minutes apart (holders 16:45, free at ready-idle ~16:44) and the free column drifts by tens of MiB as Chrome breathes — a live poll while writing this task read 1 370 free. State that in the test docstring and keep the tolerances; do not chase the last MiB.
- Do not touch `services/vllm.sh`. Its pre-`docker run` guard reads `memory.free` with no attribution at all, which is why it refused correctly on a box the doctor waved through (A23's severity bound). Giving the launcher the same matcher is a separate question and not this task's.
- Never run `services/vllm.sh`, `astra up`, or any GPU command to find out what the driver prints: the fixture is the recording, and the engine currently running on the box is the human's.
