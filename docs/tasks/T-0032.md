## Goal

The session is deaf. `local_astra/conductor/session.py` never consumes `audio.stt_blocks()` and
never calls `stt.feed()`, so no audio ever reaches moshi-server; `MoshiSttClient` deliberately
never emits `SttEndOfTurnEvent` (the reflex lane decides end-of-turn) and nothing in
`local_astra/reflex/` is wired into the session, so `E.UserSpeechOnset` and `E.SttEndOfTurn` are
produced by nothing but test scripts and `scripts/demo/session_rung.py`'s typed path — on hardware
`IDLE → LISTENING → ENDPOINTING → THINKING` can never fire. This task closes the loop: a capture
pump that hands every `SttFrame` straight to the STT client untouched, a pure endpointer in
`reflex/turn_predictor.py` that turns the Kyutai pause heads (and a silence backstop) into onset
and end-of-turn decisions with *audio* timestamps, the `mark(t_eot_ms)` that makes `SttFlushed`
arrive so ENDPOINTING leaves on the settle tick and not on a timer, and the two `cli.py` defects
that would have made the wiring invisible (`hasattr(audio, "stt_frames")` tests a name that does
not exist, and `VllmClient()` ignores `services.vllm`). Group A proves, over the fakes alone, that
a *spoken* question reaches THINKING and SPEAKING without a test submitting a single `stt.*` event
— the test that would have caught the deafness. Group B answers one spoken question on the box.

## Acceptance criteria

### Group A — the loop over the fakes

1. **Every STT frame is fed, verbatim, in order.** A capture pump task, started by
   `Session.start()` and cancelled by `Session.aclose()`, iterates `stt_blocks()` and calls
   `await stt.feed(pcm, t_ms)` once per frame, where `pcm` is the frame's `samples` serialised as
   little-endian f32 bytes and `t_ms` is `int(frame.t_ms)` — the frame's own capture time.
   *Negative:* the bytes fed are byte-identical to the frame's samples and the times are
   non-decreasing and equal to the frames' — **no resampling, reframing, retiming, padding,
   gating or re-gating happens in `session.py`** (T-0021 seam; docs/02 §4 spec delta T-0021 (3)).
   The self-speech gate stays where it is: the session never touches `capture_frames()` in this
   task and never drops or zeroes a block.
2. **The capture pump cannot take the session down and cannot block the machine pump.** A
   `feed()` that raises is counted and the loop continues with the next frame; the pump task never
   calls `machine.step`, never submits an event per frame, and `aclose()` ends it (and
   `close_capture()`, when the audio object has it) within the existing settle budget. *Adversarial:*
   an audio object with no capture seam at all (the `AudioIO` protocol does not declare
   `stt_blocks`; T-0021 ruling A1 kept it on the concrete class) leaves the session fully
   functional and logs the absence exactly once — it must not raise at `start()`.
3. **The endpointer is pure and lives in `reflex/turn_predictor.py`.** It takes STT events with
   their audio times (`SttWordEvent`, `SttStepEvent`) and returns decisions; no `asyncio`, no
   `time`, no I/O, no logging side effects (`.claude/rules/conductor.md`). Its rules, each a named
   constant with the regime stated in its docstring:
   (a) **onset** — the first word (or first speech-bearing step) of a run yields
   `("onset", audio_ms)` at that word's `start_ms`;
   (b) **end of turn, primary** — an EMA of the pause head over consecutive steps crossing
   `EOT_P_THRESHOLD` (0.6, docs/02 §2.2's Unmute-inherited rule) yields
   `("end_of_turn", t_eot_ms, p_end)` with `t_eot_ms` = the last word's `end_ms`;
   (c) **end of turn, backstop** — `EOT_SILENCE_MS` of step-clock time after the last word's
   `end_ms` with no new word yields the same decision. The silence clock is the step stream's
   audio time (a `Step` arrives every 80 ms whether or not anyone speaks), **never** a wall clock;
   (d) at most one end-of-turn per run: after one fires, the next requires an intervening onset;
   (e) a run with **no words** never produces an end-of-turn, however long the silence.
4. **Onset and end-of-turn reach the machine with audio times.** The session submits
   `E.UserSpeechOnset(t_ms=<clock>, onset_ms=<audio ms>)` and
   `E.SttEndOfTurn(t_ms=<clock>, t_eot_ms=<audio ms>, p_end=…)` from the endpointer's decisions,
   taking the audio times from the STT stream's own fields (docs/02 §4: "transcript segments are
   attributed by reflex-onset *audio* timestamps, never by state-entry wall time"). *Negative:*
   `onset_ms`/`t_eot_ms` are never `clock.now_ms`. The delivery stamp `t_ms` stays the clock's, as
   `_pump_stt` already does for words.
5. **`mark()` is sent once per end-of-turn, off the pump, and no flush is ever synthesised.**
   After submitting `E.SttEndOfTurn(t_eot_ms=T)` the session awaits `stt.mark(T)` exactly once,
   from a producer task (never from `_handle`/the executor path, which would put an await for a
   service in the one pump). If the machine re-enters LISTENING and endpoints again, the new
   `t_eot` is marked again. *Negative criterion:* `session.py` contains no code path that submits
   `E.SttFlushed` from a timer, from silence, or from `close()` — the only `SttFlushed` is the one
   `_pump_stt` maps from the client's `SttFlushedEvent` (T-0007a criterion 4; docs/02 §4 row 6). A
   `SttFlushedEvent` that arrives while the machine is not ENDPOINTING changes nothing.
6. **Headline: a spoken question over the fakes reaches THINKING and SPEAKING.** With
   `FakeAudioIO` producing capture and a `FakeStt` that emits words/steps *in response to fed
   audio* (with its 500 ms lag) and echoes `mark()` as `SttFlushedEvent`, the session goes
   `IDLE → LISTENING → ENDPOINTING → THINKING → SPEAKING → IDLE` and writes exactly one
   `turns.jsonl` line whose `user_text` is the spoken sentence. **The test submits no
   `E.UserSpeechOnset`, `E.SttEndOfTurn`, `E.SttWord` or `E.SttFlushed` of its own** — only audio
   and virtual time. ENDPOINTING is left on a `Tick` `eot_settle_ms` after the flush (the existing
   row), not on any new mechanism. *This is the test whose absence hid the deafness.*
7. **`SttClient` declares `mark`.** `local_astra/contracts/protocols.py` gains
   `async def mark(self, t_eot_ms: int) -> int: ...` to `SttClient` (the one-line follow-on
   T-0007a ruling A3 assigns here), `MoshiSttClient` and `tests/fakes/stt.py` both satisfy it, and
   a static pin proves the real class is an `SttClient` **under mypy**, not merely at runtime —
   the T-0007 ruling A13 landmine (`played_ms -> int` vs `-> float`), reused.
8. **The counters exist and are in the turn line.** The session counts frames fed and feed
   failures for the life of the session and writes, per turn, `extras` keys
   `stt_frames_fed`, `stt_feed_errors`, plus `capture_xruns`/`capture_gated_blocks` read from
   `capture_stats()` when the audio object exposes it, and the span `reflex.onset` (both existing
   names in `telemetry/spans.py`; no new span name is added). *Negative:* absent a capture seam
   the keys are absent or zero — never invented.
9. **`cli.py`'s two defects are fixed and tested.** (a) The "microphone path is not wired yet"
   line tests the seam that actually exists (`stt_blocks`), so on a real `PipeWireAudioIO` it does

   already read theirs off `Services` (T-0007a ruling A5). *Negative:* no non-loopback endpoint is
   constructible through this path (the `Services`/`VllmEndpoint` validators stay the gate).
10. **Nothing else changes.** *Negative criteria, all checkable:* `conductor/turn_machine.py` is

## Implementation notes

Constraints and landmines; the design is the implementer's.

- **The one pump, the one clock.** The capture pump and the STT producer are *producer tasks*, not
  the pump: they may `await`, they must never call `machine.step`, and time still reaches the
  machine only as `Tick` (`session.py` module docstring; docs/02 §4). `mark()` is awaited in a
  producer task, never inside `_handle` or a `CommandExecutor` handler — the executor is T-0028's
  file and is not owned here, which also settles where the call cannot go.
- **Why the pause heads and not Silero (the design ruling this task makes).** `reflex/vad.py` and
  the current `reflex/turn_predictor.py` are both `raise NotImplementedError("T-0007")` stubs;
  Silero v6 would need an ONNX model, a CPU thread, and thresholds nobody has measured, and it
  reads the **raw** capture stream — which, on speakers with no AEC, contains our own voice. The
  Kyutai pause heads are already on the wire (`SttStepEvent.pause_heads`, one `Step` every 80 ms),
  already parsed by `MoshiSttClient`, cost no extra model, and docs/02 §2.2 already names the rule
  (EMA of the pause head > 0.6, inherited from Unmute). That is what works tonight with what is
  merged. Consequences to be stated, not hidden: (i) onset is derived from the STT stream, which
  lags audio by ≈ 0.5 s, so `snapshot_frame`/`prefill_open` happen ≈ 0.5 s after the user starts
  speaking; (ii) the self-speech gate silences the **STT tap** while we speak (docs/02 §4 spec
  delta T-0021 (1)), so this path produces **no barge-in at all** — `SPEAKING → PAUSED` still
  cannot fire on hardware. **Do not paper over that**: barge-in needs a VAD on `capture_frames()`
  and an echo-path residual number from T-0023, and it is a follow-up task (**T-0034**), not a
  side effect of this one. No criterion here claims barge-in works, and the report must say it
  does not.
- **Constants, with their provenance.** `EOT_P_THRESHOLD = 0.6` (docs/02 §2.2, inherited, not
  measured here), the EMA α stated as a half-life in steps (≈ 3 steps / 240 ms) rather than a bare
  number, `EOT_SILENCE_MS` (a chosen backstop, ≈ 800 ms, in the same band as the B3 budget's
  end-of-turn column), `MIN_SPEECH_MS` before an end-of-turn may fire, and `PAUSE_HEAD_INDEX`
  **documented as unverified** until criterion 12 reads the real `prs` vector. X2/Q5 (docs/03,
  docs/02 §10) is the experiment that replaces these with measurements; each docstring says which
  number is chosen and which is measured. A number without its regime is not a number
  (CLAUDE.md).
- **Audio time vs delivery time.** `E.SttStep` carries only `pause_heads` and the delivery `t_ms`;
  the *audio* time lives on `speech.SttStepEvent.t_ms` and is dropped by `_pump_stt` today. The
  endpointer must therefore be consulted where the raw `SttEvent` is still in hand (inside
  `_pump_stt`), not from the machine's event stream. Do **not** widen `contracts/events.py` — it
  is not owned here, and the decision does not need to travel through the machine.
- **`feed()` is already non-blocking and already counts.** `MoshiSttClient._enqueue` drops and
  counts on a full bounded queue and emits one error event; the session's counters are its own
  (frames handed over, failures), not a re-derivation of the client's. T-0007a ruling A9: a
  dropped inbound event would stall ENDPOINTING silently — if `stats()` is available, surface a
  rising `dropped_events` once through the existing error path rather than inventing a recovery.
- **Bytes, not arrays.** `SttFrame.samples` is a `numpy` f32 array; `feed()` takes bytes. Ensure
  C-contiguity and little-endian f32 and convert — nothing else. `session.py` importing `numpy`
  for one `tobytes()` is acceptable; a dtype change, a copy through Python floats, or a `struct`
  loop in the pump is not.
- **`mypy --strict`** on `local_astra/`: the capture seam is not on `AudioIO`, so declare a narrow
  `runtime_checkable` Protocol in `session.py` for what the pump needs (`stt_blocks`, optionally
  `capture_stats`/`close_capture`) and check it structurally. Do not `cast` the audio object to
  something it is not, and do not widen `AudioIO` (T-0021 A1).
- **Fakes move with the interface** (CLAUDE.md): the `SttClient.mark` widening changes
  `tests/fakes/stt.py` in the same PR — the test-author's edit, not the implementer's; the
  implementer never edits `tests/` (hook-enforced). If a test looks wrong, say so to the reviewer.
- **docs/02 §4 wants a spec line, not a silent change.** If the implementation needs a rule the
  architecture does not state (e.g. that the session marks at the `freeze_after` row), it goes in
  as a spec delta paragraph in the reviewer's ruling and into `docs/02-architecture.md` §4 by the
  orchestrator — not as an undocumented behaviour.

- **Census evidence (2026-09-14, T-0007a A12–A14) the implementer builds on.** The live server sends `Ready`, `Step` (`prs` is a **4-vector**; the example at speech was `[1.3e-05, 1.4e-04, 2.8e-04, 5.8e-04]`), `Word` (`start_time` seconds), `EndWord` (`stop_time` seconds — T-0038 maps it; until T-0038 merges every `SttWordEvent` has `end_ms == start_ms`, so `t_eot_ms` from the last word's `end_ms` is its start), and echoes `Marker` **only while audio keeps arriving**: on CPU STT the echo landed 3.0 s after mark() with silence fed continuously, and never landed when feeding stopped. Consequences: (i) the capture pump feeds through ENDPOINTING and SPEAKING without pause — criterion 1's "no gating in session.py" already forbids stopping it, and this is why; (ii) the ENDPOINTING wait for `SttFlushed` on CPU STT is seconds, so the existing ENDPOINTING timeout row must be longer than the backlog or the turn falls into the error path — the implementer reports the value it relies on, and group B measures mark→flush per utterance as its own column (criterion 13); (iii) `PAUSE_HEAD_INDEX` picks one of four heads; criterion 12 reads the vector at silence to choose.

## Regime

State this beside every number reported.

**Group A (fast suite):** fakes only (`FakeAudioIO`, `FakeStt`, `FakeLlm`, `FakeTts`,
`FakePerception`, `FakeMemory`), `ScriptedClock`/`VirtualClock` (no wall time),
`TurnPolicy(thinking=True)`, `eot_settle_ms=120`, STT word lag 500 ms, step cadence 80 ms, capture
48 kHz / 10 ms blocks → 24 kHz / 1920-sample STT frames, n=1 deterministic per test (no sampling, no
seed), CPU only. No latency number from group A is quotable.

**Group B (the box, `/launch`, clean tree):** profile `lean`; vLLM `v0.29.0` container with
`nvidia/diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it-NVFP4` rev per `services/versions.lock`, canvas 256, steps 48,
`max_num_seqs 3`, KV 4 GiB bf16, thinking on; STT `kyutai/stt-1b-en_fr-candle @095e38f6` in
`astra-moshi:0.6.4`, `batch_size 2`, `asr_delay_in_tokens 6`, **state GPU or `ASTRA_STT_CPU=1`**
(on CPU the measured step time is 100–108 ms per 80 ms of audio — RTF ≈ 1.3 — so end-of-turn
delay grows with utterance length; state it); Pocket TTS on CPU; PipeWire, **headset or speakers
stated explicitly** (it changes the gate and the echo path), the sink and source node names,
`libportaudio2` present; driver 595.84; n = 3 utterances, one speaker, the sentence text quoted;
git commit of the tree. Report per utterance: transcript vs spoken text, last-word audio time →
`stt.end_of_turn`, last word → first audio, and the `prs` vector evidence for criterion 12. n=3 is
not B3 (50 turns, docs/03 §7) and must not be compared to it.

## Blast radius

**High** — the session pump, the endpointing decision and the audio capture path; a defect here is
a session that mishears, cuts the user off, or hangs in ENDPOINTING. **Two reviewers** (CLAUDE.md),
at least one of whom reads `turn_predictor.py` against docs/02 §4's ENDPOINTING rows and one of
whom mutation-tests criterion 5's negative (delete the `mark()` call; reinstate a drain timer —
both must turn the suite red). Group B is a GPU + audio-device run: `/launch` first, clean tree,
one `PROGRESS.md` Runs line with the whole regime.

## Rulings

- **A1 (criterion 6, `user_text`).** `TurnRecord` has no `user_text`; the criterion means the turn's history entry. The test asserts one
  `turns.jsonl` line plus `history[0].user_text == <spoken sentence>`. `turns_log.py` is not touched.
- **A2 (criterion 5, "existing timeout row").** There is no ENDPOINTING timeout row in the machine (`_on_tick` in ENDPOINTING is a no-op;
  `llm_timeout` arms only in THINKING). T-0032 adds none — `turn_machine.py` stays sha-pinned. A session whose mark is never echoed stays in
  ENDPOINTING; the test asserts exactly that. Whether ENDPOINTING needs a timeout row is a spec question for a follow-up (note the CPU
  backlog: the flush lands ≈ 3 s after mark when audio keeps flowing, census run 3), not this task.
- **A3 (criterion 2, "logs the absence once").** One `logging.getLogger("local_astra.conductor.session").warning(...)` naming `stt_blocks`,
  emitted at `start()`, once. No new message type.
- **A4 (`PAUSE_HEAD_INDEX`).** The real `prs` vector has **4** entries (census A12); the fake sends 8. The constant must be `< 4`, documented
  as unverified until group B reads the vector at silence. `EOT_SILENCE_MS >= 400` (the EMA-vs-spike row needs four steps of slack); ≈ 800 is
  the chosen default.
- **A5 (activation).** The test-author hit its turn limit after committing (0424f84); the orchestrator removed the group-A `pending` markers
  mechanically on the branch. The group-B integration module keeps its marker until group B runs.
- **A6 (fast-suite cost).** The new modules add ≈ 5–8 s serial to a suite already over the 120 s ceiling (T-0031 A3); parallel stays ≈ 28 s.
  Recorded, not fixed here.
- **A7 (review A, F1 — the backstop is inert at the seam).** The machine drops `SttEndOfTurn` with `p_end < policy.eot_p_threshold`
  (0.6). On the silence backstop the EMA is not high by construction (empty/short `prs` ⇒ EMA never moves ⇒ `p_end == 0.0`), so no
  `FreezeAfter`, no `mark()`, LISTENING for ever — group B's most likely failure. Ruling: a backstop decision carries `p_end = 1.0` (the
  `SttEndOfTurnEvent` default exists for this), a head decision carries the EMA; the endpointer's decision is authoritative and the machine's
  threshold is a second gate only on the head path. Spec delta recorded in docs/02 §4 by the orchestrator. A crossing test
  (endpointer → machine, no session) is owed by the test-author: backstop with `prs=[]` must reach ENDPOINTING.
- **A8 (review A, F2 — `MIN_SPEECH_MS` hangs short utterances).** `MIN_SPEECH_MS` applies to the **head** path only; the silence backstop
  is unconditional (criterion 3(c) as written). Until T-0038's end times flow, run length is between word starts; the docstring says so.
- **A9 (review A, F3–F5 — unpinned constants and an equivalent-looking mutant).** Test-author adds: a two-run row where the second run's
  early step carries a moderate head (≈ 0.55) — the EMA reset at onset must make it survive (removing the reset must cut it); band
  assertions `400 <= EOT_SILENCE_MS <= 1200`, `0 <= PAUSE_HEAD_INDEX < 4`, `0 < MIN_SPEECH_MS <= 500`. The class docstring's stated reason for
  the reset (pre-speech silence heads) is wrong — steps are ignored while no run is open; the real reason is discarding the previous run's
  crossing EMA. Implementer fixes the docstring.
- **A10 (review A, minor).** `session.py` reads `stt.stats()` when available and surfaces a rising `dropped_events` once through the
  existing error path (Implementation note / T-0007a A9); `scripts/demo/session_rung.py` constructs `VllmClient(VllmEndpoint(base_url=
  profile.services.vllm))` (owned file; same defect as criterion 9(b)); drop the unread `Session._capture` attribute or read it.
- **A11 (review B, finding 1 — the pump-never-stops rule is untestable with the fake as written).** `tests/fakes/stt.py` releases a
  pending flush regardless of whether audio keeps arriving, so a pump that skips frames during ENDPOINTING/SPEAKING passes the whole suite
  and hangs on hardware (census A14). Ruling: in reactive mode the fake releases a pending flush only once `fed_ms` has advanced past
  `t_eot_ms + FLUSH_BACKLOG_MS` (choose ≈ 500 ms; the real backlog is seconds on CPU) — a session that stops feeding never leaves
  ENDPOINTING. Test-author edit; the mutation "pump `continue`s while ENDPOINTING/SPEAKING" must go red.
- **A12 (review B, finding 2 — `close_capture()` is dead to the suite).** No fake defines `close_capture`, so `if False` around the call
  passes. Test-author adds a capture-closing audio double to the listening module: records the call and asserts it happens after the
  pump task is cancelled and exactly once; the DeafAudio row stays.
- **A13 (review B, finding 4 — two clock origins meet in the machine).** On hardware `onset_ms`/`t_eot_ms` are ADC positions zero-based
  at the PortAudio stream's first callback, while `SessionClock` is zero-based at `Session()` construction; `SnapshotFrame(at_ms=onset_ms)`
  and the takeover arm compare them. Group A cannot see it (one virtual clock). Not an edit here: docs/02 §4 gets a spec line (orchestrator)
  and group B measures the construction→first-callback offset per run; if it exceeds one perception frame period the follow-up is a
  session-clock rebase at the first capture frame (T-0039 candidate).
- **A14 (review B, minor).** `stt_frames_fed` counts handed-over frames including ones whose `feed()` raised (the errors counter is the
  complement); the comment at the `close_capture()` call about "padding the tail once" is wrong (the pump is already cancelled) — implementer
