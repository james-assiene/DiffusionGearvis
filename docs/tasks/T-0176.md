## Goal

In James's desk session (t7) three searches ran (duckduckgo, then brave_api twice). The model then made four more `google_search` calls, each refused with `search failed: at most 3 searches per request; answer with what you have`. The Tools pane said "search limit reached", which James read as a provider quota. The turn ended voicing only "I will search for the latest volume of the Vinland Saga Deluxe edition available on Amazon.", the announcement of a call that never ran.

After this task:
- the cap is 5 searches per question, counting only searches that returned results (zero results included);
- failed searches count against a separate bound of 3 failures;
- the search response that reaches the cap tells the model that no searches are left;
- at most one further search reaches the model, as a refusal, and the step after it is a forced answer step: a search drafted there or later is refused before it runs and ends the turn, while other tools still work;
- the tool response, the Tools pane ("Gearvis limit: 5 searches per question") and the docs say plainly that this is Gearvis's own limit, never "search failed";
- each `web search` session-log note lists which engine refused and with what kind of failure (T-0163b), never the error text.

The `tools` request field and the system prompt stay byte-identical across steps. Removing the declaration would move the system turn and miss the whole prefix cache (implementation note 1).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Counting (T-0163b; replaces T-0125 criterion 5).** `MAX_SEARCHES_PER_TURN = 5`. A search counts only when it reached the backend and returned `SearchResults` (zero rows included).
   - A search that raised (`SearchFailed` of any kind, `TimeoutError` or the session deadline, any other exception) does not count toward the 5. It counts toward `MAX_FAILED_SEARCHES_PER_TURN = 3` (H-T176-2).
   - Calls the executor refused count toward neither, as at T-0125.
   - A search cancelled by barge-in, takeover or reset keeps T-0125 amendment 3's handling.
   - Both counts reset per turn and on session reset (§4 reset: `RESET_CLEARS`).
2. **Withdrawal point.** Search is *withdrawn* for the rest of the turn from the moment either count reaches its bound. Before that, behaviour is T-0125's, except for the numbers and wording in criteria 1, 5 and 7.
3. **Notice at the cap.**
   - The executed response of the search that brings the counted total to 5 gains one key: `limit` = `SEARCH_LIMIT_NOTE`, a module constant of at most 40 characters (e.g. `that was search 5 of 5; no searches left`).
   - The failure response that brings failures to 3 gains `limit` = `SEARCH_FAILED_LIMIT_NOTE` (e.g. `that was failed search 3 of 3; no searches left`).
   - No other response gains the key.
   - At the worst-case raw bounds (T-0125's `test_untrusted_note_present_and_uncut_at_worst_case_bounds` rig), both `SEARCH_UNTRUSTED_NOTE` and `SEARCH_LIMIT_NOTE` render uncut. If they cannot, shorten the limit note; never the untrusted note or `MAX_RESPONSE`.
4. **The first search after withdrawal.** The first `google_search` call drafted after withdrawal never reaches the backend. It settles `refused`, its step text stays withheld (R15, unchanged) and the loop continues to the next step, as today. Its response is `status_payload("refused", SEARCH_CAP_REASON)`, or `SEARCH_FAILED_CAP_REASON` when the failure bound withdrew search.
5. **Wording for the model** (exact strings: H-T176-1).
   - `SEARCH_CAP_REASON = "Gearvis's own limit is 5 searches per question and all 5 are used; no more searches will run for this question, so answer now with what you found"`.
   - `SEARCH_FAILED_CAP_REASON = "search is not working right now: 3 searches failed for this question; no more searches will run, so answer without searching"`.
   - Neither starts with `search failed`. Both are built from the two constants, so changing a number changes the text.
6. **Forced answer step.** The step right after the criterion-4 refusal is the forced answer step.
   - From there to the end of the turn, every `google_search` draft is refused by the session before execution. This covers drafts from the step itself, from the T-0172 re-ask, and from thought-call recovery.
   - The machine sees a refused draft (R15: `tool.cancel(refused)`). No `tool.execute`, no backend call and no further `llm.continue` follow for it, and the turn ends at that step.
   - A non-search call at the forced step or later (live: `click_at` and the rest) runs exactly as today.
   - The forced step's request body is byte-identical to today's step request (`loop.step_prompt(base, steps)`): no new prompt text, and `tools` unchanged (H-T176-3).
7. **Wording in the Tools pane and HUD.**
   - The card of every refused-at-cap search (criteria 4 and 6) has the reason `Gearvis limit: 5 searches per question`, or `Gearvis limit: 3 failed searches per question` under the failure bound.
   - The HUD shows the reason verbatim (`hud/src/tools/index.js:102`, `panels/tool_call.js:135`), so no HUD file changes.
   - `search limit reached` no longer appears in `local_astra/`.
8. **Per-engine failures (T-0163b).**
   - `DdgsSearch`, `BraveApiSearch` and `FallbackSearch` expose `last_attempts: tuple[tuple[str, str], ...]`: each engine tried during the most recent search, in order, with the kind of failure it raised. The kind is one of `error`, `rate_limited`, `network`, `timeout` or `closed`. The engine that answered is omitted. The value is `()` before any search and while one is running.
   - `FallbackSearch` concatenates the primary's attempts with the fallback's.
   - The session copies it into every `web search` note as `failures: [{engine, kind}, …]`, or an empty list when the backend has no `last_attempts` (FakeSearch). Values are filtered by `_ENGINE_LABEL` and the kind set, so no message text, query or URL can enter.
   - Unchanged: the note's `status` values, the `tool.search` error record (`repr(exc)`), and the exception `test_ddgs_backend.py::test_every_engine_refusing_raises_the_last_refusal` pins.
9. **Accounting kept.** Every refused-at-cap search still gets its one `web search` note (status `refused`) and counts in the turn line's `searches` key, as at T-0125.
10. **Negative: no change outside search.**
    - The turn machine (`turn_machine.py`), its rows and its invariants are untouched.
    - With search off, or with a FakeSearch kit without `web_search`, every request, record and card is byte-identical to BASE.
    - The `tools` field and system prompt of every step of a turn are byte-identical to BASE.
    - T-0172's re-ask rules are unchanged.
    - What R15 voices at a refused draft is unchanged: the forced step's own text may still be voiced, and T-0178 owns that rule.
11. **Negative: privacy.** `turns.jsonl` gains no key; `failures` appears only in the session-log note. No new egress path. `tests/unit/test_search_egress_scope.py` stays green unedited.

## Implementation notes

1. **Why the tool is not removed from the request.** The Gemma 4 template renders the `tools` declarations inside the system turn (`eval/cd/twopass.py:177`).
   - Removing `google_search` from one step's `tools` changes the first message, so every remaining step would re-prefill the whole prompt: system, history and up to `Budget.max_images` = 4 images of about 1,092 tokens at 1120.
   - It would also not stop calls: `native._v1_names()` accepts any name from the static `tools_v1.json`, and the earlier calls sit in the context.
   - So `tools`, `PromptBuilder.tools` and the system prompt stay fixed, and withdrawal is host-side only.
2. **Where to refuse.**
   - Criterion 4 reuses today's execution-time branch (`session.py` ~5334).
   - Criterion 6 refuses at judging time, on the path T-0102's retracted calls use, so the machine gets a refused draft and R15 is unchanged. No machine event or field is added.
   - Every place a draft is judged (the step, the re-ask with `after_reask=True`, thought-call recovery) goes through the one check.
3. **Counts.** Prefer deriving both counts and the withdrawal state from `_search_facts` statuses plus one per-turn marker, so T-0125's "search attributes" pin and `RESET_CLEARS` need no new entries. If an attribute must be added, classify it in `RESET_CLEARS` and tell the test-author.
4. **The `limit` key.** Add it after `note` in the payload; the renderer sorts keys. Check the binary-search cap in `loop.render_response` at the worst-case rig before choosing the final string.
5. **`last_attempts`.**
   - Set it only from failure kinds already computed by `_failure`/`SearchFailed.kind`, and never store `str(exc)`.
   - Read it in `_search_outcome`/`_decide_search` right after the search settles. The loop is serial, as `last_engine` is today.
   - Leave `brave_api.py` key handling untouched: the key never enters any attribute or log.
6. **Rules.** Read the conductor rules in `.claude/rules/` first: never voice uncommitted text, never voice withheld step text, no thinking in history.
7. **The residual case is intended.** At the forced step, an announcement followed by a search is voiced by R15. T-0178 owns that rule; do not special-case it here.

## Open rulings (planned at the recommendation)

- **H-T176-1, exact wording:** as in criteria 5 and 7. The alternative is shorter: "Gearvis's limit of 5 searches per question is used up; answer now with what you found".
- **H-T176-2, what counts as failed:** every failure kind (all engines refused, network, timeout, other) is outside the 5 and inside a failure bound of 3, because a timeout costs about 10 s and gives the model nothing. The alternative: only `error`/`rate_limited` are outside the cap.
- **H-T176-3, forced-step prompt:** no new prompt text. The refusal is the instruction, termination is deterministic, and the cache cost is zero. The alternative is an append-only nudge, but its effect is unmeasured, and in t7 T-0172's nudge led to another search.
- **H-T176-4, the silent end:** a forced step that ends on a refused search with no answer text voices nothing more until T-0178's honest line covers it; the Tools pane shows the Gearvis limit.
- **H-T176-5, per question means per turn:** one user turn. A follow-up question in a new turn gets a fresh 5.
