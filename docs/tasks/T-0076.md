## Human decisions (2026-10-03)
- **Q1 — commit rule:** the new arm commits the **joint constrained MAP over the model's distribution p**
  (argmax Σ log p_i over strings in the language), as D-H4 says and as `perstep_mar` does — not Mosaic's commit-time
  proposal over q. Only the settling rule differs from `perstep_mar`.
- **Q2 — headline statistics:** pre-register one new pair, `("perstep_mar_qargmax", "perstep_mar")`, in
  `stats.PREREGISTERED_PAIRS` (N_COMPARISONS 10 → 12, Bonferroni α follows), fixed before T-0071c touches a test cut.
  `perstep_mar_qargmax_sc` is reported with its Wilson CI but not significance-tested (like `perstep_mar_sc`).
- **Q3 — SC twin calibration:** `perstep_mar_qargmax_sc` reuses `perstep_mar_qargmax`'s frozen knobs (as `perstep_mar_sc`
  reuses Mar's); calibration stays ≈ 2× the original GPU time.
- **Q4 — engine contact:** no separate engine smoke; T-0071b b1's first `perstep_mar_qargmax` cell is the first engine
  contact, under its C2 abort-on-internal rule.

## Goal
Add `perstep_mar_qargmax`: identical to `perstep_mar` (T-0068 criterion 1) in the joint draw fed back where accepted,
uniform renoise elsewhere, acceptance and `confident` on H(q), and `sc_embeds`; its convergence **history** holds the
per-position argmax of the constrained marginals q (as Mosaic's vLLM integration does, `mosaic_vllm.py:632-668`), and the
joint constrained MAP (always in the language) is written only at the converging step and committed. Motivation
(`logs/t0071a-b13-e82ab84/`): Sudoku `perstep_mar` averaged 9.5 steps per canvas (tail 14–21) because one MAP position
keeps flipping in a history that `cd/steps.py:267` fills with `pr.map_row`; `perstep_mar_sc` averaged 3.8. Its SC twin
`perstep_mar_qargmax_sc` (constrained SC, as Mosaic's `:687`). Both are wired through the eval harness.

## Acceptance criteria
**Tier 1 — CPU (`make cd-test`, fast suite)**
1. **Wire (T-0064).** `astra_cd_mode="perstep_mar_qargmax"` is accepted by `xargs.MODES`, `requests.SERVED_MODES`,
   `steps.PERSTEP_MODES` and `contracts.cd.CdMode`, with `self_conditioning="constrained"` and the
   `entropy_bound`/`confidence_threshold` overrides like `perstep_mar` (P13); pydantic request and plugin parser round-trip
   it; the fake server (`tests/cd/fakes/vllm_server.py`) records it. Every pre-existing wire case decodes to the same `CdSpec`.
2. **Arm semantics (extends T-0068 c1).** Every denoise step of a `perstep_mar_qargmax` slot: the history row and
   `argmax_canvas` are `where(i < L_eff, argmax_v q_i(v), map_row)` (first index on ties; beyond L_eff exactly
   `perstep_mar`'s rows); accept mask, `confident`, fed-back canvas, `sc_embeds`, `n_acc` and CD-generator consumption are
   bit-identical to a `perstep_mar` slot given the same logits and generator state; convergence is the stock rule on this
   history; at convergence `argmax_canvas = canvas = MAP` and δ\* is taken of the MAP. Hand-built expectations, q from the
   fp64 oracle.
3. **History is argmax-of-q, not the MAP.** Language {ad, ae, bc}; p₁ = (a .5, b .5); p₂ = (c .4, d .3, e .3): the new arm's
   history on [0, 2) is `ac`, `perstep_mar`'s is `bc`. Mutants writing the MAP, the draw or `fed` fail.
4. **Commit stays in the language when argmax-of-q is not (adversarial).** Same instance, logits fixed, knobs making it
   `confident`: converges at step ST, commits the MAP `bc` (host replay accepts), never `ac`.
5. **Multi-canvas guarantee (T-0068 c3, SPEC §3.1b).** The 2 000-run matrix includes both new arms with the same budgets,
   automata and adversarial logits: viable prefix after every commit; CS = 1; zero loud errors on feasible instances;
   termination by `max_tokens` at the latest. Routine count under `ASTRA_CD_GUARANTEE_RUNS`; full count once by the verifier.
6. **Convergence speed (CPU, vendored stock world).** (a) Gated: language {ac, bc, bd}; p₂ = (c .51, d .49) fixed; p₁(a)
   alternates .55/.45 between steps — `perstep_mar` reaches the step cap, `perstep_mar_qargmax` converges in ≤ ST + 1 steps
   and commits an accepted string. (b) Reported: T-0063's Sudoku automaton with seeded noisy logits (n = 200,
   pre-registered seeds), mean/median/p90 steps per canvas of both arms with their regime.
7. **Records and tap.** Records name `arm = "perstep_mar_qargmax"` (the SC twin's too; the harness joins on `record_tag`);
   `mean_accepted_per_step` and `steps` filled; `end_projection` fields null; schema unchanged. Tap `denoise` frames keep
   the R10 shape; their `argmax` row is the argmax-of-q row and `stable` is the history's.

## Implementation notes
- The only new device work is `marg.q.argmax(-1)` per L_eff candidate, inside the candidate loop, selected with
  `torch.where(hit, …)` like `tokens`; never keep q alive across candidates (T-0073 D6 item 2).
- Branch on a host-known flag in `PerStep` so the existing arms' op sequence is literally unchanged; `marginal=` at
  `new_perstep` true for the new mode; grep every `== "perstep_mar"` / `startswith` check.
- The fed-back canvas stays `where(accept, x̂, uniform)` (Mosaic re-noises from the joint draw too, `:649`).
- The MAP is still computed every call (the host cannot know the phase without a sync); this arm saves steps, not per-step
  cost. A converging-call-only MAP is out of scope.
- No host syncs; criterion 11 is the proof. Keep the history in `accepted_canvas_history` so T-0011's `stable` mask reports
  what convergence uses. The wire change and its fake land here.
- **For T-0071b (dependency, not a ruling here):** its driver and tests pin `arm == "perstep_mar"`; after T-0076 merges
  they gain the arm and per-arm frozen names (`calibration-<task>-<arm>.json`) through T-0071b's own test-author.
