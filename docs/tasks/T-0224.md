## Goal

1. **Repeat stop.** In P4 r1 the model kept generating after its answer and card: it wrote self-talk in the answer channel ("(Wait, I have an extra closing brace in the JSON. Let me fix that.)", "Let me be careful.", a component listing) and then re-emitted its whole diagnosis. The T-0052 voice guard refused the JSON sentences (`speech.refused` ×2), but the self-talk and the repeated diagnosis were voiced (`tts.utterance` u4, u6, u7, u9–u11). The degeneracy guard did not trip. After this task, once the answer channel re-emits a sentence already voiced in the same turn, that sentence and every later answer sentence of the turn are refused (not voiced, not in history).
2. **Maths markup.** Several spoken sentences carried LaTeX/markdown: `$x squared$`, `$2x \cos(x squared)$`, `$2\times2$`, `$mg \cos \theta$`, `$20 = 5t + 4.9t^2$`. Nothing strips it (`normalise_for_tts`, `speech_gate.py:312`; `tts_client.speak` posts text verbatim). After this task the text sent to TTS has no `$` delimiters or inline backticks, and common maths commands are spoken as words.

## Acceptance criteria

**A. Repeat stop (`local_astra/conductor/speech_gate.py`).**
1. Within one turn, an answer sentence whose normalised form (casefold, whitespace collapsed, trailing punctuation dropped) equals that of a sentence already voiced in the turn, and is at least 20 characters long, is refused with reason `"repeat"`, and so is every later answer sentence of that turn, in the same way the voice guard refuses interface data (`text = ""`; a `speech.refused` record with the reason; nothing enters history). A short repeated sentence ("Yes." "OK.") does not trigger it.
2. Replaying P4 r1's committed canvases (from its session log) through the gate voices the first diagnosis and none of u9–u11's repeat; the self-talk sentences that come *before* the first repeat are still voiced (accepted limit: they are not repeats).
3. Negative: a turn with no repeated sentence voices exactly what it voices on main (every existing speech-gate test and the conservation property, adapted only by the documented refusal rule, stay green). The turn machine is untouched.

**B. Maths markup (`normalise_for_tts` or one new pure helper next to it).**
4. Before TTS: `$…$` and `$$…$$` delimiters removed; inline single-backtick spans unwrapped (their content kept, unless the existing voice guard refuses it); LaTeX commands spoken as words: `\cos` `\sin` `\tan` `\log` `\ln` → the name; Greek letters (`\theta`, `\alpha`, `\beta`, `\lambda`, `\mu`, `\pi`, `\omega`, `\Omega`, …) → their names; `\times` → "times"; `\cdot` → "times"; `\frac{a}{b}` → "a over b"; `\sqrt{x}` → "square root of x"; `^2` → " squared", `^3` → " cubed", `^{n}`/`^n` → " to the power n"; `_{1}`/`_1` → " 1"; remaining `\name` → name; stray `{` `}` removed. Unicode maths already in plain text (θ, ², √) is left as it is today.
5. Applied to every spoken sentence and nowhere else: cards (A2UI), the session log's committed text and history keep the model's text; history keeps what was voiced (the cleaned text), consistent with "store only what was spoken".
6. The T-0223 examples become: "x squared", "2x cos(x squared)", "2 times 2", "mg cos theta", "20 = 5t + 4.9t squared".
